A complete 23-part roadmap for me and anyone preparing for entry-level software engineering, backend development and applied AI. Learn the shared foundation first, then choose an FDE or ML engineering specialization.

Readiness checklist

+

Begin serious primary-role applications when you can demonstrate these skills. Add applied AI roles when you can evaluate model behavior and handle failures. Add FDE roles when you can demonstrate customer delivery. Add ML engineering roles when you can demonstrate reproducible training and model operations. Mark topics Not started, Learning, Practiced or Demonstrated. Support demonstrated skills with code, a project or an explanation.
