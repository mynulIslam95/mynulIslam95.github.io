Lot Sizes: Standard, Mini, Micro and Nano

The core principle

Lot size tells you how many units of the base currency you control.

1.00100,000 base units
0.1010,000 base units
0.011,000 base units

Typical retail forex convention. Broker specifications take precedence.

01 / What is a lot?

Forex-এ Lot হলো Position Size Express করার Convention। 1 Standard Lot সাধারণত 100,000 Base Currency Units। EUR/USD-এ 1 Standard Lot মানে €100,000 Exposure, $100,000 নয়। USD/JPY-এ 1 Standard Lot মানে $100,000 Exposure।

Base units = number of lots × contract size

100,000 units per standard lot is a widespread retail FX convention, not universal across every broker, CFD, futures contract or asset.

02 / Standard, mini, micro and nano

Lot Size-এর চারটি Common Category মুখস্থ না করে 10-এর Power দিয়ে বুঝো। Standard থেকে Mini = 1/10, Micro = 1/100 আর Nano = 1/1000।

TypeLotsBase units
Standard1.00100,000
Mini0.1010,000
Micro0.011,000
Nano0.001100

Not all brokers offer nano sizes or increments of 0.001 lot.

03 / See the scale

একই EUR/USD Pair-এ Lot Size বাড়লে Market Exposure Proportionally বাড়ে।

Original relative-scale illustration

Mini · 10,000 units

Micro · 1,000 units

Bars are proportional to base-currency units, not to profitability.

04 / Reading 0.01, 0.10 and 1.00

Broker Order Ticket-এ Volume 0.01 দেখলে সাধারণত 1,000 Base Units। 0.10 হলো 10,000 আর 1.00 হলো 100,000।

0.01Micro · 1,000
0.10Mini · 10,000
1.00Standard · 100,000

Always confirm the instrument's contract size before using these conversions.

05 / The base currency changes what the units mean

GBP/USD-এ 0.10 Lot মানে £10,000। USD/JPY-এ 0.10 Lot মানে $10,000। EUR/GBP-এ 0.10 Lot মানে €10,000।

EUR/USD0.10 lot = €10,000
GBP/USD0.10 lot = £10,000
USD/JPY0.10 lot = $10,000

06 / Lot size versus notional value

Base Units আর Account Currency Notional Value এক না। EUR/USD 1.1000 হলে 0.10 Lot = €10,000, যার USD Notional Value $11,000।

10,000 EUR × 1.1000 USD/EUR = $11,000

This is hypothetical notional exposure. It is not the cash required to open a leveraged position.

07 / Lot size versus margin

0.10 Lot মানে Broker-কে €10,000 Deposit দিতে হবে, এমন নয়। Leveraged Product-এ Margin Required হতে পারে Notional Exposure-এর একটি Portion। কিন্তু Loss হিসাব হয় Exposure-এর Price Movement অনুযায়ী, শুধু Deposit অনুযায়ী নয়।

Important distinction
$11,000Hypothetical USD notional
$366.67Illustrative 3.333% initial margin

At 30:1 leverage, $11,000 ÷ 30 ≈ $366.67. This example does not establish what leverage or margin your broker legally offers.

08 / Lot size and pip value

EUR/USD-এর Pip Size সাধারণত 0.0001। তাই 1 Lot-এ 100,000 × 0.0001 = $10/Pip। Micro Lot-এ $0.10/Pip।

Pip value (quote currency) = base units × pip size
EUR/USD lotsBase unitsUSD/pip
0.001100$0.01
0.011,000$0.10
0.1010,000$1
1.00100,000$10
2.00200,000$20

09 / USD/JPY is different

USD/JPY-এ Pip Size 0.01 JPY per USD। 0.10 Lot = 10,000 USD, তাই 1 Pip = ¥100। USD Account-এ Convert করতে Current USD/JPY Rate লাগে।

10,000 × 0.01 = ¥100/pip
At USD/JPY 150.00: ¥100 ÷ 150 = ~$0.67/pip

10 / EUR/GBP and account currency

EUR/GBP 0.10 Lot মানে €10,000 Base Exposure। 1 Pip = £1। USD Account হলে GBP/USD 1.2500 ধরে £1 = $1.25।

10,000 × 0.0001 = £1/pip
£1 × 1.2500 USD/GBP = $1.25/pip

Illustrative synchronized conversion rates, excluding conversion costs.

11 / Calculate P&L from lots

EUR/USD 0.20 Lot Long Entry Ask 1.1002 আর Exit Bid 1.1022। Position Size = 20,000 EUR।

20,000 × (1.1022 − 1.1002) = $40 gross gain

Actual entry and exit prices already incorporate the relevant quote sides. Do not subtract the spread twice. Commissions and financing remain additional.

12 / Losing example: lot size magnifies losses

EUR/USD 1.00 Lot-এ Price 30 Pips Against গেলে Quote Currency Loss প্রায় $300। 0.01 Lot-এ একই 30 Pip Move হলে প্রায় $3।

−$3001.00 lot × 30 pips × $10/pip
−$30.01 lot × 30 pips × $0.10/pip

Assumes a linear EUR/USD position, excluding fees and slippage.

13 / Position sizing from a risk budget

ধরো Demo Account-এ তুমি একটা Trade-এর Planned Price Risk $20 রাখতে চাও, Stop Distance 25 Pips। EUR/USD-এ $20 ÷ 25 = $0.80 per Pip।

$20 ÷ 25 = $0.80/pip
$0.80 ÷ $10 per standard-lot pip = 0.08 lots

0.08 lots = 8,000 EUR base units. This is a planning estimate, not a guaranteed maximum loss: stop orders may slip or gap.

14 / Account balance does not determine a safe lot

$1,000 Account থাকলে 0.10 Lot Safe, এমন কোনো Universal Rule নেই। Stop Distance, Volatility, Leverage, Broker Costs, Maximum Loss Tolerance আর Available Margin সব Matter করে।

Compare two hypothetical trades

0.10 lot EUR/USD at 10-pip adverse move: about −$10 before costs.

0.10 lot EUR/USD at 100-pip adverse move: about −$100 before costs.

Same lot size. Ten times the price risk.

15 / Contract size is not always 100,000

FX Spot-style Retail Contract-এ Standard Lot প্রায়ই 100,000 Base Units। কিন্তু Forex Futures-এর Exchange-defined Contract Size আলাদা হতে পারে। Gold, Oil, Indices আর Crypto CFD-তে 1 Lot-এর Meaning আরও আলাদা হতে পারে।

Check these broker fields: contract size, volume minimum, volume step, volume maximum, tick size, tick value, margin currency, profit currency and trading hours.

Never copy an EUR/USD lot formula directly into XAU/USD, a futures contract or a non-linear option without reviewing its specification.

16 / Minimum lot and volume step

Broker যদি Minimum 0.01 Lot আর Volume Step 0.01 দেয়, তুমি 0.015 Lot Order দিতে পারবে না। 0.08 Lot Valid হতে পারে, কিন্তু 0.085 Invalid।

0.08Valid for 0.01 step
0.085Invalid for 0.01 step

Round down a risk-based size to a permitted step when you need to avoid increasing planned exposure. Recalculate risk after rounding.

17 / Hedging, netting and partial closes

কিছু Trading Account-এ Multiple Long/Short Positions আলাদা রাখা যায় (Hedging Mode)। অন্য Account-এ Opposing Trades Net হয়ে যেতে পারে। Partial Close মানে Position-এর কিছু Units Close করা।

Example: 0.30 lots EUR/USD long and a partial close of 0.10 lots leaves 0.20 lots open, if the broker supports that operation and no other trades change the exposure.

Do not assume two opposite 0.10-lot positions eliminate all risk, fees or margin requirements. Account mode and provider rules matter.

18 / Scaling in and scaling out

একটা Trade 0.10 Lot দিয়ে Open করে পরে আরও 0.10 Lot Add করলে Total Exposure 0.20 Lot হয়। Average Entry Price Calculate করতে Each Fill-এর Base Units দিয়ে Weight করতে হবে।

Buy 10,000 EUR at 1.1000 and another 10,000 EUR at 1.1020:

Weighted entry = (10,000×1.1000 + 10,000×1.1020) ÷ 20,000 = 1.1010

New total = 20,000 EUR = 0.20 lots. Spread, commission and financing can change the economic break-even.

19 / Why adding to a loser is dangerous

Price তোমার Against গেলে Position Double করলে Potential Loss Speed-ও বাড়তে পারে। “Martingale” বা Unlimited Averaging Down কোনো Guaranteed Recovery Method নয়।

Suppose you increase exposure from 0.10 to 0.40 lots. For EUR/USD, a further 20-pip adverse move now represents about $80 in additional price loss rather than $20, before costs.

More exposure is not the same as a better trade.

20 / Lot size versus percentage of equity

Equity-এর 1% Risk Rule শুনে Directly 0.01 Lot Choose করা যায় না। আগে Stop Distance এবং Pip Value Estimate করতে হবে। তারপর Volume Calculate করতে হবে।

Planned risk amount = account equity × chosen risk fraction
Allowed pip value = planned risk amount ÷ stop distance
Lots = allowed pip value ÷ account-currency pip value per lot

A chosen percentage is a personal risk constraint, not a promise of safety. Account conversion, costs and slippage still apply.

21 / Practical demo workflow

Demo Platform-এ এই Checklist Follow করো।

  1. Choose EUR/USD and confirm its contract size.
  2. Read minimum lot, volume step and account currency.
  3. Enter 0.01 lot in the order preview without submitting.
  4. Estimate pip value and 10-, 25- and 50-pip losses.
  5. Compare the margin requirement with notional exposure.
  6. Check bid, ask, commission and overnight charges.
  7. Repeat with 0.10 lot to see how exposure scales.
  8. Journal the results and verify them against the platform's contract specification.

22 / Common mistakes

Professional Skill মানে শুধু Lot Size Table মুখস্থ না।

  1. Thinking 1 lot always means $100,000.
  2. Confusing margin with total exposure.
  3. Assuming $10/pip applies to every pair.
  4. Ignoring quote-to-account currency conversion.
  5. Using a lot step the broker does not allow.
  6. Assuming a stop guarantees the planned loss.
  7. Applying FX lot sizes to gold, indices or futures.
  8. Increasing lots just to recover a loss.

23 / Practice lab

নিজে Solve করো, তারপর Answer Open করো।

A. How many base units is 0.35 lot under a 100,000-unit standard contract?

Answer

35,000 base units.

B. EUR/USD 0.05 lot. What is USD pip value?

Answer

5,000 × 0.0001 = $0.50/pip.

C. USD/JPY 0.20 lot at 160.00. Pip value in JPY and approximate USD?

Answer

20,000 × 0.01 = ¥200/pip. ¥200 ÷ 160 = $1.25/pip.

D. EUR/USD USD account, $30 risk budget and 20-pip stop. What idealized lot size?

Answer

$30 ÷ 20 = $1.50/pip; $1.50 ÷ $10 = 0.15 lots, before fees and slippage.

E. GBP/USD 0.25 lot at 1.2800. Approximate USD notional?

Answer

25,000 GBP × 1.2800 = $32,000.

F. Minimum 0.01, step 0.01. Risk formula gives 0.067 lots. What conservative permitted volume?

Answer

0.06 lots, then recalculate actual planned risk.

24 / Mastery check

Answers না দেখে Explain করো।

1. What is a standard lot of EUR/USD?

Conventionally 100,000 EUR of base-currency exposure, subject to the contract specification.

2. Why isn't 0.10 lot always $10,000?

0.10 lot is usually 10,000 units of the base currency, which might be EUR, GBP, USD or another currency.

3. How do lot size and pip value relate?

For a linear exposure, doubling base units doubles pip value in the quote currency.

4. What is the difference between margin and notional exposure?

Notional is the value of the underlying exposure. Margin is collateral required under the product and broker rules.

5. Why must a lot-size calculation consider stop distance?

The same volume can create very different planned losses over different adverse price movements.

6. What should you check before trading an unfamiliar instrument?

Contract size, tick and pip definitions, min/step volume, currency conversion, margin, fees and execution rules.
