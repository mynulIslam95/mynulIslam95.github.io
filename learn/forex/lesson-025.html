Module 02 · Market Structure and Participants

Bid-Ask Dynamics

Understand how bid and ask prices move, why the midpoint can be misleading and how quote dynamics affect trade execution. বাংলা ও English explanations, worked calculations and practical assignments.

32 teaching sections 20 mastery questions Quote analyzer project

Study objectives and readiness

What you should be able to do

  1. Identify the correct quote side for entries and exits.
  2. Calculate midpoint, spread and transaction costs.
  3. Explain parallel, asymmetric and widening quote changes.
  4. Analyze demo bid/ask data without assuming that midpoint is executable.

Use demo or hypothetical data. Completion does not establish live-trading proficiency.

Three prices, different purposes

MidReference average
AskDealer sells base

01 · Bid and ask from first principles

Bid is the price at which a quoting dealer is willing to buy the base currency. Ask is the price at which the dealer is willing to sell it. The customer's buy usually uses ask and sell uses bid.

Dealer-এর Bid-এ তুমি Sell করো, Ask-এ Buy করো।

02 · The quote is a pair of prices

EUR/USD 1.1000/1.1002 means bid 1.1000 USD per EUR and ask 1.1002 USD per EUR. The two numbers should not be treated as interchangeable.

একটি FX Quote-এ Bid আর Ask দুটো Price থাকে।

03 · Spread in pips

For EUR/USD, one pip is usually 0.0001. A 1.1000 bid and 1.1002 ask give (1.1002−1.1000)/0.0001 = 2 pips.

Spread = (Ask − Bid) ÷ Pip Size।

04 · Midpoint or midprice

The midpoint is (bid+ask)/2. For 1.1000/1.1002 it is 1.1001. The midpoint is a reference, not necessarily an executable price.

Midprice Reference, Guaranteed Fill নয়।

05 · Bid-ask bounce

Successive transactions can alternate between bid and ask even if the midpoint stays constant. A chart of transaction prices may therefore show small oscillations unrelated to a change in fundamental value.

Midprice Same থেকেও Last Trade Price Up-Down হতে পারে।

06 · How dealers update both sides

A dealer can raise both bid and ask after a change in valuation, widen the gap during uncertainty or skew one side to manage inventory.

Bid আর Ask একসাথে বা আলাদাভাবে Move করতে পারে।

07 · Parallel quote shift

A quote changing from 1.1000/1.1002 to 1.1005/1.1007 raises the midpoint by 5 pips while preserving a 2-pip spread.

Both Side +5 Pip হলে Spread Same থাকে।

08 · Symmetric spread widening

A quote changing from 1.1000/1.1002 to 1.0997/1.1005 preserves midpoint 1.1001 but widens the spread from 2 to 8 pips.

Midpoint Same থাকলেও Trading Cost বাড়তে পারে।

09 · Asymmetric quote change

If bid falls from 1.1000 to 1.0998 while ask stays 1.1002, the midpoint falls 1 pip and the spread widens from 2 to 4 pips.

শুধু Bid Move করলেও Mid আর Spread Change হয়।

10 · Market maker inventory skew

A dealer with excess EUR inventory might make selling EUR less attractive and buying EUR from clients less attractive, depending on its risk policy. Actual quote adjustments depend on hedging and competition.

Inventory Risk Quote Skew-এর কারণ হতে পারে।

11 · Order flow and quotes

Aggressive buying can consume available asks, encouraging providers to reprice upward. The size and persistence of any move depend on replenishment and other order flow.

Buy Pressure সবসময় Predictable Price Rise Guarantee করে না।

12 · Depth behind the best quote

The best ask may only be executable for a limited size. A large buy can consume multiple ask levels, raising its average price.

Top Ask Price-এ Unlimited Size পাওয়া যায় না।

13 · Price improvement

A buy order can sometimes fill below the quoted reference ask and a sell above the reference bid, subject to venue rules and available liquidity.

Better-than-Expected Fill Possible।

14 · Spread markup and commission

One broker may add a markup to a liquidity provider's quote, while another may show a tighter spread and charge commission. Compare all-in cost.

Tight Spread মানেই Cheapest Account নয়।

15 · Variable spread behavior

Spreads may tighten with competition and available liquidity or widen with uncertainty, reduced depth and fast price changes.

Spread Fixed ধরে Strategy Test করো না।

16 · JPY and fractional pip quotes

USD/JPY commonly uses 0.01 as a pip; many platforms display an additional fractional digit. Always verify pip conventions for the instrument.

JPY Pair-এর Pip Size সাধারণত 0.01।

17 · Trade direction and quote side

A long EUR/USD enters at ask and closes at bid. A short enters at bid and closes at ask. Using midpoint for both would overstate performance.

Long: Ask → Bid। Short: Bid → Ask।

18 · Long trade worked example

Buy 10,000 EUR at ask 1.1002 and close at bid 1.1010. Gross P&L is (1.1010−1.1002)×10,000 = $8 before commissions and financing.

Long Trade-এর Gross P&L = (Exit Bid − Entry Ask) × Units।

(1.1010 − 1.1002) × 10,000 = $8

19 · Short trade worked example

Sell 20,000 EUR at bid 1.1000 and cover at ask 1.0994. Gross P&L is (1.1000−1.0994)×20,000 = $12 before commissions and financing.

Short Trade-এর Gross P&L = (Entry Bid − Exit Ask) × Units।

(1.1000 − 1.0994) × 20,000 = $12

20 · Immediate round-trip cost

If bid/ask stay 1.1000/1.1002, a 10,000-EUR buy followed immediately by a sell loses $2 before other fees. This is a simplified spread-only example.

Instant Entry-Exit করলেই Spread Cost হয়।

21 · Spread cost by position size

For EUR/USD, 2 pips on 1,000 EUR is about $0.20, on 10,000 EUR about $2 and on 100,000 EUR about $20, assuming the same executable spread.

Position Size বড় হলে Spread Dollar Cost বাড়ে।

22 · Bid and ask charts

Some platforms draw candles from bid quotes only, others offer ask or midpoint views. Always check which series you are looking at.

Chart-এর Candle কোন Quote থেকে এসেছে তা Check করো।

23 · Stops and bid-ask triggers

A long's sell stop may trigger on bid; a short's buy stop may trigger on ask. Broker-specific trigger policies and order types must be verified.

Ask Chart না দেখলে Short Stop Trigger Confusing হতে পারে।

24 · Spread-only stop event

Suppose a short has a buy stop at 1.1004 and the ask rises from 1.1002 to 1.1005 while the midpoint stays near 1.1001. The buy stop may trigger under ask-based rules.

Midprice Same থেকেও Ask Stop Trigger করতে পারে।

25 · Effective spread

Execution-quality analysis may compare actual trade price to a contemporaneous midpoint. For a buy, twice (fill−mid) is one common effective-spread convention, with units of price.

Effective Spread Quoted Spread থেকে Different হতে পারে।

Effective spread for buy = 2 × (fill − reference midpoint)

26 · Quoted versus realized spread

Quoted spread is the displayed ask-bid gap. Realized spread measures a dealer's execution relative to a later midpoint under a specified methodology. It is not the dealer's net profit.

Realized Spread মানে Dealer-এর Guaranteed Profit নয়।

27 · Latency and stale quotes

Displayed bid and ask may change between observation and order execution. Record quote and fill timestamps before attributing differences to slippage or misconduct.

Different Timestamp-এর Quote Compare করো না।

28 · Cross-broker quote comparison

Compare the same pair, UTC timestamp, account type, order size and pricing convention. A different spread alone does not establish wrongdoing.

Broker Compare করতে Same Conditions দরকার।

29 · Bid-ask data in backtesting

Use historical bid/ask where available. A midpoint-only backtest can miss entry, exit and stop-trigger costs, especially during stressed periods.

Midprice Backtest Real Cost Underestimate করতে পারে।

30 · Practical spreadsheet task

Create 12 hypothetical quote observations. Calculate spread, midpoint, changes in each side and cost for 1k, 10k and 100k EUR positions. Label the market condition for each observation.

Spreadsheet-এ Bid/Ask Quote Analyzer বানাও।

  1. Enter UTC time, bid, ask and size.
  2. Calculate spread = (ask − bid)/0.0001 for EUR/USD.
  3. Calculate midpoint = (bid + ask)/2.
  4. Compute spread-only cost for 1k, 10k and 100k units.
  5. Discuss quote latency and missing liquidity depth.

31 · Common mistakes

Do not buy at bid, assume the midpoint is executable, confuse a pipette with a pip, double-count spread and slippage or infer market manipulation from a single quote difference.

Quote Convention ভুল হলে P&L Calculation ভুল হবে।

32 · Mastery project

Explain how three quotes with the same midpoint can create different execution costs. Show a 2-pip, 4-pip and 8-pip spread and compute costs for 10,000 EUR. Then test a demo platform's chart convention.

Same Midprice-এ Different Spread Cost নিজে Calculate করো।

1. Dealer bid means what?

Price the dealer is willing to pay for the base currency.

2. Customer market buy typically uses which side?

Ask.

3. Customer market sell typically uses which side?

Bid.

4. Spread of 1.1000/1.1002?

2 pips.

5. Midpoint of 1.1000/1.1002?

1.1001.

6. Does midpoint guarantee a fill?

No.

7. Spread of 1.0997/1.1005?

8 pips.

8. Midpoint of 1.0997/1.1005?

1.1001.

9. 1.1000/1.1002 becomes 1.1005/1.1007: spread?

Still 2 pips.

10. 1.1000/1.1002 becomes 1.0998/1.1002: spread?

4 pips.

11. Long 10k at ask 1.1002, close bid 1.1010: gross P&L?

$8.

12. Short 20k at bid 1.1000, cover ask 1.0994: gross P&L?

$12.

13. Immediate spread-only cost at 2 pips for 100k EUR/USD?

$20.

14. Does a bid-only chart always show the ask?

No.

15. Can an ask-based buy stop trigger due to widening?

Yes.

16. What is one common pip size for USD/JPY?

0.01 JPY per USD.

17. Is quoted spread identical to net dealer profit?

No.

18. What is the common effective spread for a buy filled at 1.1003 with midpoint 1.1001?

2×(1.1003−1.1001)=0.0004, or 4 pips.

19. Should a backtest use bid/ask where available?

Yes.

20. Does different broker pricing alone prove manipulation?

No.
