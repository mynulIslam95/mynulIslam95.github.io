Margin Calls, Stop-Outs and Negative Balance Protection

The core distinction

Warning, forced closure and liability protection are three different things.

A margin call is not guaranteed advance notice. Stop-out is not your personal stop-loss. Negative balance protection is not insurance against losing your deposit.

01 · The three terms are not interchangeable

Margin call হলো insufficient collateral নিয়ে warning বা demand। Stop-out হলো broker-এর position close করার process। Negative balance protection হলো eligible account-এর liability limit, যাতে CFD trading-এর কারণে account balance zero-এর নিচে owed amount না থাকে। তিনটি আলাদা জিনিস।

AlertMargin call
ClosureStop-out
Loss cap*Negative balance protection

*Account-level legal protection where applicable, not a cap on each trade or protection of deposited funds.

02 · How margin calls happen

Open Trade-এর Floating Loss Equity কমায়। Used Margin Same থাকলে Margin Level কমে। Broker-এর Warning Threshold Cross করলে Alert, additional funds request অথবা trading restriction হতে পারে। Some brokers close positions without a separate warning.

Margin level (%) = equity ÷ used margin × 100

03 · Equity, used margin and free margin

Example: balance $1,000, floating P&L −$300, used margin $400.

$700Equity
$300Free margin
175%Margin level
Equity = $1,000 − $300 = $700

Broker accounting and fees can change these values.

04 · Original visual: the falling margin level

Used Margin $400 Fixed ধরে Floating Loss বাড়লে Margin Level কীভাবে নামে দেখো।

05 · Margin call does not promise time to react

“Margin Call আসলে Deposit করবো” Strategy Reliable নয়। Fast Market-এ Price দ্রুত Move করে, Spread Widen হতে পারে আর Broker Automated Liquidation Trigger করতে পারে। Notification আসার আগেও Closure হতে পারে।

Warning ≠ grace period. Check the broker's contract and do not rely on receiving a phone call.

06 · What is stop-out?

Stop-out-এ Broker এক বা একাধিক Position Reduce বা Close করে Margin Requirement Restore করার চেষ্টা করে। Which Position First Close হবে, Partial Close Allow করবে কি না, Threshold কত, সব Broker/Product Rules-এর ওপর নির্ভর করে।

Stop-out is an account-protection mechanism, not a guaranteed exit price.

07 · EU retail CFD close-out framework

EU Retail CFD Framework-এ Account-Level Margin Close-Out Protection সাধারণভাবে Minimum Required Initial Margin-এর 50% Level-এ Trigger হয়। এখানে Broker-কে One or More CFDs Close করতে হয়, কিন্তু Exact Fill Price Guarantee করা হয় না। National Permanent Measures এবং Legal Entity Check করো।

Illustrative threshold calculation

Required initial margin across CFDs = €800. 50% threshold = €400 account funds calculated under the applicable rule.

€800 × 0.50 = €400

This regulatory calculation is not always identical to a trading terminal's displayed equity/used-margin ratio. Refer to the applicable legal definitions and broker disclosures.

08 · Stop-out versus your stop-loss

Stop-Loss হলো তুমি Position-এর জন্য Set করো। Stop-Out হলো Account Margin Rules-এর কারণে Broker Initiate করে। Stop-Loss Trigger হতে পারে অনেক আগে। দুটোতেই Gap বা Slippage হলে Expected Price-এ Fill নাও হতে পারে।

Stop-lossTrade-level exit instruction
Stop-outAccount-level forced reduction/closure

09 · Why liquidation may not prevent a large loss

Market Gap, Illiquidity, News Shock, Technical Delay আর Wider Spread-এর কারণে Broker Planned Threshold-এর চেয়ে Worse Price-এ Position Close করতে পারে। Liquidation Mechanism থাকা মানে Equity Always Positive থাকবে, এমন নয়।

Example: a position is marked near a liquidation threshold before a weekend. Monday's first executable price may be materially worse. The loss can exceed the amount suggested by the earlier screen.

10 · What negative balance protection means

Eligible EU Retail CFD Account-এ Negative Balance Protection সাধারণত CFD Trading Liability-কে CFD Account-এর Funds পর্যন্ত Limit করে। অর্থাৎ Extreme Move-এ Trading Account Negative হলেও Provider-কে সেই CFD Loss-এর জন্য Extra Money Owe করতে হবে না, যদি Protection Apply করে।

But: deposited funds can still be lost. Other accounts, products, fees or legal arrangements may have different rules. Read the precise terms.

11 · Negative balance protection is not deposit insurance

NBP Market Loss থেকে Deposit Protect করে না। এটা Bank Deposit Guarantee, Investor Compensation Scheme বা Broker Insolvency Protection-এর Same Thing না।

NBPLimits certain trading debt below zero
Not NBPInsurance against losing your investment

12 · Hypothetical negative account scenario

Suppose an eligible CFD account has €1,000 in funds. A severe gap creates a €1,400 trading loss, leaving a raw calculated account value of −€400.

€1,000 − €1,400 = −€400 (pre-protection calculation)

Applicable Negative Balance Protection-এর অধীনে CFD Trading Liability Account Funds-এর বাইরে Extend না করার কথা। Exact Adjustment, Eligibility এবং Timing Provider-এর Terms অনুযায়ী হবে।

Do not assume protection applies to every leveraged instrument or offshore entity.

13 · Who may not have the same protection?

Professional Clients, Some Non-EU Entities, Certain Futures/Other Products এবং Different Jurisdictions-এ EU Retail CFD Protections Apply নাও করতে পারে। “Broker EU-তে Famous” হওয়া যথেষ্ট নয়। তোমার Contract কোন Legal Entity-এর সঙ্গে সেটা Check করো।

Verify: entity name, regulator register, account classification, product and written negative balance terms.

14 · Why higher leverage increases stop-out risk

Leverage নিজে Fixed Position-এর Pip Value Change করে না। কিন্তু High Maximum Leverage ব্যবহার করে Larger Position Open করলে Small Adverse Move Equity দ্রুত Reduce করতে পারে। Margin Buffer-ও ছোট হতে পারে।

Price P&L ≈ position units × exchange-rate movement

15 · Spread widening and news

Economic News-এর সময় Bid-Ask Spread Widen হতে পারে। Long Position Mark-to-Market সাধারণত Bid Side-এ আর Short Ask Side-এ Reflect হতে পারে। ফলে Midprice খুব বেশি না নড়লেও Floating P&L খারাপ দেখাতে পারে।

Spread widening can push an already stressed account toward a margin threshold. Check the broker's valuation and close-out method.

16 · Hedged positions may still use margin

EUR/USD Long আর EUR/USD Short একই Size থাকলে “Risk Zero” ধরে নিও না। Broker Hedging Mode, Spread, Financing, Margin Offsets এবং Execution Matter করে। Netting Accounts-এ Opposite Order Existing Position Reduce করতে পারে।

Margin offsets are provider-specific and can change.

17 · Adding funds versus reducing exposure

Extra Deposit Equity Raise করতে পারে, কিন্তু Losing Trade-এর Fundamental Risk Remove করে না। Position Reduce করলে Exposure এবং Future Price Sensitivity কমতে পারে। Neither action guarantees recovery or prevents further loss.

Example: 20,000 EUR/USD exposure has roughly $2 per pip. Reducing to 10,000 units lowers future price sensitivity to roughly $1 per pip, before costs.

18 · Build your own warning buffer

Broker Liquidation Threshold-কে Personal Risk Limit বানিও না। আগে Planned Risk Budget, Stop Distance, Position Size, Available Margin আর Gap Risk Consider করো। Multiple Positions থাকলে Combined Currency Exposure Review করো।

Plan before entry: risk amount → stop distance → units → margin check

19 · Broker due diligence checklist

  1. Confirm the exact contracting legal entity and regulator.
  2. Confirm retail or professional client classification.
  3. Identify whether the instrument is a CFD, spot forex, future or another derivative.
  4. Read initial and maintenance margin terms.
  5. Find warning, close-out and liquidation policies.
  6. Read negative balance protection eligibility and exceptions.
  7. Review execution, slippage, spread and financing policies.
  8. Test margin calculations in a demo environment.

20 · Practice and mastery check

1. Balance $1,500, floating −$450, used margin $350. Calculate equity, free margin and margin level.

Equity $1,050. Free margin $700. Margin level = 1,050 ÷ 350 × 100 = 300%.

2. Required initial margin €600. What is 50%?

€300. This arithmetic alone does not determine the exact broker liquidation time or price.

3. Is margin call the same as stop-out?

No. A margin call is a warning or collateral demand, while stop-out is a broker-driven position closure or reduction.

4. Does a stop-loss guarantee a maximum loss?

No. Market gaps, slippage and execution terms can create a worse fill.

5. Does negative balance protection mean you cannot lose your deposit?

No. It limits certain debt beyond account funds where applicable, not ordinary trading losses.

6. Do all professional and offshore accounts receive EU retail CFD protection?

No. Classification, entity, jurisdiction and instrument must be checked.

7. What happens to pip sensitivity when position size is halved?

For a linear FX exposure, it approximately halves.

8. Why can a margin level drop even without a large midprice move?

Spread widening, costs, conversion changes or increased margin requirements may affect the account.
