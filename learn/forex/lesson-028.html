Module 02 · Market Structure and Participants

Centralized Futures Volume versus Decentralized Spot Forex

Understand exactly what currency futures volume measures, why spot forex has no universal trade tape and how to compare futures, tick volume and open interest without misleading conclusions. English ও বাংলা with calculations and research assignments.

40 teaching sections 20 mastery questions Data comparison project

Study objectives and readiness

What you should be able to do

  1. Distinguish exchange futures volume from fragmented OTC spot data.
  2. Calculate contract notional and explain volume versus open interest.
  3. Identify limitations of tick volume, CVD and retail depth feeds.
  4. Write a documented futures-versus-spot dataset comparison.

Use hypothetical or demo data. Lesson completion does not demonstrate live trading proficiency.

One distinction to remember

FuturesExchange-specific contracts, trades and reported volume
OTC spot FXFragmented venues, dealer quotes and no universal public tape

01 · The central distinction

Exchange-traded currency futures report trades and contracts for their own listed markets. Global OTC spot FX is decentralized, so no single public tape captures all worldwide spot transactions.

Futures Volume একটি Exchange-এর Trade দেখায়। Spot FX-এর Global Single Tape নেই।

02 · What centralized means

A futures exchange operates under common trading and clearing rules, with exchange-specific order books, trades and market data. Centralized does not mean all global currency activity occurs there.

Centralized মানে Exchange-এর নিজস্ব Rules আর Data।

03 · What decentralized means

OTC spot FX trades across banks, non-bank liquidity providers, brokers and electronic venues. Quotes and executed volume differ across counterparties and feeds.

Spot Forex-এ অনেক Dealer আর Venue থাকে।

04 · Spot, forward and futures are not identical

Spot involves near-term currency exchange; forwards specify future exchange terms bilaterally; futures are standardized exchange-listed contracts with margin and daily settlement rules.

Spot, Forward আর Futures আলাদা Product।

05 · What a futures contract represents

A listed FX futures contract has an exchange-defined currency pair, contract size, expiration and settlement process. Always check the specific product specification.

Futures Contract-এর Size আর Expiry Exchange Define করে।

06 · Example: EUR futures quotation

An illustrative EUR/USD-style futures price of 1.1000 means USD per EUR for a specified contract. Product conventions vary, so never infer contract units from a spot chart.

Contract Specification না দেখে Pip Value Assume করো না।

07 · What futures traded volume counts

Reported futures volume commonly counts the number of contracts traded over an interval. It does not automatically equal the number of euros exchanged in spot markets.

Futures Volume সাধারণত Contract Count।

08 · Worked contract-volume conversion

Suppose a hypothetical contract represents 125,000 EUR and 800 contracts trade. Contract face amount is 100,000,000 EUR. This is contract notional, not global spot turnover.

800 × 125,000 = 100 Million EUR Contract Notional।

800 contracts × 125,000 EUR/contract = 100,000,000 EUR notional

09 · Notional is not cash exchanged

Futures traders typically post margin rather than paying full notional upfront. The notional amount is exposure, not necessarily the cash transferred in each trade.

Notional Value মানে Deposit Amount নয়।

10 · Open interest versus volume

Volume counts contracts traded during a period; open interest counts outstanding contracts under exchange reporting rules. One contract may trade multiple times without increasing open interest.

Volume আর Open Interest আলাদা।

11 · Worked open-interest example

A new buyer and new seller opening a contract can increase open interest by one; two closing counterparties can reduce it by one. A transfer between an opener and closer can leave it unchanged.

Open Interest Change Trade-এর Position Effect-এর ওপর Depend করে।

12 · Order book and depth in futures

Exchange feeds can publish bids, asks and available sizes for a listed contract, subject to feed level, venue rules and hidden liquidity. The book covers that contract on that venue.

Futures DOM Venue-Specific, Global FX Book নয়।

13 · Why spot has no consolidated tape

Many OTC trades are bilateral and not all trade details are publicly broadcast in real time. Retail platforms typically show a subset of quotes and activity.

Retail Spot Chart সব Bank-এর Trades দেখায় না।

14 · Retail tick volume

Some spot platforms show the number of quote updates in each bar, often called tick volume. It is feed-specific and is not consolidated traded spot volume.

Tick Volume Quote Change Count হতে পারে।

15 · Tick volume worked example

If a broker's EUR/USD feed updates its quote 320 times in a minute, tick volume may show 320. It does not mean 320 contracts or 320 million euros traded.

320 Tick মানে 320 Global Trade নয়।

16 · Dealer-reported volume

A broker may publish its own client or liquidity-provider trade volume. That dataset is useful only with clear definitions and cannot automatically be generalized to all spot FX.

Broker Volume তার নিজের Coverage অনুযায়ী।

17 · BIS turnover surveys

The Bank for International Settlements periodically surveys global FX turnover across reporting dealers and instruments. Survey estimates are not a live global order book or per-minute tape.

BIS Survey Market Size Estimate দেয়, Live Flow নয়।

18 · Why futures and spot prices relate

Spot and futures respond to shared macroeconomic information, interest-rate expectations and hedging activity. Arbitrage links markets but basis and product rules matter.

Spot আর Futures Related, কিন্তু Price Always Identical নয়।

19 · Basis and carry

The difference between a futures price and spot price can reflect interest-rate differentials, time to expiry and market frictions. A price gap alone is not free arbitrage.

Futures-Spot Gap Guaranteed Free Profit নয়।

20 · Expiry and rolling

A futures contract expires. Continuous charts often stitch contracts together using a roll method that can affect historical prices and volume comparisons.

Continuous Futures Chart-এ Roll Adjustment Check করো।

21 · Volume shifts around rollover

Activity can migrate from an expiring futures contract to the next one. A sudden drop in front-month volume may be mechanical rather than loss of interest in the currency.

Expiry Near হলে Volume অন্য Contract-এ Move করে।

22 · Futures volume as a proxy

Exchange futures volume can offer a transparent view of participation in that contract. It can complement spot analysis but cannot measure total global spot activity.

Futures Volume Useful Proxy, Complete Spot Data নয়।

23 · CVD in futures

Cumulative volume delta sums buyer-initiated minus seller-initiated traded volume when the venue feed or classification supports it. It describes that futures market, not all FX.

Futures CVD শুধুই ঐ Contract/Venue-এর Flow।

24 · CVD in retail spot

A platform may estimate delta from quote ticks or limited trade feeds. The label CVD does not guarantee true global aggressor-side trade volume.

Spot CVD Data Source না জেনে Trust করো না।

25 · Example: two feeds disagree

Feed A reports 500 quote changes while feed B reports 720 over the same minute. Neither number by itself establishes how many spot trades occurred globally.

Different Broker Tick Volume Different হতে পারে।

26 · Time-zone and session alignment

Exchange futures have defined trading schedules and reporting conventions. OTC spot operates across global sessions. Align timestamps and market holidays before comparing charts.

UTC আর Session Align না করলে Comparison ভুল হতে পারে।

27 · Pair direction and inversion

A futures contract may use a quotation convention different from a spot pair. If comparing inverses, transform prices and return directions carefully.

USD/JPY আর JPY/USD Same Direction নয়।

28 · Data quality and revisions

Check whether feeds are real time or delayed, include block trades, use adjusted histories and define volume consistently. Missing data can distort conclusions.

Delayed Data দিয়ে Real-Time Signal Claim করো না।

29 · Price discovery leadership

At different times, information may appear first in futures, spot or related markets. Observed lead-lag patterns require rigorous timestamped testing and can change.

Futures Always Lead করে এমন Rule নেই।

30 · Correlation is not identical volume

Spot and futures prices may correlate strongly even when their reported volumes measure different populations and units.

Price Correlation মানে Volume Equivalent নয়।

31 · Execution differences

Futures execution follows exchange contract and clearing rules; retail OTC spot execution follows broker and product terms. Spreads, commissions, funding and leverage differ.

Same Price View হলেও Trading Cost Different।

32 · Margin differences

Futures margin is governed by exchange and broker requirements; leveraged retail spot or CFDs follow different regulatory and broker rules. Margin is collateral, not a guaranteed maximum loss.

Margin Deposit Maximum Loss Guarantee নয়।

33 · Regulatory and counterparty considerations

Exchange clearing changes counterparty arrangements, while OTC dealer or CFD exposure depends on contract terms and regulation. Neither eliminates market risk.

Exchange Cleared হলেও Market Risk থাকে।

34 · When futures data helps

Use contract-specific volume, open interest and depth to study that contract's liquidity, session behavior and execution. State the venue and contract month.

Futures Data দিয়ে Futures Market Analyze করো।

35 · When spot data helps

Use broker-specific bid/ask histories to study your actual instrument's spreads, slippage and trading conditions. Clearly label the feed limitations.

Spot Broker Data Execution Cost Study করতে Useful।

36 · Comparison table and checklist

Compare traded volume, open interest, depth, price, trading hours and fees with definitions before combining datasets.

37 · Spreadsheet exercise

Build two datasets: hypothetical futures contracts traded and spot quote updates. Convert futures contracts to notional and show why tick counts cannot be converted to global spot turnover.

Futures Contracts থেকে Notional বের করো, Spot Ticks থেকে নয়।

  1. Enter futures contract size 125,000 EUR and volumes 100, 250 and 800 contracts.
  2. Calculate notionals: 12.5m, 31.25m and 100m EUR.
  3. Enter spot feed quote-update counts 160, 220 and 320.
  4. Explain why no valid universal EUR conversion exists for those tick counts.
  5. Document what each dataset actually measures.

38 · Practical mini research

Pick one exchange-listed currency futures contract and one spot broker feed. Document contract specification, timestamps, data coverage, roll conventions and limitations.

Data Source Documentation লিখো।

39 · Common myths

Do not claim retail spot has a universal order book, futures volume equals spot volume, tick count equals cash turnover or a CVD divergence guarantees a reversal.

Volume Indicator Guaranteed Profit Signal নয়।

40 · Mastery project

Create a short research note with a data dictionary, example conversions, volume/open-interest chart definitions and five limitations. Explain which conclusions are supported and which are not.

Data Dictionary আর Limitations সহ Research Note তৈরি করো।

  1. Define futures contract, exchange, month and reporting interval.
  2. Define the spot feed and whether its volume means ticks or trades.
  3. Show a contract-to-notional conversion with units.
  4. Describe rollover, delayed feeds and missing OTC venue coverage.
  5. Conclude only what the selected data can support.

Mastery check

আগে নিজে Answer করো, তারপর Solution দেখো।

1. Is all global spot FX traded on one exchange?

No, OTC spot FX is decentralized.

2. What does futures volume usually count?

Number of contracts traded in the reporting period.

3. Does futures volume equal worldwide spot volume?

No.

4. 800 contracts of 125,000 EUR represent what notional?

100,000,000 EUR.

5. Does 100 million EUR notional mean 100 million EUR was posted as margin?

No.

6. What is open interest?

Outstanding futures contracts under the exchange's reporting methodology.

7. Can volume increase without open interest increasing?

Yes.

8. Does a retail spot tick equal a global executed trade?

No.

9. What does a tick volume value of 320 usually mean?

320 quote updates under that feed's definition, not 320 global trades.

10. Does the BIS FX survey provide a live global order book?

No.

11. Can futures and spot prices differ?

Yes, due to carry, basis and other factors.

12. What happens to contract volume near expiry?

It may migrate to the next contract.

13. Does futures CVD cover all spot FX order flow?

No.

14. Can two spot brokers report different tick volumes?

Yes.

15. Why align timestamps before comparing datasets?

To avoid misleading comparisons across time zones, sessions and delays.

16. Can a futures quote be inverted relative to a spot pair?

Yes, check the product convention.

17. Is a futures margin deposit the maximum possible loss?

No.

18. What does a futures DOM show?

Depth for the listed contract and venue, subject to feed rules.

19. Does high price correlation imply equivalent volume data?

No.

20. Name two required items in a data dictionary.

Instrument and venue, units, timestamp convention, feed coverage or contract month.
