01 · What is the interbank FX market?

The interbank market refers to currency trading among banks and professional dealers through bilateral relationships, electronic venues and brokers. It is not one centralized exchange.

Interbank Market কোনো একক Exchange নয়। Bank আর Professional Dealer-রা বিভিন্ন Channel-এ Trade করে।

02 · Who can participate?

Large banks and other eligible institutions generally need credit arrangements, onboarding and access to suitable venues. Retail traders normally reach FX markets through intermediaries.

Retail Trader সাধারণত সরাসরি Interbank Market Access পায় না।

03 · Interdealer versus dealer-to-client

Interdealer trading is between professional dealers. Dealer-to-client trading serves corporations, funds and other customers. Prices and terms can differ between these channels.

Dealer-to-Dealer আর Dealer-to-Client Quote এক নাও হতে পারে।

04 · Bilateral credit relationships

Institutions set credit limits for each counterparty. Even an attractive quote might not be tradable if credit or settlement limits are exhausted.

Credit Limit থাকলে Quote দেখা গেলেও Trade নাও হতে পারে।

05 · Electronic communication venues

Professional FX trades can occur on electronic matching venues, request-for-quote systems or direct dealer platforms. Their order and execution rules differ.

সব Electronic Venue একইভাবে Order Match করে না।

06 · What does a market maker do?

A market maker continuously or selectively quotes prices at which it may buy or sell, subject to size and market conditions. It earns trading revenues while taking inventory and execution risk.

Market Maker Bid-Ask Quote দেয় এবং Inventory Risk নেয়।

07 · Bid and ask from the dealer's view

If EUR/USD is 1.1000 bid / 1.1002 ask, the dealer buys euros at 1.1000 and sells euros at 1.1002. The customer buying euros typically pays the ask.

Customer Buy করলে Ask, Sell করলে Bid।

Bid 1.1000Dealer buys EUR
Ask 1.1002Dealer sells EUR

08 · How spreads are measured

For most non-JPY pairs, one pip is 0.0001. A 1.1000/1.1002 quote has a 0.0002 difference, equal to 2 pips.

EUR/USD-এ 0.0001 = 1 Pip।

09 · Spread is not pure profit

A dealer may pay hedging costs, venue fees, funding costs and suffer adverse price moves. The quoted spread is not automatically the dealer's net profit.

Dealer পুরো Spread-টাই Profit হিসেবে রাখতে পারে না।

10 · Inventory risk example

A dealer sells EUR 100,000 to a client at 1.1002 and later buys back EUR 100,000 at 1.1005. The simplified price difference is a $30 loss before other costs.

Inventory Hedge Late হলে Dealer-ও Loss করতে পারে।

(1.1002 − 1.1005) × 100,000 = −$30

11 · Quote skewing

A dealer holding too many euros may adjust bid and ask quotes to encourage trades that reduce inventory. This can move its quotes relative to other dealers.

Inventory Position অনুযায়ী Dealer Quote Change করতে পারে।

12 · Adverse selection

When better-informed or faster counterparties trade against stale quotes, a market maker can lose. Dealers may widen spreads or update quotes more quickly during uncertainty.

Fast Market-এ Stale Quote Risk বাড়ে।

13 · Market making versus manipulation

Providing two-way liquidity and managing inventory are ordinary market functions. Manipulative conduct is different and can violate applicable laws.

Market Maker হওয়া আর Market Manipulation এক নয়।

14 · Firm and indicative quotes

An indicative price is informational. A firm quote is intended for execution under its terms, but size, time limits and venue rules still matter.

Indicative Quote মানে Guaranteed Execution নয়।

15 · Last look

Some FX liquidity providers have a disclosed short window to accept or reject a request. Its use, controls and permissible practices depend on venue rules and standards.

Last Look থাকলে Order Reject হতে পারে।

16 · Request for quote

An RFQ workflow asks one or more dealers for a price. The response may have a short validity window, and accepting it is subject to agreed rules.

RFQ-তে Dealer-এর কাছে Price চাওয়া হয়।

17 · Streaming quotes

Electronic price streams update rapidly. A displayed top-of-book price may be valid only for a particular size and customer relationship.

Best Quote সব Volume-এর জন্য Available নয়।

18 · Liquidity aggregation

Aggregators compare prices from multiple providers. Routing logic, credit and execution constraints can matter as much as the headline best bid or ask.

Aggregator Multiple LP-এর Quote Compare করে।

19 · Depth of market

Depth is available size across price levels. A large order can use up the best quote and fill remaining units at worse prices.

বড় Order একাধিক Price Level-এ Fill হতে পারে।

20 · Market impact and slippage

A trade may execute away from the initially observed price due to changing quotes, insufficient depth or latency. Positive and negative slippage are possible.

Slippage Positive বা Negative দুটোই হতে পারে।

21 · Market orders and resting orders

An aggressive order seeks immediate execution against available liquidity. A resting limit order waits at a chosen price, but may never fill.

Market Order Liquidity Consume করে, Resting Order Wait করে।

22 · Why retail quotes differ

Retail brokers may use different liquidity providers, markups, pricing engines and execution policies. Two brokers need not show identical candles or spreads.

Broker A আর Broker B-এর Price Slightly Different হতে পারে।

23 · Interbank does not mean one official price

Spot FX is fragmented. A quote depends on provider, venue, size, time and counterparty. There is no universal interbank tick everyone must display.

একটা Universal Interbank Price Feed নেই।

24 · Interbank activity and sessions

Liquidity often changes as Tokyo, London and New York business hours overlap. Holidays and economic events can matter more than session labels.

Session Overlap-এ Liquidity বদলাতে পারে।

25 · News and liquidity withdrawal

During major releases, dealers may rapidly reprice, reduce size or temporarily withdraw quotes. Spreads and rejection rates can increase.

High-Impact News-এ Spread Widen হতে পারে।

26 · The role of prime brokers

Prime brokers can provide eligible institutions credit intermediation and operational support for executing with multiple dealers. Access depends on agreements.

Prime Brokerage Institutional Access Facilitate করে।

27 · Counterparty risk

In bilateral OTC transactions, one party may fail to pay or perform. Credit limits, collateral and netting help manage this exposure but cannot erase it.

Counterparty Default Risk সবসময় Zero হয় না।

28 · Settlement risk

Currencies may be paid in different payment systems. Payment-versus-payment mechanisms can reduce certain settlement exposures for eligible transactions.

Settlement Risk আর Market Price Risk আলাদা।

29 · Tick volume versus traded volume

Retail platform tick volume counts quote updates or similar feed events, not all global spot-FX transactions. It can be a proxy, not a universal trade tape.

MT4 Tick Volume মানে Global Forex Volume নয়।

30 · Worked spread comparison

Dealer A quotes 1.1000/1.1002, a 2-pip spread. Dealer B quotes 1.0999/1.1004, a 5-pip spread. For 10,000 EUR, the approximate immediate round-trip spread costs are $2 and $5, ignoring other fees.

Same Pair-এ Different Spread-এর Cost Different।

2 pips × $1/pip = $2; 5 pips × $1/pip = $5

31 · Worked multi-level fill

Suppose a buy order for EUR 100,000 fills EUR 40,000 at 1.1002 and EUR 60,000 at 1.1004. The weighted average entry is 1.10032, not 1.1002.

Weighted Average Price দিয়ে Real Execution Cost হিসাব করো।

(40,000 × 1.1002 + 60,000 × 1.1004) ÷ 100,000 = 1.10032

32 · Simple execution-quality journal

Record UTC timestamp, instrument, order size, displayed bid/ask, actual fill, slippage, fees, session and any rejection. Review results across many trades.

Journal-এ Quote আর Actual Fill দুটোই Save করো।

33 · Practical demo exercise

Open two demo quote feeds, record simultaneous EUR/USD bid and ask, compare spreads and observe changes around a scheduled economic release. Do not risk real funds.

Demo Data দিয়ে Market Making আর Spread Behavior Observe করো।

  1. Use demo quotes only.
  2. Record two brokers at the same UTC timestamp.
  3. Compute each spread in pips.
  4. Compare normal versus news conditions.
  5. Explain any observed differences without assuming manipulation.

34 · Common misconceptions

Interbank is not a secret single-price feed, market makers are not inherently scams, advertised zero spread is not zero cost and deep markets can still gap.

Market Structure বুঝলে Misleading Claims এড়ানো সহজ।

Mastery check

Answer নিজে বের করে তারপর Solution দেখো।

1. Is the interbank FX market one centralized exchange?

No.

2. What does a market maker quote?

Bid and ask prices under stated conditions.

3. EUR/USD 1.1000/1.1002 spread?

2 pips.

4. Does a dealer keep the entire spread as profit?

Not necessarily. Hedging and other costs apply.

5. Dealer sells 100,000 EUR at 1.1002 and repurchases at 1.1005: simplified P&L?

−$30 before fees.

6. What is quote skewing?

Adjusting bid/ask quotes, often to manage inventory.

7. Does an indicative quote guarantee execution?

No.

8. What is last look?

A short acceptance or rejection window permitted under certain FX trading arrangements.

9. Can a large order fill at multiple prices?

Yes.

10. What is the weighted average of 40,000 at 1.1002 and 60,000 at 1.1004?

1.10032.

11. Can different brokers show different quotes?

Yes.

12. Does tick volume show all global spot-FX trades?

No.

13. What is counterparty risk?

Risk that a contractual counterparty fails to perform.

14. Does liquidity guarantee zero slippage?

No.
