Bid, Ask and Spread: The Real Cost of Entering a Trade

One sentence you must remember

You buy at ASK and sell at BID.

Dealer যে Price-এ তোমার কাছ থেকে Base Currency কিনবে সেটা Bid। যে Price-এ তোমাকে Base Currency Sell করবে সেটা Ask। Difference হলো Spread।

Spread = Ask − Bid

01 / What are bid and ask?

ধরো তুমি Currency Exchange Counter-এ গেলে। Counter তোমার কাছ থেকে Euro কিনবে এক Rate-এ, আর তোমাকে Euro Sell করবে একটু বেশি Rate-এ। এই দুই Rate হলো Bid আর Ask।

Dealer's perspective · original visual
1.1000BID · dealer BUYS 1 EUR
1.1002ASK · dealer SELLS 1 EUR

Customer হিসেবে তুমি EUR Buy করলে Ask Pay করবে, EUR Sell করলে Bid Receive করবে। Bid এবং Ask সবসময় Base Currency-এর প্রতি 1 Unit-এর Quote Currency Price।

Bid ≤ Ask · Spread = Ask − Bid

Illustrative EUR/USD dealer quote, not a live market price.

02 / Why does a broker show two prices?

একটা Single Price দেখলে মনে হতে পারে Buy আর Sell একই Rate-এ হবে। কিন্তু বাস্তবে Dealer বা Liquidity Provider Quote-এর মধ্যে Difference রাখতে পারে। সেটা Market-Making Cost, Risk, Liquidity আর Commercial Markup-এর সঙ্গে Related হতে পারে।

Order direction map
SELL EURYou hit dealer BID
⇄
BUY EURYou lift dealer ASK

Memory trick: You buy at the higher ask and sell at the lower bid, assuming a conventional two-way quote.

03 / Spread calculation, step by step

EUR/USD Bid 1.1000 আর Ask 1.1002 হলে Spread হলো 0.0002 USD per EUR। Standard EUR/USD Convention-এ 1 Pip = 0.0001, তাই Spread = 2 Pips।

1.1002 − 1.1000 = 0.0002 = 2 pips

For a typical JPY-quoted pair, 1 pip is 0.01. Example: USD/JPY bid 150.12 and ask 150.15 gives 0.03 JPY per USD, or 3 pips.

150.15 − 150.12 = 0.03 = 3 JPY-pair pips

Pip conventions vary by instrument. Some platforms display fractional pips, often called pipettes. Lesson 005 will cover pip value and contract sizes in detail.

04 / Why does a fresh trade often show an immediate loss?

ধরো EUR/USD Buy করেছো Ask 1.1002-এ। একই মুহূর্তে Close করতে চাইলে সাধারণত Bid 1.1000-এ Sell করতে হবে। তাই Market একদম না নড়লেও Round-Trip Position-এর Gross P&L Negative দেখাতে পারে।

Long position · same moment
1.1002Entry: buy at ASK
1.1000Exit: sell at BID
1.1000 − 1.1002 = −0.0002 USD per EUR

For a hypothetical 10,000 EUR base-currency position, the difference is −$2 before any other fees: 10,000 × −0.0002 = −2 USD.

Illustration assumes the same quote remains available and no slippage or commission.

05 / Long and short: which side closes the position?

The execution matrix
OPEN LONGBuy at ASK
CLOSE LONGSell at BID
OPEN SHORTSell at BID
CLOSE SHORTBuy at ASK

Long Position-এর Profit Calculate করতে Exit Bid থেকে Entry Ask Subtract করো। Short Position-এর Profit Calculate করতে Entry Bid থেকে Exit Ask Subtract করো।

Long P&L (quote currency) = Q × (Exit bid − Entry ask)
Short P&L (quote currency) = Q × (Entry bid − Exit ask)

Q is the base-currency position size. These formulas exclude commission, financing, conversion costs and execution adjustments.

06 / Worked long trade: profitable or not?

EUR/USD Ask 1.1002-এ 10,000 EUR Buy করলে। পরে Market Up হলো, কিন্তু Exit করতে হবে Bid-এ। ধরো Exit Bid = 1.1010।

Long trade calculation
10,000 × (1.1010 − 1.1002) = $8 gross profit

The entry ask is already included in this calculation. Do not subtract the entry spread a second time. Additional commissions or financing would reduce the result.

এখানে 1.1002 থেকে 1.1010 হলো 0.0008, অর্থাৎ 8 Pips Net Price Movement between actual fills। 10,000 EUR Position-এর জন্য EUR/USD-এ প্রতি Pip-এর Quote-Currency Value $1, তাই $8।

07 / Worked short trade: the reverse logic

এবার EUR/USD Short। Entry Bid 1.1000। পরে Exit Ask 1.0992। 10,000 EUR Short Exposure-এর Gross Result কত?

10,000 × (1.1000 − 1.0992) = $8 gross profit

If the exit ask had risen to 1.1012 instead, the gross result would be −$12. A short position does not avoid spreads or execution costs.

08 / Fixed vs variable spreads

Fixed spread

Advertised as relatively stable

কিছু Broker Fixed Spread Offer করে, কিন্তু Product Terms-এ Exceptions, Requotes বা Restrictions থাকতে পারে। “Fixed” লেখা থাকলেই Every Condition-এ Same Fill Guaranteed নয়।

Variable spread

Changes with conditions

Liquidity কমলে বা Volatility বাড়লে Spread Widen হতে পারে। Calm Active Session-এ Tight হতে পারে।

Always verify actual contract terms and execution reports rather than assuming one model is universally cheaper.

09 / Why spreads widen

Common causes
  1. Major news: unexpected inflation, central bank or employment releases can cause sharp repricing.
  2. Thin liquidity: fewer executable quotes during certain market hours or holidays.
  3. Volatility: dealers may widen quotes when inventory or hedging risk rises.
  4. Market disruption: sudden gaps, technical problems or unusual conditions.
  5. Provider policy: broker markups and execution models differ.

Professional Trader News-এর আগে শুধু Chart দেখে Entry নেয় না। Spread আর Execution Condition-ও Check করে। Tight Spread কোনো Permanent Guarantee না।

10 / Spread vs commission vs slippage vs swap

CostMeaningExample
SpreadDifference between ask and bid1.1002 − 1.1000
CommissionSeparate fee charged by provider$3 each side on an illustrative trade
SlippageDifference between requested and executed priceRequested 1.1002, filled 1.1004
Swap / financingHolding-related charge or credit under contractOvernight financing
Conversion feeAccount-currency conversion chargeUSD profit converted to EUR

Commission-Free মানেই Cost-Free না। Broker Spread-এর মধ্যে Markup রাখতে পারে। SEC Investor Bulletin-ও এই Risk Highlight করেছে।

11 / Total-cost example with commission

Imagine a 10,000 EUR long trade. Entry ask = 1.1002, exit bid = 1.1010. The provider charges $0.70 per side, and there are no other charges.

Gross P&L = 10,000 × (1.1010 − 1.1002) = $8
Total commission = $0.70 + $0.70 = $1.40
Net P&L = $8 − $1.40 = $6.60

এখানে Spread Already Actual Entry Ask আর Exit Bid-এর মধ্যে Accounted For। আবার Extra Spread Subtract করলে Double-Count হবে।

12 / Break-even: why the mid-price must move

Suppose bid = 1.1000, ask = 1.1002 and the spread stays constant at 2 pips. The mid-price is 1.1001. You buy at 1.1002.

Illustrative break-even timeline
1.1001Entry midpoint
1.1003Later midpoint

If the later spread is still 2 pips, the later bid is 1.1002. You can then close the long at its original entry ask and break even before commission.

Required mid move = 0.0002 = 2 pips

This is the spread at entry under the constant-spread assumption, not a universal rule. Changing spreads or fees change break-even.

13 / Bid/ask charts and stop orders

অনেক Platform-এর Candle Chart Bid, Mid বা অন্য Price Basis-এ Draw হতে পারে। কিন্তু Buy/Sell Execution আলাদা Side-এ হয়। তাই Chart-এর High/Low দেখে Stop Trigger বুঝতে গেলে Platform-এর Price Basis জানতে হবে।

Example of a quote-side mismatch

Suppose a platform displays bid candles and the current bid is 1.2000 while ask is 1.2003. A buy order normally executes against the ask, not the bid chart line. Trigger rules for pending orders, stops and take-profits are broker- and instrument-specific.

Do not assume that every chart uses bid or that every broker triggers every stop the same way. Check the actual order policy and historical quote-side data.

14 / Spread measurement and effective spread

Displayed Spread হলো Quote-এর Ask-Bid Difference। কিন্তু Actual Execution-এ Slippage বা Price Improvement হলে Effective Trading Cost আলাদা হতে পারে।

Advanced concept · effective spread

For a single trade, a common market-microstructure measure is:

Effective spread = 2 × |execution price − midpoint at trade time|

Example: midpoint 1.1001, buy executed at 1.1003 → effective spread = 0.0004, or 4 EUR/USD pips.

This is a market-microstructure metric for a single fill, not automatically the full round-trip trading cost. Use comparable timestamps and quote sources.

15 / Spread in pips is not the same as spread in money

2 Pip Spread ছোট শোনাতে পারে। কিন্তু Position Size বড় হলে Dollar Cost বড় হয়। EUR/USD-এ 10,000 EUR Position আর 100,000 EUR Position-এর Cost Same হবে না।

Same spread, different exposure
$22 pips × $1/pip · 10,000 EUR
$202 pips × $10/pip · 100,000 EUR

These figures apply to the conventional EUR/USD pip size of 0.0001 and USD-denominated quote-currency P&L, before other costs. For other pairs and account currencies, pip values differ.

16 / A professional execution checklist

  1. Identify the base and quote currency.
  2. Read the live bid and ask, not only the chart.
  3. Calculate the spread in price units and pips.
  4. Estimate monetary cost for your actual position size.
  5. Check commission, overnight financing and currency conversion fees.
  6. Review news events, rollover periods and unusual liquidity conditions.
  7. Check the provider's stop trigger, slippage, rejection and execution rules.
  8. Test the complete process in a demo environment before risking money.

17 / Practice lab

A. EUR/USD bid 1.08740, ask 1.08755. What is the spread?

Answer

1.08755 − 1.08740 = 0.00015 = 1.5 pips or 15 pipettes if one pipette = 0.00001.

B. USD/JPY bid 149.82, ask 149.86. Spread?

Answer

0.04 JPY = 4 pips with 0.01 JPY per pip.

C. You buy 20,000 EUR/USD at ask 1.1004 and close at bid 1.1011. Gross P&L?

Answer

20,000 × (1.1011 − 1.1004) = $14, excluding fees.

D. You sell 20,000 EUR/USD at bid 1.1000 and close at ask 1.1008. Gross P&L?

Answer

20,000 × (1.1000 − 1.1008) = −$16.

E. Broker A quotes 0.5 pip spread and $7 round-trip commission per 100,000 EUR. Broker B quotes 1.2 pip spread and no commission. Which has lower simplified round-trip cost for a 100,000 EUR/USD trade?

Answer

At $10/pip, A = $5 spread + $7 commission = $12. B = 1.2 × $10 = $12. They tie under these assumptions, before slippage, financing and changes in spread.

18 / Mastery check

নিজে Answer দাও। তারপর Explanation Expand করো।

1. Why does a customer buy at the ask?

The ask is the dealer's offered selling price for the base currency.

2. Why does a newly opened long often show negative P&L?

It enters at the ask but is marked or closed against the lower bid, creating a spread cost even if the market midpoint has not moved.

3. How do you calculate a short trade's gross quote-currency P&L?

Base-currency quantity × (entry bid − exit ask).

4. Does zero commission mean zero trading cost?

No. Spread, financing, slippage and other charges can still apply.

5. Why may the chart price differ from the price triggering an order?

The chart may display bid, ask or midpoint, while order execution and trigger rules use specific quote sides under the broker's terms.

6. Why must a professional measure execution quality beyond the displayed spread?

Because fills can differ from quotes due to slippage, price improvement, rejections, latency and market conditions.
