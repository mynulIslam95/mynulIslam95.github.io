The big idea · এক লাইনে

Forex = One currency exchanged for another

ধরো তুমি Germany-তে আছো আর USA-তে Travel করবে। তোমার কাছে Euro আছে, কিন্তু USA-তে Dollar দরকার। তুমি EUR Sell করে USD Buy করলে। এটাই Foreign Exchange বা FX-এর Basic Idea।

Forex trading means exchanging or taking exposure to one currency relative to another. A currency's exchange rate is always a relative price, not a standalone value.

01 / Currency exchange: a real-life example

ধরো Exchange Rate হলো EUR/USD = 1.1000। এর মানে 1 Euro-এর Value 1.10 US Dollar। এখন তুমি €100 Exchange করলে, Fees বাদ দিয়ে $110 পাবে।

Illustration · not a live quote

→
$110US Dollar (USD)
€100 × 1.1000 USD/EUR = $110

Assumes no spread, commission or other conversion charges. Real exchange providers charge costs.

এখন Rate যদি 1.2000 হয়, একই €100 দিয়ে $120 পাওয়া যাবে। অর্থাৎ Euro, Dollar-এর তুলনায় Stronger হয়েছে। Rate যদি 1.0500 হয়, €100 দিয়ে $105 পাবে।

EUR strengthens

1.1000 → 1.2000

One euro buys more dollars.

EUR weakens

1.1000 → 1.0500

One euro buys fewer dollars.

Important: This example explains currency conversion. It is not a claim that a retail leveraged forex trade is the same as exchanging cash.

Forex Market-এ Currency Pair দেখা যায়, যেমন EUR/USD, GBP/USD বা USD/JPY। একটা Pair-এ দুইটা Currency থাকে। First Currency-কে Base Currency আর Second Currency-কে Quote Currency বলে।

EUR/USD = 1.1000 means 1 EUR costs 1.10 USD

তুমি EUR/USD Buy করলে সাধারণভাবে Euro-এর Value Dollar-এর তুলনায় বাড়বে বলে Position নিচ্ছো। Sell করলে উল্টো Direction-এর Exposure নিচ্ছো। তবে Spot, Futures আর CFD-এর Contract Rules এক নয়।

03 / Why does the forex market exist?

Forex শুধু Chart দেখে Profit করার জন্য তৈরি হয়নি। Global Economy-তে প্রতিদিন Currency Exchange দরকার হয়।

International business

German Company USA থেকে Product কিনলে USD Payment করতে হতে পারে। তখন EUR থেকে USD Conversion দরকার।

Travel and remittances

Travel, বিদেশে Study বা Family-কে Money পাঠানোর সময় Currency Convert হয়।

Hedging

একটা Company ভবিষ্যতের Exchange Rate Change থেকে Loss কমাতে Forward Contract ব্যবহার করতে পারে।

Investment and speculation

Investors Foreign Assets কিনতে Currency Change করে। Traders Future Price Movement থেকে লাভ করার চেষ্টা করে, কিন্তু Loss-ও হতে পারে।

Stock Exchange-এর মতো Forex-এর সব Trade এক জায়গায় হয় না। Global Spot Forex Market অনেকটা Decentralized Network। Dealers আর Trading Venues-এ আলাদা Quotes থাকতে পারে।

06 / How big is the market?

The BIS 2025 Triennial Survey measured approximately $9.5 trillion in average daily OTC FX turnover in April 2025, across spot and FX derivative instruments. That is trading turnover, not retail trader profit or money available to win.

07 / How do traders try to make money?

ধরো EUR/USD 1.1000 থেকে 1.1050 হলো। তুমি যদি Euro-এর Value বাড়বে বলে Position নিতে, তাহলে Direction ঠিক হতো। কিন্তু Actual Profit নির্ভর করবে Position Size, Spread, Commission, Financing Cost আর Execution-এর ওপর।

Worked example · simplified spot position

You buy €1,000 at 1.1000 USD/EUR and later sell it at 1.1050.

€1,000 × (1.1050 − 1.1000) = $5 gross gain

If the price instead falls to 1.0950, the gross result is −$5. Both calculations exclude fees, spreads and financing. A leveraged product can create much larger gains or losses relative to the deposit used.

খেয়াল করো, Direction ঠিক Guess করলেই Professional Trader হওয়া যায় না। Risk Control, Trading Costs, Tested Strategy আর Discipline জরুরি। এগুলো পরে আলাদা Module-এ বিস্তারিত শিখবো।

Risk warning

Leverage মানে অল্প Deposit দিয়ে বড় Position-এর Exposure নেওয়া। এতে Profit যেমন বড় হতে পারে, Loss-ও দ্রুত বড় হতে পারে। Demo Account-এ Practice না করে Real Money Risk নেওয়া উচিত নয়।

Forex trading does not provide guaranteed returns. Check your product type, broker authorization and applicable retail protections before depositing money.

09 / Common beginner mistakes

  1. “Forex is a single exchange.” No. Spot FX is largely OTC and fragmented.
  2. “The biggest market must be easy money.” Trading volume does not establish profitability.
  3. “Buying EUR/USD means buying both currencies.” You are taking a relative currency position.
  4. “A chart predicts the future.” A chart records observations; predictive claims require testing.
  5. “If I am right about direction, I must profit.” Costs, timing, size and leverage matter.

11 / Mastery check

Answer without looking at the notes. Expand each item to compare your answer.

1. What is the exchange rate EUR/USD measuring?

The number of US dollars needed to purchase one euro, before transaction-specific costs.

2. Why is forex called a decentralized market?

Most spot FX trading takes place OTC across dealers and venues rather than on one central exchange.

3. Why might a German importer need dollars?

To pay an American supplier that invoices in USD.

4. Does a CFD position mean you own the underlying currency?

Usually not. A CFD is a contract whose value follows the referenced market price under its terms.

5. Why doesn't correct price direction guarantee a profit?

Because position size, timing, spread, fees, financing, slippage and leverage affect the final result.
