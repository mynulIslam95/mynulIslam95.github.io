Inside Bars and Outside Bars

Understand two-candle range relationships, distinguish consolidation from expansion and test breakout hypotheses without treating chart shapes as guaranteed signals.

2. Exact classification rules

Let bar 1 be the completed mother/reference bar with H₁ and L₁. Let bar 2 be the next completed bar with H₂ and L₂. All OHLC must be valid: H≥max(O,C) and L≤min(O,C).

Inside: H₂ ≤ H₁ and L₂ ≥ L₁, with H₂ < H₁ or L₂ > L₁
Outside: H₂ ≥ H₁ and L₂ ≤ L₁, with H₂ > H₁ or L₂ < L₁

Boundary policy: One equal boundary is allowed if the other is strictly contained or expanded. Two identical high-low ranges are classified as neither. Other charting platforms use different equality conventions, so disclose your rule before backtesting.

High-Low দুটোই সমান হলে এই Lesson-এর Rule অনুযায়ী Neither। Equal Boundary-এর Rule আগেই ঠিক করতে হবে।

3. Three worked EUR/USD examples

A. Inside bar

Mother O=1.0850, H=1.0880, L=1.0820, C=1.0870. Second O=1.0860, H=1.0870, L=1.0835, C=1.0840. Mother range=60 pips, inside range=35 pips. Range compression = 1−35/60 = 41.67%. Second high is 10 pips below the mother high and its low is 15 pips above the mother low.

B. Outside bar

Prior H=1.0870, L=1.0830. Current H=1.0890, L=1.0810, with valid O=1.0840 and C=1.0880. Prior range=40 pips; outside range=80 pips. Range expansion=(80/40−1)×100=100%. This says nothing certain about the next bar's direction.

C. Neither

Prior H=1.0880, L=1.0820. Current H=1.0890, L=1.0830. The second high breaks above the prior high, but its low remains above the prior low. It is neither contained nor containing. Calling it an outside bar would be mathematically incorrect.

Inside Bar-এ Range ছোট হয়। Outside Bar-এ Range বড় হয়। কিন্তু Direction বা Profit নিশ্চিত নয়।

4. Inside versus engulfing: do not confuse them

Inside barCurrent H-L range inside prior H-L rangeSmall body or imminent breakout
Outside barCurrent H-L range contains prior H-L rangeReal-body engulfing or reversal
Bullish engulfingUnder a stated rule, bullish second real body contains bearish first real bodyFull-range outside bar

Nested inside bars can be defined relative to the immediately preceding bar or to the original mother bar. Those definitions are not equivalent. Record which one your scanner uses.

6. Breakout, false breakout and execution realism

Some traders study entries above the mother-bar high or below its low after an inside bar. A breakout must be defined precisely: a wick breach, a candle close beyond the boundary or an executable stop order fill are different events. A false breakout also needs an explicit rule, such as price closing back inside within N completed bars. Choose N before testing.

For a hypothetical EUR/USD long, a midpoint price of 1.0881 above a 1.0880 mother high does not automatically prove that a buy stop was filled at 1.0881. Actual execution uses broker-specific ask prices and can include spread, slippage and gaps. OHLC alone cannot determine whether a stop-loss or take-profit was touched first inside the same bar.

Mid-price Breakout আর Broker-এর Ask-price Fill এক নয়। Stop Loss আগে Hit করেছে নাকি Target, শুধু OHLC দিয়ে সবসময় জানা যায় না।

7. Institutional context and market microstructure

Inside bars describe a narrower observed price range. They do not prove that banks are accumulating positions, that liquidity has been engineered or that a large order is pending. Outside bars describe range expansion and can occur around news, session transitions or ordinary volatility. Their cause cannot be established from the candle shape alone.

Forex is decentralized. Bid, ask and midpoint feeds may produce different highs and lows, especially during volatile periods or thin liquidity. Candle boundaries depend on broker server time and aggregation method. Compare like-for-like feeds before interpreting a pattern frequency.

8. Professional research protocol

  1. Choose a currency pair, timeframe, session calendar, quote side and data provider. Document missing and duplicate observations.
  2. Predefine inside/outside equality rules, mother-bar selection and the signal close timestamp.
  3. Predefine breakout threshold, entry order type, exit, stop, maximum holding period and no-trade conditions.
  4. Model bid/ask spread, commissions, slippage, latency, financing and unfilled orders. Do not infer intrabar fill ordering from OHLC.
  5. Compare inside-bar signals with an unconditional entry baseline, no-trade benchmark and outside-bar control group.
  6. Split development and untouched holdout periods chronologically. Avoid selecting rules using future outcomes.
  7. Report signal counts, net expectancy, drawdown, confidence intervals, costs and performance by volatility regime.
  8. Document every threshold tested and discuss multiple-comparison bias. Paper-test before risking money.

Future Data দিয়ে Past Signal বানানো যাবে না। Realistic Spread, Slippage, Baseline এবং Holdout দরকার।

10. Mastery examination · 20 questions

Q1. What defines an inside bar?

Current high is at most prior high and current low is at least prior low, with at least one strict boundary under this lesson's rule.

Q2. What defines an outside bar?

Current high is at least prior high and current low is at most prior low, with at least one strict boundary.

Q3. Does an inside bar mean a breakout will occur?

No. It describes range containment, not the probability or direction of a future breakout.

Q4. Does an outside bar mean reversal?

No. It can appear during continuation, reversal or volatile two-way trade.

Q5. What is the mother bar?

The earlier reference bar whose high-low range contains the inside bar.

Q6. Can inside bars have bullish bodies?

Yes. The range relationship is independent of open-close direction.

Q7. Can an outside bar close inside the prior body?

Yes. Outside is based on highs and lows, not body engulfment.

Q8. Is outside bar identical to engulfing candle?

No. Outside uses full ranges; real-body engulfing uses open-close containment.

Q9. Can both labels apply to the same two unequal ranges?

No, with the strict-boundary rules used here.

Q10. What about identical high and low ranges?

Neither label applies under our at-least-one-strict-boundary convention.

Q11. Can an inside bar equal one mother-bar boundary?

Yes, provided the other boundary is strictly inside.

Q12. What is a nested inside bar?

A sequence of bars with progressively contained ranges, defined explicitly.

Q13. When is a two-bar signal fully known?

Only after the second bar closes.

Q14. What is a false breakout?

A predeclared boundary breach followed by a defined failure to sustain the move.

Q15. Does a wick breach guarantee a fill?

No. Bid/ask, quote feed, order type, spread and slippage matter.

Q16. What does OHLC omit?

The order of intrabar highs/lows and detailed order-flow sequence.

Q17. Why distinguish mid, bid and ask candles?

Signals and executable prices can differ across quote sides.

Q18. What is the danger of using the mother-bar high from the future?

Look-ahead bias: only known completed bars may define the reference.

Q19. How should thresholds be selected?

Specify them before evaluating the untouched holdout to avoid overfitting.

Q20. What proves a profitable inside-bar strategy?

No single test proves future profitability; use robust out-of-sample evidence, realistic costs and uncertainty analysis.
