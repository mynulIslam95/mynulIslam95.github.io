Leverage and Margin: Understand the Exposure Before the Trade

30:1 Leverage আসলে কী? $500 Margin দিয়ে কত বড় Position Open করা যায়, কেন Small Market Move-এ Account-এর বড় Loss হতে পারে, আর EU Retail CFD Protection কীভাবে কাজ করে? Zero থেকে Professional Risk Awareness পর্যন্ত।

The single most important idea

Leverage changes collateral requirements. It does not change the risk of a fixed position.

01 / What leverage actually means

Concept 01

Leverage মানে অল্প Margin দিয়ে বড় Notional Exposure Control করা। 30:1 মানে সর্বোচ্চ 30 গুণ Exposure, 30 গুণ Guaranteed Profit নয়.

Notional exposure = position base units × current exchange rate

02 / Margin is collateral, not a fee

Concept 02

Margin হলো Position Support করার জন্য Required Collateral। এটা সাধারণত Trade Open করার সঙ্গে সঙ্গে Expense হয়ে যায় না। কিন্তু Floating Loss Equity কমিয়ে দেয়।

Initial margin ≈ account-currency notional ÷ permitted leverage

03 / A complete EUR/USD example

Concept 03

EUR/USD = 1.1000; Position = 10,000 EUR; USD Account; Leverage 30:1। USD Notional = $11,000। Required Initial Margin ≈ $366.67।

$11,000 ÷ 30 = $366.67

04 / Leverage does not change pip value

Concept 04

10,000 EUR/USD Units-এ 1 Pip = $1, সেটা 1:1 হোক বা 30:1। Leverage বদলায় Required Margin, একই Exposure-এর Pip Value নয়।

10,000 × 0.0001 = $1 per pip

05 / Same deposit, different exposure

Concept 05

$1,000 Account-এ 1,000 EUR Exposure আর 20,000 EUR Exposure-এর Risk Same নয়। Margin Permission বড় Position Allow করলেও সেটা নেওয়া বাধ্যতামূলক না।

Price P&L = base units × (exit rate − entry rate)

06 / How leverage magnifies percentage returns

Concept 06

$1,000 Equity-তে $10,000 Notional Position হলে Underlying 1% Change প্রায় $100 Gross P&L, অর্থাৎ Equity-এর 10%। Direction বিপরীত হলে একইভাবে Loss।

1% of $10,000 = $100 = 10% of $1,000

07 / Effective leverage vs broker maximum

Concept 07

Broker 30:1 Allow করলেও তুমি 5:1 Effective Leverage Use করতে পারো। Effective Leverage হলো Total Gross Notional Exposure ÷ Current Equity।

Effective leverage = gross notional ÷ equity

08 / Initial vs maintenance margin

Concept 08

Initial Margin হলো Open করার সময় Requirement। Maintenance Margin বা Close-Out Threshold নির্ধারণ করে Position কতটা Loss Tolerate করতে পারবে। Retail CFD Rules আর Broker Method আলাদা হতে পারে।

Do not treat a margin threshold as a stop-loss guarantee.

09 / Used margin, free margin and equity

Concept 09

Used Margin হলো Open Position Support করতে Allocated Amount। Equity = Balance + Floating P&L। Free Margin ≈ Equity − Used Margin, broker-specific adjustments বাদে।

Equity = balance + unrealized P&L

10 / Margin level

Concept 10

অনেক Retail Platform Margin Level % = Equity ÷ Used Margin × 100। Equity $600, Used Margin $300 হলে Margin Level 200%। Used Margin Zero হলে Percentage Undefined হতে পারে।

$600 ÷ $300 × 100 = 200%

11 / Margin call versus stop-out

Concept 11

Margin Call হলো Warning বা Additional Funds Demand। Stop-Out হলো Broker-initiated Position Closure। Threshold, Timing, Order Selection আর Protection Broker/Product/Jurisdiction অনুযায়ী বদলায়।

A margin call is not a promise that you can deposit before liquidation.

12 / EU retail CFD leverage limits

Concept 12

EU Retail CFD Product Intervention Rules-এ Major Currency Pair-এর জন্য 30:1 এবং Non-Major Currency Pair-এর জন্য 20:1 Initial Leverage Limit আছে। Other Instruments-এ Different Limits। National Measures এবং Classification Check করতে হবে।

30:1 major FX; 20:1 non-major FX (EU retail CFD framework)

13 / EU margin close-out rule

Concept 13

ESMA Framework-এ Retail CFD Account-এর Aggregate Margin Close-Out Threshold সাধারণভাবে Required Initial Margin-এর 50% Level-এ Applied হয়। এটা Individual Trade Stop-Loss নয়। Provider Terms Read করো।

50% of required initial margin is a regulatory close-out threshold, not a maximum loss.

14 / Negative balance protection

Concept 14

EU Retail CFD Protections Account-Level Negative Balance Protection Include করে, কিন্তু Product Eligibility, Legal Entity, Jurisdiction এবং Exceptional Conditions Check করতে হবে। এটা Trading Loss থেকে Protection নয়।

Protected account balance does not mean invested funds are protected.

15 / Why 500:1 can be dangerous

Concept 15

500:1 Marketing দেখে Risk কম মনে হতে পারে। Actually Small Deposit দিয়ে Huge Exposure নেওয়া যায়। 0.2% Adverse Move 500:1 Fully Used Margin-এর তুলনায় প্রায় 100% Equivalent হতে পারে, Costs বাদে।

500 × 0.2% = 100% of margin-equivalent exposure

16 / Margin vs risk budget

Concept 16

$500 Margin Required মানে $500 Maximum Loss না। Stop Distance, Position Size, Spread, Slippage, Financing এবং Gap Realized Loss Determine করে।

Planned price risk ≈ stop pips × account-currency pip value

17 / Stop-loss and gaps

Concept 17

Stop-Loss Order Normal Market-এ Risk Control Help করতে পারে, কিন্তু Sudden Gap বা Slippage হলে Worse Fill Possible। Broker Stop Type, Guaranteed Stop Availability ও Fees Check করো।

A stop order does not guarantee a fill at its trigger price.

18 / Multiple positions and portfolio exposure

Concept 18

EUR/USD Long এবং GBP/USD Long দুটোতেই USD Short Exposure থাকতে পারে। আলাদা Pair মানেই Independent Risk না। Correlation আর Concentration Monitor করো।

Gross notional and net currency exposure are different measures.

19 / Currency conversion and margin currency

Concept 19

USD Account-এ EUR/GBP Position Trade করলে Profit Currency GBP হতে পারে। Margin Account Currency-তে Convert হতে পারে। Broker Contract Specification-এ Profit Currency ও Margin Currency Check করো।

Quote-currency P&L must be converted to account currency.

20 / Interest, swap and financing

Concept 20

Leveraged Position Hold করলে Overnight Financing, Swap বা Other Charges Apply হতে পারে। Rate Differential থেকে Credit পাওয়ার Guarantee নেই। Weekend/Rollover Rules Vary।

Net P&L = price P&L − commissions ± financing − other costs

21 / Leverage and volatility stress test

Concept 21

$2,000 Equity এবং $20,000 Exposure হলে Effective Leverage 10:1। Underlying −3% Move ≈ −$600 Price P&L, অর্থাৎ Equity-এর 30%, Costs বাদে।

$20,000 × 3% = $600

22 / A sensible sizing workflow

Concept 22

প্রথমে Maximum Affordable Loss Decide করো, তারপর Stop Placement Justify করো, Pip Value Calculate করো, Lot Size Round Down করো, Margin Requirement Check করো। Leverage Limit দেখে Position Size Decide করবে না।

Risk budget → stop distance → pip value → lots → margin check

23 / Broker risk checklist

Concept 23

Regulated Legal Entity, Client Classification, Product Type, Leverage Cap, Margin Close-Out, Negative Balance Protection, Spread, Commission, Financing এবং Execution Policy Verify করো।

Regulation and protections depend on where and with whom you trade.

24 / Professional pitfalls

Concept 24

Margin-কে Fee মনে করা, Leverage-কে Profit Multiplier ভাবা, Pip Value Ignore করা, Stop-Out-কে Stop-Loss ভাবা, Correlated Trades Double Count না করা এবং High Leverage দিয়ে Loss Recover করতে যাওয়া Avoid করো।

Maximum leverage is not recommended leverage.

Practice lab and mastery check

Answer না দেখে Calculate করো। Wrong হলে Concept-এ ফিরে যাও।

1. EUR/USD 1.1000, 20,000 EUR exposure, USD account, 30:1 leverage. What is initial margin?

Notional = $22,000. Initial margin ≈ $22,000 ÷ 30 = $733.33.

2. $1,500 equity and $12,000 total gross notional. Effective leverage?

$12,000 ÷ $1,500 = 8:1.

3. 10,000 EUR/USD units. What is pip value in USD at 30:1 versus 10:1?

$1 per pip in both cases. Leverage changes margin, not pip value for fixed units.

4. $1,000 balance, −$240 floating P&L, $400 used margin. Equity, free margin and margin level?

Equity = $760. Free margin ≈ $360. Margin level = $760 ÷ $400 × 100 = 190%, before adjustments.

5. $25,000 notional, 2% adverse movement. Approximate price loss?

$500 before costs. If equity was $2,000, this is 25% of starting equity.

6. Is a 50% margin close-out threshold a guaranteed maximum loss?

No. It is a liquidation mechanism under applicable rules, not a guaranteed execution price or loss cap.

7. $500 risk budget, 50 pip stop, EUR/USD USD account. What base units correspond to $10/pip?

$500 ÷ 50 = $10/pip. 100,000 EUR base units, or 1.00 conventional standard lot. This is a mathematical exercise, not a suitable recommendation; fees and gaps may increase loss.

8. Why might EUR/USD and GBP/USD longs create concentrated risk?

Both are economically short USD versus another currency, so broad USD strength can hurt both positions.

Professional Forex case study · worked answer

Leverage is not a stop-loss

Worked calculation: EUR/USD notional EUR 10,000 at 1.1000 is USD 11,000. At 20:1 leverage, illustrative required margin is USD 550. A 100-pip adverse move on 10,000 EUR costs USD 100 before fees.

Professional limitation: Margin depends on broker and jurisdiction; leverage changes collateral required, not pip sensitivity for a fixed position.

বাংলা ব্যাখ্যা: Leverage Margin কমায়, কিন্তু একই Position-এর Pip Loss কমায় না।
