1. Executable EUR/USD trade calculator

For EUR/USD, a buy enters at ask and exits at bid. A sell enters at bid and exits at ask. P&L in USD = EUR units × signed exit-minus-entry price, minus USD fees. This is an illustrative spot-style P&L model, not a CFD contract specification.

Buy করলে Ask-এ Entry, Bid-এ Exit। Sell করলে Bid-এ Entry, Ask-এ Exit। Spread ও Commission বাদ দিয়ে Net P&L দেখো।

2. Risk sizing and drawdown mathematics

For EUR/USD with USD account currency, a one-pip move (0.0001) is USD 0.0001 per EUR. Illustrative risk units = dollar risk budget ÷ (stop distance in pips × 0.0001). This assumes a fixed stop fill and excludes gaps, slippage, financing and commissions. Real loss can exceed planned risk.

Drawdown recovery formula: required gain = drawdown ÷ (1 − drawdown), expressed as decimals. A 20% loss requires a 25% gain to return to the original equity.

3. Statistical edge, not chart-pattern certainty

Expectancy in R-multiples: E[R] = win probability × average win R − loss probability × average loss R. For a strategy with 40% wins, 2R average wins and 1R average losses: E[R] = 0.4×2 − 0.6×1 = +0.2R before unmodeled costs. Positive sample expectancy is not proof of a persistent edge.

Selection bias, nonstationarity, clustered losses, fat tails and regime shifts can invalidate naïve extrapolation. A confidence interval for the win rate alone is not a confidence interval for strategy profitability.

4. Research protocol for professional-level evidence

  1. Hypothesis: Specify instrument, timeframe, entry, exit, transaction-cost model and why the hypothesis might work. Include a falsifiable prediction.
  2. Data audit: Record provider, timezone, bid/ask availability, missing observations, rollover, holidays and survivorship or selection issues.
  3. Baseline: Compare with a no-trade benchmark and simple rules. Separate gross from net returns.
  4. Backtest: Use executable bid/ask, commissions, slippage, financing and realistic stop/gap handling. Do not use future bars to decide past trades.
  5. Validation: Freeze parameters before untouched out-of-sample testing. Use walk-forward analysis when appropriate and document all attempted variants to reduce data-snooping bias.
  6. Stress test: Increase costs, delay fills, remove favorable observations and test different market regimes. Report max drawdown, turnover and exposure.
  7. Forward test: Use demo or paper execution. Compare expected with actual fills and investigate discrepancies.
  8. Decision: Document why evidence supports, weakens or rejects the hypothesis. A profitable backtest is not enough.

প্রতিটি Strategy-তে Hypothesis, Data Quality, Transaction Cost, Out-of-Sample Test, Stress Test এবং Failure Conditions লিখবে। শুধু Backtest Profit দেখিয়ে Strategy সফল বলা যাবে না।

5. Fully solved calibration checks

EUR/USD long: 10,000 EUR, ask entry 1.08412, bid exit 1.08500, USD 2 commission

Gross = 10,000 × (1.08500 − 1.08412) = USD 8.80. Net = USD 6.80. The 8.8-pip executable move differs from a midpoint chart move.

EUR/USD short: 10,000 EUR, bid entry 1.08400, ask exit 1.08514, USD 2 commission

Gross = 10,000 × (1.08400 − 1.08514) = −USD 11.40. Net = −USD 13.40.

EUR/USD OHLC: O 1.0840, H 1.0860, L 1.0825, C 1.0850

Range 35 pips; body 10 pips; upper wick 10 pips; lower wick 15 pips. Check: 10 + 10 + 15 = 35 pips.

EUR/USD position sizing: USD 1,000 equity, 1% risk, 25-pip stop

Budget = USD 10. One EUR unit moves USD 0.0001 per pip, so estimated size = 10 ÷ (25 × 0.0001) = 4,000 EUR. This is a planning approximation, not a guarantee of maximum loss.

EUR/USD futures notional: 120,000 contracts, EUR 125,000 per contract

120,000 × 125,000 = EUR 15,000,000,000 gross contract notional. It cannot be directly equated with global spot turnover or unique risk transferred.
