Base Currency and Quote Currency

EUR/USD-এর প্রথম Currency কেন Base আর দ্বিতীয়টা Quote? Price বাড়লে কোন Currency Strong হয়, Buy বা Sell করলে আসলে কী Exposure নিচ্ছো, আর Rate উল্টালে Calculation কীভাবে Change হয়, সবকিছু Example দিয়ে বুঝবো।

One rule to remember

BASE / QUOTE = quote-currency units for 1 base-currency unit

একটা Currency Pair দেখলে প্রথম Currency-কে Base আর দ্বিতীয় Currency-কে Quote বলবে। Number-টা সবসময় বলে: 1 Base Currency কিনতে কত Quote Currency লাগে।

EURBASE = 1 unit
→
USDQUOTE = 1.1000 units
EUR/USD = 1.1000 → €1 = $1.10

All exchange rates in this lesson are invented for education, not live quotes.

01 / Why do we need two currencies?

একটা Apple-এর Price €2 বলতে পারো, কারণ Euro হলো Payment Currency। কিন্তু Euro নিজে কত Worth, সেটা বলতে আরেকটা Currency লাগবে। যেমন EUR/USD দিয়ে Dollar-এর তুলনায় Euro-এর Value Measure করি।

Think of a price tag
1 appleCosts €2
1 EURCosts $1.10

Just as a product price needs a currency, an exchange rate needs a second currency. The base is what is priced; the quote is the unit used to express that price.

02 / Reading pairs without guessing

Original example gallery
GBP/USD1 GBP = 1.2500 USD
USD/JPY1 USD = 150.00 JPY
EUR/GBP1 EUR = 0.8800 GBP
USD/CHF1 USD = 0.9000 CHF
AUD/USD1 AUD = 0.6500 USD
USD/BDT1 USD = 120.00 BDT

USD/JPY-তে USD First, তাই USD Base। GBP/USD-তে GBP First, তাই GBP Base। USD সবসময় Base হবে, এমন কোনো Rule নেই।

The USD/BDT figure is hypothetical and included to practice notation. Actual BDT market access, quotes and rules may differ.

03 / When the number rises, who is stronger?

এটাই Beginner-দের সবচেয়ে Common Confusion। EUR/USD 1.1000 → 1.1500 হলে 1 Euro কিনতে আগে $1.10 লাগতো, এখন $1.15 লাগে। তাই Euro Dollar-এর তুলনায় Strong হয়েছে।

Price moves up
1.1000Before: $1.10 per €1
→
1.1500After: $1.15 per €1
Pair ↑ → Base appreciates relative to Quote

একই সময়ে Dollar Euro-এর তুলনায় Weaker হয়েছে। কিন্তু Dollar পৃথিবীর সব Currency-এর বিপরীতে Weak হয়েছে, এমন Conclusion করা যাবে না।

Price moves down
1.1000Before
→
1.0500After
Pair ↓ → Base depreciates relative to Quote

EUR/USD কমলে Euro, Dollar-এর তুলনায় Weaker হয়। Dollar তুলনামূলক Stronger হয়।

04 / The tricky example: USD/JPY

USD/JPY 150.00 থেকে 155.00 হলে অনেকেই ভাবে Yen Strong হয়েছে, কারণ Number বেড়েছে। আসলে ঠিক উল্টো।

Visual logic
150 JPYNeeded for $1 before
155 JPYNeeded for $1 after

USD strengthened against JPY. One dollar now buys more yen. Equivalently, one yen buys fewer dollars.

USD/JPY ↑ → USD stronger, JPY weaker

Always Pair-এর First Currency Follow করবে। Base/Quote Direction বোঝার জন্য Country-এর নাম, Currency-এর Popularity বা Dollar-এর Position Guess করবে না।

05 / Buying or selling a currency pair

তুমি যখন EUR/USD Buy করো, তুমি Base EUR-এর Value Quote USD-এর তুলনায় বাড়বে বলে Position নিচ্ছো। EUR/USD Sell করলে EUR তুলনামূলক Weak হবে বলে Position নিচ্ছো।

BUY / LONG EUR/USD

Expect EUR ↑ vs USD

Economically long the base currency and short the quote currency.

SELL / SHORT EUR/USD

Expect EUR ↓ vs USD

Economically short the base currency and long the quote currency.

Professional nuance: In a deliverable spot transaction you exchange currencies. In a retail CFD you typically hold a derivative tracking the pair, not a deliverable currency balance. The exposure language is similar, but ownership and contractual obligations differ.

06 / Inverse exchange rates: turn the pair around

ধরো EUR/USD = 1.2500। তাহলে 1 EUR = $1.25। কিন্তু 1 USD কিনতে কত Euro লাগবে? Rate-এর Reciprocal নিতে হবে।

Worked example · reciprocal
USD/EUR = 1 ÷ (EUR/USD) = 1 ÷ 1.2500 = 0.8000
EUR/USD1 EUR = 1.25 USD
⇄
USD/EUR1 USD = 0.80 EUR

দুইটা Quote একই Relationship-এর Opposite Direction। Rate-এর Product 1 হয়, যদি Same Moment-এর Idealized Single Price ব্যবহার করো।

1.2500 × 0.8000 = 1

A real market has separate bid and ask prices, so you cannot invert a tradable bid and ask independently without switching sides. We'll cover that in Lesson 004.

07 / Direct and indirect quotation: depends on your home currency

Direct Quote আর Indirect Quote বুঝতে আগে জানতে হবে তুমি কোন Currency-কে Domestic Currency ধরছো। Germany-তে Domestic Currency EUR। USA-তে USD। তাই একই Pair এক Person-এর কাছে Direct, আরেকজনের কাছে Indirect হতে পারে।

Germany-based viewpoint · EUR is home currency
USD/EUREUR per 1 USD · direct quote
EUR/USDUSD per 1 EUR · indirect quote

For a euro-based observer, a direct quotation expresses the price of one unit of foreign currency in euros. An indirect quotation expresses the price of one euro in foreign currency.

Important terminology caveat: financial textbooks sometimes write units as USD/EUR to mean “USD per EUR”, rather than the retail platform pair label EUR/USD. Always check the author's notation before applying a formula.

08 / Bid and ask: why a screen can show two prices

Forex Platform-এ অনেক সময় EUR/USD-এর পাশে দুইটা Number দেখবে: Bid আর Ask। Dealer-এর Bid হলো যে Price-এ Dealer Base Currency কিনতে রাজি। Ask হলো যে Price-এ Dealer Base Currency Sell করতে রাজি।

Illustrative dealer quote
1.0998BID · dealer buys EUR
1.1000ASK · dealer sells EUR

Customer buys the base currency at the ask and sells it at the bid. The difference is the spread.

Spread = 1.1000 − 1.0998 = 0.0002 = 2 pips

যদি EUR/USD Buy করতে চাও, সাধারণভাবে Ask Price-এ Enter করবে। Sell করলে Bid Price-এ। Real Platforms-এ Fees, Slippage আর Contract Details-ও Matter করে।

For EUR/USD, 1 pip is conventionally 0.0001. Lesson 004 covers bid/ask in depth.

09 / Mini case study: exchange €500 into dollars

ধরো তুমি Hamburg থেকে New York যাচ্ছো। Currency Provider-এর EUR/USD Conversion Rate 1.0800। তুমি €500 Convert করলে কত USD পাবে?

Calculation
€500 × 1.0800 USD/EUR = $540

Now imagine the rate rises to 1.1200:

€500 × 1.1200 USD/EUR = $560

একই Euro দিয়ে $20 বেশি পাওয়া গেল, কারণ Euro Dollar-এর তুলনায় Stronger। কিন্তু Exchange Provider-এর Fees বা Offered Rate থাকলে Actual Amount কম বা আলাদা হতে পারে।

10 / Advanced intuition: relative, not absolute strength

EUR/USD Up মানে Euro Dollar-এর তুলনায় Strong। কিন্তু EUR/GBP Down হতে পারে একই সময়ে। কারণ Pound, Euro-এর চেয়েও বেশি Strength Gain করতে পারে।

Three-currency illustration

Suppose EUR/USD rises from 1.1000 to 1.1200 while GBP/USD rises from 1.2500 to 1.3000.

EUR/GBP: 1.1000 ÷ 1.2500 = 0.8800
EUR/GBP: 1.1200 ÷ 1.3000 ≈ 0.8615

EUR strengthened versus USD but weakened versus GBP. Both statements can be true at the same time.

Idealized synchronized mid-quotes; no spreads or fees included.

11 / Common beginner mistakes

  1. Thinking the second currency is the base. The first currency in the retail pair label is the base.
  2. Assuming a rising USD/JPY means yen strength. It means USD strength relative to JPY.
  3. Forgetting to invert. EUR/USD and USD/EUR are reciprocal representations of the same relative price at a single mid-rate.
  4. Mixing retail pair notation with units notation. USD/EUR can mean different things in different textbooks; read definitions.
  5. Confusing a CFD with actual currency delivery. Check the instrument's contract.
  6. Calling one currency universally strong. Strength is relative to another currency and a specific time interval.

12 / Practice lab

A. In AUD/USD = 0.6500, identify the base, quote and meaning.

Show answer

Base = AUD; quote = USD; 1 AUD costs 0.65 USD.

B. USD/JPY rises from 145 to 150. Which currency strengthened relative to the other?

Show answer

USD strengthened relative to JPY because 1 USD buys more yen.

C. EUR/USD = 1.2500. Calculate USD/EUR at the same idealized mid-rate.

Show answer

1 ÷ 1.2500 = 0.8000 EUR per USD.

D. GBP/USD = 1.3000. What is the idealized USD value of £250?

Show answer

£250 × 1.3000 = $325.

E. If you buy GBP/USD, which currency are you expecting to appreciate relative to the other?

Show answer

GBP, the base currency, relative to USD.

13 / Mastery check

Answers না দেখে Explain করার চেষ্টা করো। তারপর Open করে Check করো।

1. Why is the base currency always treated as one unit?

The pair expresses how many units of the quote currency are required for one unit of the base currency.

2. If EUR/GBP falls, what happened to EUR relative to GBP?

EUR depreciated relative to GBP.

3. Why is USD/EUR not simply the same number as EUR/USD?

They express opposite directions. With a single mid-rate, one is the reciprocal of the other.

4. What is the dealer's bid price?

The price at which the dealer is willing to buy the base currency, stated in quote currency units.

5. Can EUR strengthen against USD but weaken against GBP?

Yes. Currency strength is relative, and GBP can rise against USD by more than EUR does.
