Module 02 · Market Structure and Participants

Banks, Dealers, Liquidity Providers and Retail Brokers

01 · Who actually participates in forex?

Banks, dealers, non-bank liquidity providers, retail brokers, corporations, asset managers and central banks interact for different purposes. Not everyone trades to speculate.

Forex Market-এ শুধু Retail Trader নয়, অনেক Institution কাজ করে।

BanksCustomer FX and dealing
LPsQuotes and liquidity
BrokersRetail account access

02 · Commercial banks

Banks exchange currencies for customers, manage funding, hedge exposure and may quote two-way FX prices. Some operate large dealing desks.

Bank Client Transaction আর নিজস্ব Risk Manage করে।

03 · Investment banks and global dealers

Large dealers provide liquidity and risk intermediation to institutions, often across spot, forwards, swaps and options.

Global Dealer বিভিন্ন FX Product-এ Quote দিতে পারে।

04 · What a dealer does

A dealer quotes a bid to buy and an ask to sell. It may temporarily hold inventory risk before offsetting exposure.

Dealer-এর Bid হলো সে যে Price-এ Buy করবে, Ask হলো Sell করবে।

05 · Dealer inventory

After buying EUR from a customer, a dealer may hold extra EUR exposure. It can offset risk by trading elsewhere, adjusting quotes or using derivatives.

Inventory Risk থাকলে Dealer Quote Adjust করতে পারে।

06 · Bid-ask spread

A EUR/USD quote of 1.1000 bid and 1.1002 ask has a two-pip spread. This is one component of the transaction cost.

Spread = Ask − Bid।

07 · Liquidity providers

A liquidity provider supplies prices and potentially executable size to counterparties under contractual and market conditions.

Liquidity Provider Quote দিলেও সবসময় Fill Guaranteed নয়।

08 · Bank versus non-bank LP

Banks may combine client services with market making. Non-bank electronic firms can also provide liquidity using automated pricing and risk systems.

Non-bank LP-ও FX Market-এ Price Quote করে।

09 · Liquidity depth

A quoted price may be available only for a limited quantity. Larger orders can execute at several prices or be rejected, depending on the venue.

Top Price-এ Unlimited Volume Available নয়।

10 · Quote aggregation

An aggregator receives multiple provider quotes and applies routing and selection logic. The displayed best quote may have constraints on size or execution.

Aggregator Multiple LP-এর Quote Compare করে।

11 · Retail brokers

Retail brokers provide accounts, platforms, margin systems and trade execution or dealing arrangements. The exact legal entity and contract matter.

Retail Broker Platform Access দিলেও Execution Model আলাদা হতে পারে।

12 · Broker as principal

A broker acting as principal is the contractual counterparty. It may hedge the resulting exposure or keep some of it internally.

Broker Principal হলে Client Trade-এর Opposite Party হতে পারে।

13 · Broker as agent

An agent arranges execution for the client under its mandate. Agency status and obligations must be checked in the agreement.

Agent Broker-এর Role Contract-এ লেখা থাকে।

14 · Market maker model

Market makers quote prices and can internalize client flow. Conflicts may arise, but the model alone does not prove unfair execution.

Market Maker মানেই Scam নয়।

15 · STP and ECN labels

STP and ECN are common marketing descriptions. They do not establish exact routing, counterparty identity, spread or investor protection.

ECN/STP Label নয়, Execution Policy Verify করো।

16 · A-book and B-book

A-book broadly refers to externally hedged or routed exposure; B-book to internalized exposure. Many brokers use combinations rather than one pure model.

A-book/B-book একমাত্র Safety Test নয়।

17 · Prime brokerage

Prime brokers provide eligible institutions credit and operational access to multiple executing counterparties, subject to agreements.

Prime Broker Institutional Credit আর Access Support করে।

18 · Prime-of-prime

Prime-of-prime intermediaries may serve smaller brokers and institutions, but service and credit arrangements vary.

Prime-of-prime নাম দেখে Guaranteed Liquidity Assume করো না।

19 · Corporate FX demand

Importers and exporters exchange currencies to pay invoices and hedge future cash flows. They may prioritize certainty rather than speculation.

Importer Future Payment-এর জন্য FX Hedge করতে পারে।

20 · Asset managers

Global investors convert currencies for securities purchases and may hedge foreign-currency exposure.

Foreign Asset Investment-এ FX Transaction লাগে।

21 · Hedge funds

Hedge funds may trade FX for macro views, relative value or hedging. Strategies, leverage and risk differ widely.

Hedge Fund-এর Strategy Retail-এর মতো নাও হতে পারে।

22 · Central banks

Central banks implement monetary policy and may intervene in currency markets under specific mandates. Intervention does not guarantee a permanent price direction.

Central Bank Policy Currency Price Affect করতে পারে।

23 · Interdealer relationships

Dealers transact with one another using electronic venues, brokers and bilateral relationships. Credit limits affect available trading access.

Interbank Market একটি Single Website নয়।

24 · Credit and counterparty limits

An institution may refuse or limit trading with another if credit exposure exceeds its limits. Market access depends on more than visible prices.

Credit Risk Liquidity Access Reduce করতে পারে।

25 · Execution quality

Compare spreads, fill rates, latency, rejections, slippage and total costs. A low advertised spread is not sufficient evidence of good execution.

Execution Quality Measure করতে Actual Fill Data দরকার।

26 · Last look and rejections

Some venues permit a short window to accept or reject a trade request under disclosed rules. Check whether the provider uses last look.

Last Look থাকলে Quote দেখে Order Reject হতে পারে।

27 · Conflicts of interest

A broker may earn from spreads, commission or principal dealing. Check conflict disclosures and regulatory permissions.

Broker Revenue Model বুঝে Account Open করো।

28 · Counterparty and insolvency risk

In bilateral derivatives, the counterparty's failure can affect contractual claims. Client-money and compensation protections vary by product and jurisdiction.

Broker Regulated হলেও Insolvency Risk পুরো Zero নয়।

29 · Worked quote comparison

Provider A quotes EUR/USD 1.1000/1.1002, a 2-pip spread. Provider B quotes 1.0998/1.1003, a 5-pip spread. On 10,000 EUR, immediate spread-only cost is approximately $2 versus $5.

10,000 EUR-এ 1 Pip প্রায় $1, তাই Spread Cost Compare করা যায়।

Spread A = 1.1002 − 1.1000 = 2 pips ≈ $2 for 10,000 EUR
Spread B = 1.1003 − 1.0998 = 5 pips ≈ $5 for 10,000 EUR

30 · Worked dealer inventory example

A dealer sells EUR 100,000 at 1.1002 and immediately buys EUR 100,000 elsewhere at 1.1001. Simplified gross difference is $10 before fees, market impact and operational costs.

Dealer Profit সবসময় Full Spread নয়। Hedge Cost বাদ দিতে হয়।

(1.1002 − 1.1001) × 100,000 EUR = $10 gross

31 · Why brokers show different prices

Liquidity providers, account markups, quote latency, market hours and execution policies vary. A different quote is not automatically evidence of manipulation.

Different Broker-এর Chart সামান্য Different হতে পারে।

32 · How to verify a broker

Identify the exact legal entity, check it in the relevant regulator's official register, read execution and custody terms and review withdrawal rules.

Brand Logo নয়, Legal Entity Verify করো।

33 · Practical demo exercise

Collect simultaneous bid/ask quotes for EUR/USD from two demo platforms. Record UTC time, spread, account type and any differences. Repeat during normal and news hours without risking real funds.

Demo Account দিয়ে LP আর Broker Price Compare করো।

  1. Open two demo quote feeds.
  2. Record the exact UTC time and account type.
  3. Write each bid and ask.
  4. Calculate each spread in pips.
  5. Repeat on several days and compare.

34 · Common misconceptions

Retail orders do not always go straight to an interbank venue. ECN does not guarantee zero conflict. Liquidity is not infinite. A regulated broker cannot make trading risk-free.

Market Structure শেখা মানে Marketing Claim Critically Evaluate করা।

The price at which the dealer is willing to sell.

No.

No.

Credit intermediation and operational access for eligible institutions.

To manage exchange-rate exposure from payments or receipts.
