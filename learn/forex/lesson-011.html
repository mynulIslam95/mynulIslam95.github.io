01 · Why order types matter

Order type controls how you request execution, not whether a trade will be profitable.

Order Type দিয়ে Broker-কে কীভাবে Trade Execute করতে হবে তা বলো।

02 · Market order

A market order seeks immediate execution at available prices. The final fill can differ from the displayed quote, especially during volatility.

Market Order দ্রুত Execute হয়, কিন্তু Exact Price Guarantee নয়।

03 · Buy market example

EUR/USD shows bid 1.1000 and ask 1.1002. A market buy normally seeks the available ask, approximately 1.1002, subject to execution.

Buy করলে সাধারণত Ask Price Pay করতে হয়।

04 · Sell market example

With bid 1.1000 and ask 1.1002, a market sell normally seeks the available bid, approximately 1.1000.

Sell করলে সাধারণত Bid Price পাওয়া যায়।

05 · What is a limit order?

A limit order specifies a maximum acceptable purchase price or a minimum acceptable sale price. It may never fill if no executable price is available.

Limit Order Price Control করে, কিন্তু Fill Guarantee করে না।

06 · Buy limit order

EUR/USD trades near 1.1000/1.1002. A buy limit at 1.0950 seeks a purchase at 1.0950 or better if the relevant executable ask reaches the limit and liquidity is available.

Buy Limit সাধারণত Current Price-এর নিচে Place করা হয়।

07 · Sell limit order

EUR/USD trades near 1.1000/1.1002. A sell limit at 1.1050 seeks a sale at 1.1050 or better if the executable bid reaches the limit and liquidity is available.

Sell Limit সাধারণত Current Price-এর ওপরে Place করা হয়।

08 · Limit orders can miss trades

A price can touch your chart's midprice without the executable bid or ask reaching your limit. Queue priority, minimum size and liquidity can also prevent a fill.

Chart Touch করলেই Order Fill হবে না।

09 · What is a stop order?

A stop order waits for a trigger price. After triggering, a stop-market order generally becomes a market order and may fill at a worse price.

Stop Trigger হওয়ার পরে Market Order হতে পারে, তাই Slippage সম্ভব।

10 · Buy stop order

With EUR/USD around 1.1000, a buy stop at 1.1050 can be used to seek a long entry after an upward breakout. Trigger conventions vary by broker.

Buy Stop সাধারণত Current Price-এর ওপরে থাকে।

11 · Sell stop order

With EUR/USD around 1.1000, a sell stop at 1.0950 can seek a short entry after a downward breakout. The fill can be below the trigger.

Sell Stop সাধারণত Current Price-এর নিচে থাকে।

12 · Stop-limit order

A stop-limit has two prices: the stop trigger and the limit. After triggering, it submits a limit order instead of an unrestricted market order.

Stop-Limit Trigger হয়, তারপর Limit Price Condition অনুযায়ী Fill চায়।

13 · Buy stop-limit example

Set buy stop 1.1050 and buy limit 1.1055. After the trigger, execution is permitted only at 1.1055 or better. If price jumps to 1.1070 and never returns, the order can remain unfilled.

Stop-Limit Slippage Control করতে পারে, কিন্তু Entry Miss হতে পারে।

1.1050Stop trigger
1.1055Maximum buy price
1.1070Gap example: no eligible fill

14 · Sell stop-limit example

Set sell stop 1.0950 and sell limit 1.0945. After triggering, execution is permitted at 1.0945 or better. A rapid fall through the limit can leave the order unfilled.

Fast Market-এ Stop-Limit Fill না হওয়ার Risk আছে।

15 · Side-by-side comparison

Market: execution priority, price uncertain. Limit: price boundary, execution uncertain. Stop-market: trigger then market execution. Stop-limit: trigger then bounded-price limit execution.

চারটি Order Type-এর Priority আলাদা।

MarketExecutionPrice slippage
LimitPrice boundaryNo fill
Stop-marketTrigger then executionWorse fill
Stop-limitTrigger then price boundaryNo fill after trigger

16 · Order types and the bid-ask spread

Buy orders execute against ask liquidity and sells against bid liquidity under typical retail FX conventions. Charting platforms may display bid, ask or mid candles. Trigger rules are product-specific.

Trigger Price Check করার আগে Chart Bid না Ask দেখায় তা বুঝো।

17 · Slippage explained

Slippage is the difference between an expected or requested price and the actual execution price. It can be adverse or favorable depending on order type and market conditions.

Expected Price আর Actual Fill-এর Difference হলো Slippage।

18 · Gaps and weekend risk

If a stop-market trigger is crossed during a gap, the first executable price may be far away. A stop-limit may instead remain unfilled.

Weekend Gap-এ Stop-Loss Guaranteed Protection নয়।

19 · Partial fills and liquidity

Some venues can fill only part of an order at a price. Other broker platforms aggregate, reject or fill orders according to their execution policy.

Order-এর এক অংশ Fill আর বাকিটা Pending থাকতে পারে।

20 · Time in force

Good-till-cancelled, day, immediate-or-cancel and fill-or-kill instructions describe how long orders remain active or how partial execution is handled. Availability varies by venue.

Time-in-Force Order কতক্ষণ Active থাকবে তা Control করে।

21 · Pending order expiry

A pending order can remain open after your original market thesis expires. Use an expiration time where supported and review stale orders.

পুরোনো Pending Order ভুল সময়ে Trigger হতে পারে।

22 · Stop entry versus stop-loss

A buy stop may enter a new long after a breakout. A sell stop may exit an existing long as a stop-loss. The same order mechanism can serve different purposes.

Entry Stop আর Protective Stop একই Order Mechanism Use করতে পারে।

23 · Stop-limit is not a guaranteed stop-loss

A stop-limit exit can fail to execute during a fast move because its limit forbids worse fills. This may leave the position open and exposed to further losses.

Stop-Limit দিয়ে Guaranteed Loss Limit পাওয়া যায় না।

24 · Risk before entry

Choose the entry condition, maximum planned loss, invalidation level and position size. Account for spread, commissions, gap risk and correlated trades.

Entry নেওয়ার আগে Position Size এবং Risk Calculate করো।

25 · Broker-specific execution rules

Read the exact broker's order definitions, trigger side, minimum distance, expiration, guaranteed-stop availability and execution policy. CFD, spot and futures rules may differ.

সব Broker-এর Order Trigger Rules Same নয়।

26 · Demo exercise

In a demo account, record current bid/ask, place a buy limit below the market, a buy stop above it and a stop-limit with two prices. Observe trigger versus actual fill and cancellation behavior.

Demo-তে চারটি Order Type Practice করো।

27 · Mistakes to avoid

Do not assume a limit always fills, a stop always fills at its trigger, a stop-limit always exits or a chart's last price is the executable quote.

Fill Guarantee আর Price Guarantee এক জিনিস নয়।

1. Which order prioritizes immediate execution?

Market order, subject to liquidity and broker handling.

2. Can a limit order remain unfilled?

Yes.

3. Buy limit at 1.0950 when price is 1.1000: above or below?

Below.

4. Buy stop at 1.1050 when price is 1.1000: above or below?

Above.

5. What does a stop-market become after triggering?

Typically a market order.

6. Does stop-market guarantee the stop price?

No.

7. What two prices does a stop-limit use?

Stop trigger and limit price.

8. Buy stop 1.1050, limit 1.1055, price gaps to 1.1070: guaranteed fill?

No. It can remain unfilled.

9. Which side typically executes a market buy?

Ask.

10. Which side typically executes a market sell?

Bid.

11. What is slippage?

Difference between expected/requested and actual fill price.

12. Can a protective stop-limit leave a losing trade open?

Yes, if the limit price cannot be met.
