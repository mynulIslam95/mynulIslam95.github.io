Module 02 · Market Structure and Participants

Spread Widening and Volatile Market Conditions

Understand why spreads change, how news and thin liquidity affect execution and why stop-loss orders can behave unexpectedly. বাংলা ও English explanations with calculations, practical scenarios and a spreadsheet assignment.

Key idea: the midpoint can stay unchanged

01 · What is spread widening?

The bid-ask spread widens when the difference between the available selling and buying quotes increases. The midprice can remain unchanged while trading becomes more expensive.

Bid আর Ask-এর Gap বড় হলে Spread Widening হয়।

02 · Spread and volatility are different

Volatility describes variation in price. Spread describes the immediate difference between bid and ask. They often change together but are not interchangeable.

Volatility মানে Price Movement, Spread মানে Bid-Ask Difference।

03 · The normal-market quote

EUR/USD bid 1.1000 and ask 1.1002 gives a 2-pip spread. Buying 10,000 EUR and immediately selling at unchanged quotes costs about $2 before other fees.

Normal Market-এ Spread 2 Pip হলে 10k EUR-এর Immediate Cost প্রায় $2।

04 · A widened quote

EUR/USD bid 1.0997 and ask 1.1005 gives an 8-pip spread. The midpoint is still 1.1001, identical to the normal quote, yet immediate round-trip cost rises to about $8 for 10,000 EUR.

Midprice Same থাকলেও Spread 2 থেকে 8 Pip হতে পারে।

05 · Spread cost formula

For EUR/USD, approximate spread cost in USD equals spread in pips × 0.0001 × base-currency units, assuming unchanged quotes, full fills and no other costs.

Spread Cost = Pip × Pip Size × Units।

Spread cost ≈ (Ask − Bid) × EUR units

06 · Why dealers widen spreads

Dealers may quote wider spreads to compensate for inventory risk, fast price changes, uncertain valuations, hedging costs and adverse selection.

Dealer Risk বাড়লে Quote Wider হতে পারে।

07 · Adverse selection in fast markets

A liquidity provider risks trading against someone with newer information or a faster feed. Wider spreads or reduced size can limit this exposure.

Fast Trader-এর বিপরীতে Stale Quote দিলে Dealer Loss করতে পারে।

08 · Liquidity withdrawal

Providers may reduce executable size or temporarily stop quoting during uncertainty. A tight-looking quote for a tiny size may not help a larger order.

Thin Liquidity-তে Available Size কমে যায়।

09 · Scheduled news releases

Interest-rate decisions, inflation reports and employment data can trigger rapid repricing. Actual market response depends on surprises, positioning and context.

High-Impact News-এ Spread Wide আর Slippage বাড়তে পারে।

10 · Unscheduled shocks

Unexpected political, financial or geopolitical events can disrupt prices without advance warning. An economic calendar cannot predict every shock.

সব Volatility Economic Calendar-এ আগে দেখা যায় না।

11 · Session transitions and rollover

Some brokers show wider spreads around daily rollover or quiet trading hours. Exact timing varies by broker, instrument and daylight-saving rules.

Rollover Time Broker অনুযায়ী Different হতে পারে।

12 · Weekends and holidays

Reduced participation around closures and holidays may make quotes less competitive. Market reopening can include gaps.

Weekend Open-এ Price Gap Possible।

13 · Major versus less-traded pairs

Frequently traded pairs often have tighter spreads in normal conditions, while less-traded pairs may be wider. Both can widen sharply under stress.

Major Pair-ও Crisis Time-এ Wide Spread দেখাতে পারে।

14 · Fixed versus variable spread

A variable spread changes with conditions. A broker advertising a fixed spread may use other costs, restrictions or execution policies. Read the actual terms.

Fixed Spread Label দেখেই Total Cost কম Assume করো না।

15 · Commission and markup

An account may quote a low spread but charge commission. Another may add a spread markup. Compare total execution cost, not one headline number.

Zero Spread মানে Zero Trading Cost নয়।

16 · Midprice movement versus spread movement

Bid and ask can move together, apart or asymmetrically. A chart based only on bid prices may not show the full cost of entering or exiting.

Bid-only Chart দেখে Ask Trigger Miss বুঝতে পারো না।

17 · Buy and sell exposure

A market buy usually executes against ask liquidity and a market sell against bid liquidity. Widening changes the price at which each side can transact.

Buy-তে Ask, Sell-এ Bid Important।

18 · Open-position mark-to-market

A long opened at the ask is commonly valued for exit using the bid; a short opened at the bid is valued using the ask. Spread widening can worsen displayed floating P&L even without a midpoint change.

Midprice না নড়লেও Floating Loss বাড়তে পারে।

19 · Long-position example

Buy 10,000 EUR at 1.1002. With unchanged normal bid 1.1000, floating P&L is −$2. If bid drops to 1.0997 while ask rises to 1.1005, floating P&L becomes −$5. This is mark-to-market, not a realized loss unless closed.

Spread Wide হলে Long Position-এর Bid Exit Worse হতে পারে।

(1.0997 − 1.1002) × 10,000 = −$5

20 · Short-position example

Sell 10,000 EUR at 1.1000. With normal ask 1.1002, floating P&L is −$2. If ask rises to 1.1005, floating P&L becomes −$5 even though the midpoint is unchanged.

Short Position Ask দিয়ে Close হয়।

(1.1000 − 1.1005) × 10,000 = −$5

21 · Stop-loss trigger conventions

Many retail FX platforms chart bid prices. A buy-side stop for a short may trigger on ask, while a sell-side stop for a long may trigger on bid. Confirm the broker's instrument rules.

Short Stop Ask দিয়ে Trigger হতে পারে, Chart Bid দেখাতে পারে।

22 · Spread-triggered stop example

A short entry at 1.1000 has a buy stop at 1.1004. If the ask rises from 1.1002 to 1.1005 while midpoint stays near 1.1001, the stop can trigger even without a rising midpoint. Execution may be worse.

Spread Widening-এ Stop Trigger হতে পারে।

23 · Stops are not guaranteed prices

A stop typically becomes an order after its trigger. A gap or thin depth can produce execution beyond the intended stop price.

Stop Price আর Actual Fill Same Guaranteed নয়।

24 · Slippage versus spread widening

Spread widening changes the quoted bid-ask gap. Slippage compares actual fill with a specified reference. The two effects can overlap and must not be double-counted.

Spread আর Slippage একই Cost দুবার Count করো না।

25 · Volatility clustering

Large price changes often occur near other large changes, but this statistical tendency does not predict the direction of the next move.

High Volatility পরেও High Volatility আসতে পারে, Direction নিশ্চিত নয়।

26 · ATR is not a spread measure

Average True Range estimates historical price range, not transaction spread. A high ATR does not specify the actual bid-ask cost.

ATR দিয়ে Direct Spread Calculate করা যায় না।

27 · News candle limitations

A one-minute candle may hide the spread, quote cancellations and intraminute slippage. Historical OHLC alone may understate execution risk.

Candle Backtest-এ Real Spread Missing থাকতে পারে।

28 · Variable spread backtesting

Use timestamped bid/ask data when possible, plus realistic commissions, latency assumptions and slippage scenarios. A constant 1-pip assumption can be misleading.

Backtest-এ Fixed Spread Always Assume করো না।

29 · Risk budget under stress

A 20-pip planned loss plus 6 pips of adverse execution would be 26 pips before fees. At 10,000 EUR/USD units, that is about $26 rather than $20.

Planned Stop Loss আর Actual Loss Different হতে পারে।

(20 + 6) pips × $1/pip = $26

30 · Position sizing when spreads vary

A wide spread consumes more of the planned risk distance immediately. Size should reflect potential total loss and broker rules, not merely the stop's chart distance.

Spread Risk Budget-এর Part।

31 · Spread-to-stop ratio

If spread is 4 pips and planned stop distance is 10 pips, the spread equals 40% of that distance. This is a warning metric, not a universal trade/no-trade threshold.

Spread/Stop Ratio বড় হলে Cost Relative অনেক।

4 pips ÷ 10 pips × 100 = 40%

32 · Trade frequency and costs

Repeated entries and exits multiply costs. A small theoretical strategy edge can disappear after spreads, commissions and slippage.

Overtrading Cost Compound করে।

33 · Execution during flash events

Rare sharp repricings can cause severe gaps, rejected orders or extreme slippage. Stress tests should include scenarios worse than recent average conditions.

Rare Market Shock-এ Normal Rule Fail করতে পারে।

34 · Broker quote comparisons

Compare quotes from the same timestamp, account type and size. One broker's wider quote does not by itself prove misconduct.

Different Time-এর Screenshot Compare করলে Wrong Conclusion হতে পারে।

35 · Build a spread-monitor spreadsheet

Record UTC time, bid, ask, spread in pips, midpoint, session and news flag. Chart spread against time and compare distributions.

Spreadsheet-এ Spread Monitor তৈরি করো।

36 · Demo scenario: calm versus news

Collect demo quotes during a quiet period and around a scheduled release. Record the highest spread and available size. Avoid placing real-money orders.

Demo Quote Data দিয়ে Calm vs News Compare করো।

37 · Scenario: unchanged midpoint

Normal quote 1.1000/1.1002 and stress quote 1.0997/1.1005 both have midpoint 1.1001. The spread rises from 2 to 8 pips, so 10,000 EUR immediate spread cost increases from $2 to $8.

Midpoint Same হলেও Cost চারগুণ হতে পারে।

38 · What to put in a trading journal

Record expected spread, actual bid/ask, fill prices, size, commission, slippage, stop trigger side and economic context. Include skipped trades.

Trade না করলেও Spread Widening Observation Journal-এ রাখো।

39 · Common mistakes

Do not assume spreads stay fixed, treat a candle's close as an executable quote, infer manipulation from one stop-out or confuse volatility with direction.

Execution Data ছাড়া Broker Accuse করো না।

40 · Mastery project

Build a 30-row hypothetical quote dataset with calm, news and rollover regimes. Compute spread, midpoint, average and maximum spread, then explain how a 10k-EUR position's costs change.

30-row Data দিয়ে Spreadsheet Assignment Complete করো।

  1. Create 30 hypothetical timestamped bid/ask observations.
  2. Calculate spread = (ask − bid)/0.0001 and midpoint = (ask + bid)/2.
  3. Compare mean, median and maximum spread across calm, news and rollover scenarios.
  4. Calculate immediate spread-only cost for 10,000 EUR.
  5. Write three ways the simplified model differs from live execution.
