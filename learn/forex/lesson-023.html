Slippage and Execution Latency

Understand why orders fill at different prices, how network and broker delays affect execution and how to measure the true costs. Detailed বাংলা ও English explanations with worked examples and a demo study.

38 teaching sections 18 mastery questions Execution journal lab

Study objectives and readiness

What you should be able to do

  1. Distinguish latency, slippage and bid-ask spread.
  2. Calculate signed slippage in pips and dollars for buy and sell orders.
  3. Explain why stop and stop-limit orders behave differently during gaps.
  4. Build a demo execution-quality journal and interpret its limitations.

01 · What slippage actually means

Slippage is the difference between a reference or expected execution price and the actual fill. Specify which reference price you use, otherwise comparisons can mislead.

Slippage হলো Expected Price আর Actual Fill-এর Difference। Reference Price স্পষ্ট করতে হবে।

02 · Why execution is not instantaneous

An order travels through the app, network, broker controls, routing and venue or dealer execution before confirmation returns. Each step may take time.

Order Click আর Fill-এর মাঝে অনেক Processing Step থাকে।

03 · Latency versus slippage

Latency measures time delay; slippage measures price difference. Longer delay can increase exposure to price changes, but neither implies the other in every trade.

Latency Time Difference, Slippage Price Difference।

04 · The execution timeline

Record quote observed at T0, order submitted at T1, broker received at T2, execution at T3 and confirmation at T4. Different timestamps answer different questions.

Quote Time আর Fill Time আলাদা করে Log করো।

05 · Milliseconds matter differently

A 100 ms delay may be inconsequential in a quiet market but material during a rapid news release. There is no universal safe latency threshold.

একই Latency সব Market Condition-এ Same Risk নয়।

06 · Positive and negative slippage

A buy filled below its reference ask is favorable; above is unfavorable. For a sell, a higher fill is favorable and a lower fill is unfavorable.

Buy আর Sell-এর Slippage Direction উল্টো।

07 · Signed slippage convention

For a buy, adverse slippage in pips is (fill − reference ask) / pip size. For a sell, adverse slippage is (reference bid − fill) / pip size. Negative results mean improvement.

Adverse Slippage Positive, Favorable Slippage Negative হিসেবে Define করা যায়।

Buy adverse pips = (fill − reference ask) ÷ pip size
Sell adverse pips = (reference bid − fill) ÷ pip size

08 · Worked buy example

You see EUR/USD ask 1.1002 and your buy fills at 1.1005. Adverse slippage is 3 pips. For 10,000 EUR units, price difference is $3 before fees.

Buy Fill 1.1005 হলে 1.1002-এর তুলনায় 3 Pip Worse।

(1.1005 − 1.1002) ÷ 0.0001 = 3 pips; 3 × $1 = $3

09 · Worked sell example

You see EUR/USD bid 1.1000 and your sell fills at 1.0996. Adverse slippage is 4 pips. For 20,000 EUR units, price difference is $8 before fees.

Sell Fill 1.0996 হলে 4 Pip Worse।

(1.1000 − 1.0996) ÷ 0.0001 = 4 pips; 4 × $2 = $8

10 · Favorable fill example

A EUR/USD buy expected at ask 1.1002 fills at 1.1000. This is 2 pips favorable, worth $2 in price difference for 10,000 EUR units.

Positive Slippage মানে Better Execution-ও হতে পারে।

11 · Spread is not slippage

Spread is the gap between bid and ask at a given moment. Slippage is the difference between reference and fill. Both may affect total execution cost.

Spread আর Slippage Double Count করো না।

12 · Quote movement versus broker delay

A price can change legitimately between display and execution. Poor connectivity, broker processing and market volatility may all contribute; a bad fill alone does not prove misconduct.

Bad Fill দেখেই Broker Fraud Conclude করা যায় না।

13 · Order-book sweep

If a large market order exceeds available top-level quantity, it may fill across multiple price levels. Its weighted average differs from the first displayed quote.

Large Order Multiple Price Level Consume করে।

14 · Weighted average fill

A 100,000-EUR buy fills 40,000 at 1.1002 and 60,000 at 1.1005. The average is 1.10038, 1.8 pips above the first ask, or $18 difference.

Weighted Average Fill দিয়ে Actual Cost বের করো।

(40,000 × 1.1002 + 60,000 × 1.1005) ÷ 100,000 = 1.10038

15 · Partial fills and timestamps

Several fills can occur at different times and prices. Analyze each fill and the aggregate weighted price, not only the order submission time.

Partial Fill-এর আলাদা Price আর Timestamp থাকতে পারে।

16 · Market order execution

A market order seeks prompt execution at available prices. It generally offers no fixed maximum buy or minimum sell price.

Market Order Price Guarantee করে না।

17 · Marketable limit protection

A marketable buy limit can restrict the highest allowed price, but if the market moves above the limit, part or all of the order may remain unfilled.

Limit Price Protection-এর বিনিময়ে Non-fill Risk থাকে।

18 · Stop order slippage

A stop is commonly a trigger for a market-style order, not a guaranteed execution price. Gaps can cause fills well beyond the stop level.

Stop Loss Level Maximum Loss Guarantee নয়।

19 · Stop-limit non-fill risk

A stop-limit may prevent execution beyond the limit but can leave an exposed position open during a fast market.

Stop-limit Slippage Restrict করলেও Position Open থাকতে পারে।

20 · Weekend and session gaps

Prices may jump between trading sessions or around closures. A stop set before a gap may execute at the next available price.

Weekend Gap-এ Stop Price Miss হতে পারে।

21 · Economic releases

High-impact data can cause sudden repricing, thinner displayed liquidity, wider spreads and order rejections. A directionally correct view can still yield a poor fill.

News Time-এ Execution Risk বাড়ে।

22 · Low-liquidity hours

Rollover periods and holidays may reduce available counterparties. The effect depends on pair, broker and trading venue.

Thin Session-এ Quote Quality বদলায়।

23 · Network and device delay

Wi-Fi instability, mobile connectivity and app responsiveness can add delay. Test platform reliability on demo rather than assuming a faster device guarantees good fills.

Internet Speed Improve করলেও Market Slippage Zero হবে না।

24 · Broker-side processing

Risk checks, account margin validation, internal dealing and external routing can contribute to processing time. Policies vary by broker and product.

Broker Execution Policy Read করো।

25 · Requotes and rejections

Some execution models may reject or requote orders. If a request times out, check order status before resubmitting to avoid duplicate exposure.

Timeout হলে Duplicate Click করো না।

26 · Last look

Some institutional FX providers can reject a trade request within a disclosed acceptance window. The exact practice depends on agreements and venue standards.

Last Look থাকলে Quote Fill না-ও হতে পারে।

27 · Price improvement fairness

Evaluate both favorable and unfavorable slippage, not only the worst fills. A broker's claim of price improvement needs comparable execution data.

Positive আর Negative Slippage দুটোই Track করো।

28 · Measuring execution quality

Record reference quote, UTC timestamp, order type, side, size, actual fills, commissions and spread. Use a consistent benchmark and adequate sample.

Execution Quality Data দিয়ে Measure করো।

29 · Median versus average

A few extreme fills can distort average slippage. Report median, mean, worst-case observations and distribution by market condition.

শুধু Average নয়, Outlier-ও দেখো।

30 · Slippage rate

For a sample, count favorable, zero and unfavorable fills, but also compare their magnitudes. A high favorable count does not ensure low total cost.

Fill Count আর Total Dollar Cost আলাদা।

31 · Effective trading cost

For a buy, compare fill with a contemporaneous midprice if reliable. Effective half-spread is (buy fill − mid)/pip size; sell uses (mid − sell fill). Do not add the same spread again.

Cost Calculation-এ Same Spread Twice Add করো না।

32 · Position sizing and slippage

A larger position multiplies the dollar effect of each pip of slippage. For 100,000 EUR/USD units, 1 pip is about $10.

Position Size বড় হলে 1 Pip-এর Dollar Impact বাড়ে।

33 · Risk-budget stress test

If planned stop distance is 20 pips and an additional 5 pips of adverse execution occurs, the realized loss distance can be 25 pips before fees. Size for uncertainty rather than treating 20 as a guarantee.

Planned Risk আর Realized Loss আলাদা হতে পারে।

34 · Execution versus strategy edge

A strategy with a small theoretical edge can become unprofitable after spread, slippage, commissions and financing. Backtests must model realistic fills.

Backtest Profit Real Trading Profit Guarantee নয়।

35 · Demo experiment

Record 20 demo executions across quiet and active sessions. Compute signed slippage, average, median, worst fill and estimated dollar cost by size.

২০টি Demo Fill-এর Data Analyze করো।

  1. Use a demo account and no real funds.
  2. Record the quote and UTC timestamp before each order.
  3. Capture actual fills and any partial fills.
  4. Compute signed pips and dollar differences.
  5. Compare quiet and volatile periods without claiming causation from a small sample.

36 · Spreadsheet formulas

Use columns side, reference, fill, units, pip size and adverse pips. Buy adverse pips=(fill-reference)/pip; sell=(reference-fill)/pip. Dollar effect for EUR/USD=adverse pips×0.0001×EUR units.

Spreadsheet-এ Buy/Sell Formula আলাদা রাখো।

37 · Interpreting results

Small demo orders may execute differently from live orders. Demo statistics are training evidence, not proof of real-money execution quality.

Demo Result Live Performance Guarantee নয়।

38 · Common mistakes

Do not confuse spread with slippage, assume all fills are malicious, treat stop-loss as a guaranteed exit, ignore partial fills or compare quotes from different timestamps.

Execution Journal ছাড়া Broker Compare করা কঠিন।
