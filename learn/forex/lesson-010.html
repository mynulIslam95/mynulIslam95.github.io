Core concept

LONG ↑Potential gain when pair rises
SHORT ↓Potential gain when pair falls

Both directions can lose money. All results are before applicable costs unless otherwise stated.

01 · Long and short: the core difference

Long EUR/USD means buying EUR and selling USD. Short EUR/USD means selling EUR and buying USD. Long generally benefits when the pair rises, short when it falls, before costs.

EUR/USD Long মানে EUR Buy আর USD Sell। Short মানে EUR Sell আর USD Buy।

02 · Base and quote currency

The first currency is the base. The second is the quote. EUR/USD 1.1000 means one euro costs 1.1000 US dollars.

Pair-এর প্রথম Currency Base, দ্বিতীয়টি Quote।

03 · Visual direction map

Long: buy base → pair rises → potential gain. Short: sell base → pair falls → potential gain. Neither direction guarantees a profit.

Direction দেখে Trade করো, শুধু Green/Red Candle দেখে নয়।

LONG ↑Buy base, sell quote
SHORT ↓Sell base, buy quote

04 · EUR/USD long example

Buy 10,000 EUR at ask 1.1002, sell at bid 1.1050. Gross P&L = (1.1050 − 1.1002) × 10,000 = +$48.

Entry Ask আর Exit Bid ব্যবহার করতে হবে।

(1.1050 − 1.1002) × 10,000 = +$48

05 · EUR/USD short example

Sell 10,000 EUR at bid 1.1000, cover at ask 1.0955. Gross P&L = (1.1000 − 1.0955) × 10,000 = +$45.

Short-এর Entry Bid, Exit Ask।

(1.1000 − 1.0955) × 10,000 = +$45

06 · Losing long example

Buy 10,000 EUR at 1.1002 and sell at 1.0940. Gross P&L = −$62. A long loses when the executable exit price falls below its entry.

Price উল্টো গেলে Long Loss হয়।

07 · Losing short example

Sell 10,000 EUR at 1.1000 and buy back at 1.1065. Gross P&L = −$65. A short loses when its buy-back price rises.

Short-এ Price বাড়লে Loss হয়।

08 · Bid, ask and spread

Retail long positions typically open at ask and close at bid. Retail shorts typically open at bid and close at ask. The spread creates a trading cost even when the midpoint does not move.

Spread বাদ দিলে Calculation ভুল হতে পারে।

09 · Position size and pip value

For EUR/USD, 1 pip = 0.0001. With 10,000 EUR units, one pip is $1 in quote currency. With 100,000 EUR units, one pip is $10.

Lot Size বাড়লে Pip Value আর Risk বাড়ে।

10 · P&L formulas

Long gross quote P&L = (exit bid − entry ask) × base units. Short gross quote P&L = (entry bid − exit ask) × base units. Net result also accounts for commissions, financing and other charges.

Formula মুখস্থ নয়, Example দিয়ে Practice করো।

Long = (exit bid − entry ask) × units
Short = (entry bid − exit ask) × units

11 · USD strength across different pairs

A stronger USD relative to EUR tends to push EUR/USD down. A stronger USD relative to JPY tends to push USD/JPY up. Always read which currency is first.

USD Strong মানেই সব Pair Buy নয়।

12 · Directional view versus actual profit

You may correctly predict an upward move and still lose because your entry, exit, spread, timing or position size was poor.

Prediction Correct হলেই Profit Guaranteed না।

13 · Leverage does not change pip value for fixed units

A 10,000-unit EUR/USD position has the same directional dollar sensitivity whether the broker requires $100 or $500 margin, excluding any differences in trading costs.

Margin আর Position Size এক জিনিস না।

14 · Spot, CFD and futures differences

Deliverable spot FX involves currency settlement. Retail CFDs typically settle price differences without delivering currency. Futures are standardized exchange-traded contracts. Shorting, settlement and margin rules differ.

সব Forex Platform একই Product Offer করে না।

15 · Hedging and netting accounts

In a hedging account, opposing positions may coexist. In a netting account, an opposite order may reduce or reverse the existing exposure. Two opposing trades are not risk-free because of spreads, financing and execution.

একই Pair Long-Short নিলেই Risk Zero হয় না।

16 · Overnight financing and swaps

A position held overnight can incur a financing charge or credit. Direction, interest-rate differentials, broker markups and settlement calendars matter. A positive swap is not guaranteed profit.

Overnight Hold করার আগে Swap Check করো।

17 · Short-selling risk

A short can face large losses if the pair rises sharply. Liquidation rules and any negative-balance protection depend on the actual product, legal entity and jurisdiction.

Short-এ Unlimited Profit বা Guaranteed Safety নেই।

18 · Risk plan before entry

Define entry, invalidation, stop distance, position size, expected costs and exit. Check combined currency exposure and free margin before opening another trade.

Trade নেওয়ার আগে Risk Budget ঠিক করো।

19 · Flat market still has costs

A long and a short can each lose money in a sideways market because spreads, commissions and financing can exceed the gross directional gain.

Market Sideways হলেও Cost থাকে।

20 · Practice with a currency conversion

A EUR/USD trade produces USD quote-currency P&L. If your account is in EUR, the platform converts the USD P&L using its conversion methodology and possibly fees.

Account Currency আলাদা হলে Conversion লাগবে।

21 · Mistakes to avoid

Do not confuse buying USD with buying EUR/USD, calculate using midpoint prices, use excessive leverage or treat a stop-loss as guaranteed execution.

Beginner Mistake এড়াতে Entry/Exit Price লিখে রাখো।

22 · Demo trading workflow

Open a demo terminal, record bid and ask, simulate a 10,000-unit long, calculate P&L at a new bid, close it and compare your calculation with the terminal. Repeat for short.

Demo Account-এ Calculation Verify করো।

Practice questions

1. EUR/USD rises. Which direction benefits before costs?

Long.

2. EUR/USD long buys which currency?

EUR, the base currency.

3. USD/JPY rises. Which currency strengthened relative to the other?

USD relative to JPY.

4. Buy 10,000 EUR at 1.1010, sell at 1.1035. Gross result?

+$25.

5. Sell 20,000 EUR at 1.1000, cover at 1.1020. Gross result?

−$40.

6. At which price does a retail long typically open?

Ask.

7. At which price does a retail short typically close?

Ask.

8. What is the pip value for 10,000 EUR/USD units?

$1 per pip in USD quote currency.

9. Can a flat market still cost money?

Yes, spreads, commissions and financing.

10. Does a stop-loss guarantee a maximum loss?

No. Gaps and slippage can cause worse fills.
