Bullish and Bearish Candles

Understand what a green or red Forex candle actually proves, what it cannot prove and how to turn candle observations into testable research rather than unsupported predictions.

Bullish ও Bearish Candle-এর আসল অর্থ, Bid/Ask-এর পার্থক্য, Timeframe, Calculation এবং Scientific Backtesting শিখবে। শুধু Green Candle দেখে Buy কিংবা Red Candle দেখে Sell করবে না।

Learning objectives

What mastery looks like

  1. Define bullish, bearish and neutral candles precisely for a specified price feed and timeframe.
  2. Calculate body, upper wick, lower wick and range with correct pip units.
  3. Explain why candle direction is descriptive, not a prediction or an order-flow census.
  4. Distinguish bid, ask and midpoint candle prices from executable trades.
  5. Build a falsifiable candle-based hypothesis and evaluate it with realistic costs.

শেষে তুমি Formula মুখস্থ নয়, নিজে হিসাব ও Research Design ব্যাখ্যা করতে পারবে।

1. Exact definitions

Bullish: C > O · Bearish: C < O · Neutral: C = O

O is the first observed price of the bar, C the last, H the maximum and L the minimum. The comparison applies only to the particular feed (bid, ask, midpoint or traded price) and candle interval. A bullish candle is not the same as a bullish trend.

Open-এর চেয়ে Close বেশি হলে Bullish। Close কম হলে Bearish। সমান হলে Neutral। কিন্তু এর মানে পরের Candle-ও একই দিকে যাবে এমন নয়।

2. Anatomy and mathematical constraints

Body = |C − O|
Upper wick = H − max(O,C)
Lower wick = min(O,C) − L
Range = H − L

A valid candle requires H ≥ max(O,C) and L ≤ min(O,C). Body + upper wick + lower wick = range. For most EUR/USD quotes, 1 pip = 0.0001 USD per EUR. A zero-range candle makes body/range undefined, so do not divide by zero.

Fully solved bullish example

EUR/USD O=1.0800, H=1.0830, L=1.0790, C=1.0820. Body=(1.0820−1.0800)/0.0001 = 20 pips. Upper wick=(1.0830−1.0820)/0.0001 = 10 pips. Lower wick=(1.0800−1.0790)/0.0001 = 10 pips. Range=(1.0830−1.0790)/0.0001 = 40 pips. Check: 20+10+10=40.

Body + Upper Wick + Lower Wick সবসময় Full Range-এর সমান হবে। না হলে Calculation বা OHLC Data ভুল।

Fully solved bearish example

EUR/USD O=1.0850, H=1.0860, L=1.0810, C=1.0820. Body 30 pips, upper wick 10 pips, lower wick 10 pips and range 50 pips. Check: 30+10+10=50.

4. What candle color does not tell you

ObservationPermitted conclusionUnsupported leap
Green candleClose exceeds openBuyers outnumbered sellers
Red candleClose is below openNext candle must fall
Long lower wickPrice visited a lower level and closed above itA bank definitely hunted stops
Large bodyLarge open-to-close movement for that barA permanent trend has begun
Several green barsSeveral intervals closed above their opensA risk-free continuation strategy exists

Every executed transaction has a buyer and seller. Candle direction reflects changes in observed price, not a headcount of buyers versus sellers. Dealer inventory, liquidity, order timing and news can affect prices in ways a single bar cannot identify.

Green Candle মানে Buyers-এর সংখ্যা Sellers-এর চেয়ে বেশি, এই ধারণা ভুল। Candle-এর Color থেকে Institution-এর Intent নিশ্চিত জানা যায় না।

5. Bid, ask and midpoint candles

Retail FX chart feeds may use bid, ask or midpoint. A long position enters at the accessible ask and exits at the accessible bid. A short position enters at bid and exits at ask. Thus a bullish midpoint candle can coincide with a losing long trade after spread and commission.

Execution example

Suppose a midpoint candle opens at 1.10000 and closes at 1.10010: bullish by 1 pip. At the entry moment the ask is 1.10008, and at the exit moment the bid is 1.10002. Buying EUR 10,000 at 1.10008 and selling at 1.10002 produces −USD 0.60 gross. A USD 2 round-trip commission makes net P&L −USD 2.60. These are illustrative quotes, not live market data.

Chart Green হলেও Ask-এ Buy এবং Bid-এ Sell করার পর Loss হতে পারে। Spread ও Fee বাদ দিয়ে Net Result হিসাব করো।

Quotes may be asynchronous, rejected or unavailable at the requested size. Bid/ask observations should come from a consistent timestamped source when researching execution.

6. Timeframes, session boundaries and aggregation

One 1-hour bar can be bearish while some of its constituent 5-minute bars are bullish. This is not a contradiction. Different opens, closes and UTC aggregation boundaries create different candles. A broker's server timezone or daylight-saving convention can change daily bar shapes.

Example: A 60-minute interval opens at 1.1020 and closes at 1.1000, so the hourly bar is bearish. Inside it, a 5-minute interval opens at 1.0990 and closes at 1.1000, so that 5-minute bar is bullish. Both statements can be true.

5-Minute Bullish Candle এবং 1-Hour Bearish Candle একই সময়ে থাকতে পারে। Timeframe বদলালে Open ও Close বদলায়।

7. How to test a bullish-candle hypothesis scientifically

Example hypothesis, not a recommended strategy

Claim to test: After a bullish EUR/USD 15-minute candle whose body exceeds 60% of its range, the next 15-minute candle's executable return is positive more often than a specified baseline after trading costs.

  1. Define the feed, timestamps, spread convention, entry/exit prices and handling of zero-range bars.
  2. Define a baseline before seeing the results. Do not select a threshold after inspecting performance without accounting for multiple testing.
  3. Calculate signal only after the bar closes, then enter at the next available ask for a long and exit at the chosen future bid.
  4. Include commissions, financing where relevant, slippage, gaps, rejected fills and realistic size assumptions.
  5. Split chronological data into development and untouched out-of-sample periods. Prevent look-ahead leakage.
  6. Report sample size, average net return, drawdown, uncertainty intervals, sensitivity to costs and regime dependence.
  7. Test alternate feeds and time boundaries, then conduct forward paper testing before considering any real-money risk.

Signal Candle Close হওয়ার পরই জানা যাবে। Future Data আগে ব্যবহার করলে Backtest ভুয়া ভালো দেখাতে পারে। Out-of-Sample Test ছাড়া Edge দাবি করবে না।

8. Professional research assignment

Deliverable: candle-research notebook

  1. Create 100 synthetic valid OHLC bars and assert H ≥ max(O,C), L ≤ min(O,C) and wick + body identity.
  2. Label bullish, bearish and neutral candles. Show counts and their dependence on timeframe.
  3. Construct a second synthetic bid/ask feed with variable spreads and compare candle signals with executable P&L.
  4. Write a testable hypothesis, baseline, cost assumptions and a chronological validation plan.
  5. Produce a one-page failure report listing five reasons why a positive sample result may not persist.

Grading rubric: 25 points data validity, 25 correct math and currency units, 20 execution realism, 20 research validity, 10 honest interpretation. Pass ≥85/100 and zero critical look-ahead errors.

9. Mastery examination · 20 questions

Attempt each question before opening its answer. Passing the quiz is not evidence of a profitable trading edge.

Q1. What makes a candle bullish?

Close > open for the selected price series and timeframe. It does not guarantee the next candle rises.

Q2. What makes a candle bearish?

Close < open. It describes the completed interval, not an inevitable continuation.

Q3. What if close equals open?

A neutral or doji-like candle by body definition. The full range can still be large.

Q4. Does a bullish candle imply buyers outnumbered sellers?

No. Every executed trade has both sides. The candle reflects prices, not the count of buyers and sellers.

Q5. Can a bullish candle have a large upper wick?

Yes. High may be well above close even when close exceeds open.

Q6. Can a bearish candle have a long lower wick?

Yes. A lower wick describes low relative to open/close, not a guaranteed reversal.

Q7. Calculate the body for O 1.0800, C 1.0825.

25 pips for EUR/USD; bullish.

Q8. Calculate the body for O 1.0840, C 1.0810.

30 pips; bearish.

Q9. For O 1.0800 H 1.0830 L 1.0790 C 1.0820, calculate wicks.

Upper 10 pips, lower 10 pips, body 20 pips, total range 40 pips.

Q10. Can a bullish candle still lose money for a long trade?

Yes. A long entered at the ask can lose after costs even if the midpoint candle closes above its open.

Q11. Why does a candle color depend on timeframe?

Different aggregation boundaries change opens and closes. A 5-minute green candle can occur within a bearish hourly candle.

Q12. Why do UTC boundaries matter?

A shift in session or timezone boundaries changes which ticks belong to each candle.

Q13. Can two brokers show different candle colors?

Yes. Feeds, bid/ask/mid basis, aggregation and timestamp rules may differ.

Q14. How is a bullish candle different from a bullish trend?

One interval has C>O; a trend needs a defined multi-interval method and may not exist.

Q15. What is the maximum possible body relative to range?

100% when O and C coincide with opposite extrema H and L.

Q16. What is the minimum body relative to range?

0% when O=C. If H=L, the body/range ratio is undefined, not zero by division.

Q17. Does a green candle prove an institutional accumulation event?

No. Candle shape alone does not identify who traded or their intent.

Q18. What is a false continuation inference?

Assuming the next candle will be bullish merely because the current candle is bullish.

Q19. What must a strategy backtest specify?

Instrument, feed, timezone, signal timing, executable bid/ask, costs, exits, slippage and out-of-sample validation.

Q20. What would count as evidence for a candle-based edge?

A preregistered rule tested on suitable historical and untouched out-of-sample data with realistic costs and uncertainty, then forward-tested.

Professional worked case and oral defense

O=1.0840, H=1.0860, L=1.0825, C=1.0850 gives a bullish body of 10 pips, upper wick 10 pips, lower wick 15 pips and range 35 pips. The identity 10+10+15=35 checks arithmetic, not future price direction.

Formula ঠিক হলে Candle-এর Anatomy বোঝা যায়, কিন্তু Next Candle Predict করা যায় না।

Defend this example

Change C to 1.0830. The candle becomes bearish: body 10 pips, upper wick 20 pips, lower wick 5 pips, range 35 pips. Check 10+20+5=35.

Evidence and primary-source context

OHLC is a summary of observations, not a record of every transaction or the intrabar order. The FX Global Code provides professional conduct and execution context. Exchange-traded futures contract data cannot be assumed equivalent to decentralized OTC spot quotes.

  • Global Foreign Exchange Committee: FX Global Code and market conduct resources
  • BIS: FX turnover survey methodology and market structure
  • CME Group: exchange-traded FX contract information

These sources provide institutional context. They do not validate any specific candle-based trading strategy.

Risk and scope

Forex, leveraged derivatives and CFDs can produce rapid losses. A candle is not a trading recommendation. Stops may slip, spreads may widen and historical performance does not guarantee future returns. Use simulated or paper trading for coursework.
