The big idea

Every forex price compares two currencies.

Currency Pair মানে দুইটা Currency-এর Relative Value। Major, Minor আর Exotic হলো Market-এ প্রচলিত Classification। এগুলো Currency Pair-এর Nature বুঝতে Help করে, কিন্তু কোনো Pair Automatically Safe বা Profitable হয় না।

01 / First, learn the language of pairs

ধরো EUR/USD = 1.1000। EUR হলো Base Currency, USD হলো Quote Currency। 1 EUR কিনতে 1.10 USD লাগে। এই Concept না বুঝলে Major/Minor/Exotic Classification মুখস্থ করেও লাভ নেই।

02 / Major pairs: USD + another major currency

Retail Forex Education-এ সাধারণত Major Pair বলতে US Dollar-এর সঙ্গে Euro, Yen, Pound, Swiss Franc, Canadian Dollar, Australian Dollar বা New Zealand Dollar-এর Pair বোঝায়।

Seven traditional retail majors
EUR/USDEuro / Dollar
USD/JPYDollar / Yen
GBP/USDPound / Dollar
USD/CHFDollar / Swiss franc
USD/CADDollar / Canadian dollar
AUD/USDAustralian dollar / Dollar
NZD/USDNew Zealand dollar / Dollar

Some teaching materials use a six-pair list and place NZD/USD elsewhere. “Major” is a convention, not a globally binding regulatory definition.

Why are these commonly grouped together?

এই Pair-গুলোতে সাধারণত Strong Institutional Activity থাকে। Active Market Hours-এ Bid-Ask Spread অনেক সময় তুলনামূলক Tight হয়। কিন্তু Major মানেই Low Volatility বা Guaranteed Profit না। News-এর সময় Spread Expand করতে পারে।

03 / Minor pairs: major currencies without USD

Minor Pair-কে অনেক সময় Cross Currency Pair বলা হয়। এখানে USD নেই, কিন্তু সাধারণত অন্য বড় Currency-গুলো থাকে।

Common examples
EUR/GBPEuro / Pound
EUR/JPYEuro / Yen
GBP/JPYPound / Yen
EUR/CHFEuro / Swiss franc
AUD/JPYAustralian dollar / Yen
EUR/AUDEuro / Australian dollar

USD Pair-এর নামের মধ্যে নেই মানে Dollar-এর Global Influence একদম নেই, এমন না। USD Movement, Global Risk Sentiment আর Interest Rate Expectations Cross Pair-গুলোকেও Indirectly Affect করতে পারে।

04 / Exotic pairs: a major currency with a less commonly traded currency

Exotic Pair-এ সাধারণত একটি Widely Traded Currency-এর সঙ্গে Emerging Market বা Less Liquid Currency থাকে। উদাহরণ USD/TRY, USD/ZAR আর USD/MXN। তবে Classification সব Broker বা Textbook-এ একরকম নয়।

Illustrative exotic pairs
USD/TRYUS dollar / Turkish lira
USD/ZARUS dollar / South African rand
USD/MXNUS dollar / Mexican peso
EUR/PLNEuro / Polish zloty
USD/HUFDollar / Hungarian forint
USD/THBDollar / Thai baht

“Exotic” is a market label, not a judgment about a country or currency. Availability and trading conditions vary by venue.

Exotic Pair-এ Spread, Slippage, Financing Cost, Liquidity Gap আর Policy-Related Price Movement বেশি গুরুত্বপূর্ণ হতে পারে। কিছু Emerging Market Pair-এর Institutional Turnover অনেক বড়, তাই “Exotic = always illiquid” বলা ভুল।

07 / Worked example: calculate a cross pair

ধরো তোমার কাছে দুইটা Exchange Rate আছে: EUR/USD = 1.1000 এবং GBP/USD = 1.2500। এখন EUR/GBP কত হবে?

Original diagram · Cross-rate math
EUR1 EUR = 1.10 USD
USDCommon quote currency
GBP1 GBP = 1.25 USD

To express one euro in pounds, divide the dollar value of one euro by the dollar value of one pound:

EUR/GBP = (EUR/USD) ÷ (GBP/USD) = 1.1000 ÷ 1.2500 = 0.8800

অর্থাৎ 1 EUR = 0.88 GBP। এখানে USD Final Pair-এ নেই, কিন্তু Cross Rate Calculate করার জন্য Intermediate Currency হিসেবে Use করেছি।

Idealized arithmetic with simultaneous mid-rates. Real executable bid/ask cross-rates differ because spreads and timing matter.

What if the second quote is USD/JPY?

EUR/USD = 1.1000 আর USD/JPY = 150.00 হলে, EUR/JPY পেতে Multiply করবে।

EUR/JPY = 1.1000 × 150.00 = 165.00

The units cancel: (USD/EUR) × (JPY/USD) = JPY/EUR.

08 / Why trading costs matter more than the label

একটা Pair-এর Spread বেশি হলে Trade Open করার মুহূর্ত থেকেই Cost বেশি হতে পারে। Same Direction Correct হলেও Cost তোমার Result কমাতে পারে।

Illustrative cost comparison · not broker quotes
EUR/USDIllustrative spread: 0.8 pip
EUR/GBPIllustrative spread: 1.6 pips

If a hypothetical trade earns 5 pips before the spread, the simplified outcomes are:

5 − 0.8 = 4.2 pips vs 5 − 1.6 = 3.4 pips

This is a simplified illustration. The actual result depends on the entry/exit convention, spread changes, commissions, pip value, slippage and financing. A pip in different pairs may have different monetary value.

Professional Trader শুধু Pair-এর Name দেখে Trade Select করে না। Actual Spread, Average Volatility, News Calendar, Position Size আর Broker Execution Check করে।

09 / Beginner pair selection checklist

  1. Identify both currencies. Pair-এর Base আর Quote ঠিকমতো বুঝো।
  2. Know the category. Major, Minor না Exotic, আর Classification-এর Limitation কী?
  3. Check the session. তোমার Trading Time-এ Pair Active থাকে কি?
  4. Inspect live bid/ask. Broker-এর Real Spread কত?
  5. Review the calendar. Central Bank Decision, Inflation বা Employment Release আছে কি?
  6. Check contract specifications. Pip size, margin, swaps, minimum lot এবং execution terms.
  7. Start with demo observation. Real Money Risk না নিয়ে Spread আর Price Behaviour Track করো।

10 / Common myths

Myth 1: Every USD pair is a major. Reality: USD/TRY is commonly categorized as exotic.

Myth 2: Every cross pair has low liquidity. Reality: Liquidity differs significantly by pair and venue.

Myth 3: Exotic means low turnover everywhere. Reality: Institutional volumes for some emerging-market pairs are very large.

Myth 4: A low spread guarantees a good trade. Reality: Price risk and execution quality still matter.

11 / Practice lab

A. Categorize EUR/USD, GBP/JPY and USD/MXN.

Show answer

EUR/USD = major; GBP/JPY = minor/cross; USD/MXN = commonly classified as exotic.

B. Given EUR/USD = 1.0800 and GBP/USD = 1.2000, calculate EUR/GBP.

Show calculation

1.0800 ÷ 1.2000 = 0.9000 GBP per EUR.

C. Given GBP/USD = 1.3000 and USD/JPY = 150.00, calculate GBP/JPY.

Show calculation

1.3000 × 150.00 = 195.00 JPY per GBP.

D. Which is safer, EUR/USD or USD/TRY?

Show professional answer

Neither is inherently safe. EUR/USD often has better retail liquidity and tighter spreads, but risk depends on exposure, leverage, news, execution and position sizing. USD/TRY may introduce additional market and financing risks.

12 / Mastery check

নিজের ভাষায় Answer দিতে পারলে তবেই Complete Mark করো।

1. What makes EUR/USD a traditional major pair?

It contains USD paired with a major widely traded currency, EUR.

2. Why is EUR/GBP a cross?

It combines two currencies without USD appearing in the quoted pair.

3. Is USD/CNY necessarily a tiny market because some retail lists call it exotic?

No. The BIS 2025 survey reported 8.1% of global OTC FX turnover in USD/CNY, though retail access and market structure differ.

4. How do you derive EUR/GBP from EUR/USD and GBP/USD?

Divide EUR/USD by GBP/USD, assuming compatible simultaneous mid-rates.

5. What should you check before choosing a pair to trade?

Liquidity and session, live spread, volatility, economic news, contract specifications, execution quality and risk limits.
