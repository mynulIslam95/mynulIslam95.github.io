Module 01 · Forex Trading Foundations

Swap, Rollover, Commissions and Financing Costs

Understand what trading really costs, how overnight charges work and how to calculate net profit. বাংলা ও English explanations with realistic EUR/USD examples.

01 · The four cost categories

The main costs are bid-ask spread, explicit commission, overnight swap/financing and conversion or other account charges. The broker's actual schedule governs.

Spread, Commission, Swap আর Conversion Charge একসঙ্গে হিসাব করতে হবে।

Trade costsSpread + commission + slippage
Holding costsSwap + financing + other fees

02 · Spread: the first trading cost

With EUR/USD bid 1.1000 and ask 1.1002, buying and immediately selling 10,000 EUR creates roughly a $2 loss from the 2-pip spread, before other charges.

Price না নড়লেও Spread-এর জন্য Loss হতে পারে।

03 · Raw-spread versus spread-only accounts

A broker may advertise a low raw spread but charge commission separately, or include more of its compensation in a wider spread. Compare total round-trip costs.

শুধু Zero Spread দেখে Broker Choose করো না।

04 · Commission per side and round trip

A $3 commission per 100,000 units per side means a 100,000-unit opening trade costs $3 and its closing trade another $3. Round trip = $6.

Per Side আর Round Trip Commission আলাদা।

05 · Commission worked example

For 20,000 EUR/USD units, at $3 per 100,000 units per side: opening commission $0.60 and closing commission $0.60. Total = $1.20.

Lot Size অনুযায়ী Commission Scale হতে পারে।

06 · Swap and rollover

Retail rolling spot or FX CFDs may apply daily financing or swap adjustments to positions held beyond the broker's rollover cutoff. Exact product and timing matter.

Overnight Position Hold করলে Swap Credit বা Charge হতে পারে।

07 · Why overnight rates differ

Financing depends on the currency pair, direction, interest-rate environment, broker markup, contract structure and settlement conventions. Do not assume the central-bank rate difference equals your swap.

Central Bank Rate Difference মানেই Exact Broker Swap নয়।

08 · Long swap versus short swap

Long and short swap rates can differ and both may be negative after markups or other costs. Always inspect instrument specifications.

Long Positive Swap হলে Short Automatically Negative হবে, এমন Fixed Rule নেই।

09 · Swap credit is not free money

Even if a position earns a swap credit, unfavorable price moves, spreads and financing changes can more than offset it.

Positive Swap থাকলেই Guaranteed Profit নয়।

10 · Triple-swap and settlement calendar

Some brokers post an approximately three-day rollover adjustment on a designated weekday to reflect weekend settlement, often Wednesday for many spot FX pairs. Holidays and instruments change the schedule.

Triple Swap সব Pair-এ একই Day হয় না।

11 · Rollover cutoff and server time

A broker may use a rollover cutoff around 5 p.m. New York time for certain retail FX products, but actual cutoffs, DST adjustments and server timestamps vary.

Server Time আর Local Time মিলিয়ে Rollover Check করো।

12 · Spot FX value dates

Conventional spot FX typically settles T+2 business days for many pairs, with exceptions such as USD/CAD often T+1. Holidays and currency calendars affect value dates.

Settlement Date Pair আর Holiday অনুযায়ী Change হয়।

13 · CFD financing is product-specific

A rolling FX CFD may use broker-defined overnight adjustments that differ from deliverable spot FX or exchange-traded currency futures. Never apply one product's formula universally.

CFD আর Futures-এর Financing Calculation এক নয়।

14 · Swap-free accounts

Accounts marketed as swap-free may substitute administration fees, holding-period charges or other terms. Verify the actual written fee schedule.

Swap-Free মানে Cost-Free নয়।

15 · Currency conversion fees

If the account currency differs from the P&L currency, the broker converts profit or loss, possibly using a marked-up rate or explicit conversion charge.

USD Profit EUR Account-এ Convert করলে Fee হতে পারে।

16 · Deposit and withdrawal charges

Payment providers, banks or brokers may charge deposit, withdrawal or FX conversion fees. These are not always visible in trade-level P&L.

Withdrawal Fee-ও Total Trading Cost-এর অংশ।

17 · Inactivity and platform fees

Some brokers charge inactivity, market-data, subscription or platform fees. These costs matter particularly for low-volume accounts.

কম Trade করলে Inactivity Fee Check করো।

18 · Slippage as an implicit execution cost

A market order filled 0.5 pip worse than the expected price creates an additional effective cost. Favorable slippage can also occur, subject to execution rules.

Slippage Fixed Commission নয়, কিন্তু Result-এ Impact করে।

19 · Spread widening at news

During high-impact events, spreads can widen substantially. A strategy profitable at normal spreads may fail when real execution costs increase.

News Time-এ Backtest-এর Fixed Spread Unrealistic হতে পারে।

20 · Total trade P&L formula

Net P&L = gross price-movement P&L − spread not already reflected in fill prices − commissions ± swap − other applicable costs. Avoid double-counting spread when gross P&L already uses actual bid/ask fills.

Bid/Ask Fill দিয়ে P&L করলে Spread আবার Minus করো না।

Net = Gross price P&L − commission ± swap − other costs

Gross price P&L using actual executable bid/ask fills already includes the spread.

21 · Full worked example

Buy 10,000 EUR/USD at ask 1.1002, sell at bid 1.1050: gross $48. If total commission $0.60 and overnight swap −$1.20, net = $46.20, before other fees.

Gross Profit আর Net Profit আলাদা।

+$48Gross
−$1.80Commission + swap
+$46.20Net

22 · Loss example with fees

A position has gross price P&L −$30, commission $2 and financing −$3. Net P&L = −$35.

Loss-এর ওপর Fee Add হলে Total Loss বাড়ে।

23 · Break-even distance

A 10,000 EUR/USD trade with $2 round-trip commission needs approximately 2 additional pips of favorable price movement to cover that commission, beyond the spread already embedded in executable fills.

Cost Cover না করলে Price Right হলেও Net Profit Zero হতে পারে।

24 · Scalping versus swing trading costs

Scalpers may pay spread and commissions many times per day. Swing traders may pay fewer transaction costs but accumulate financing across nights. Compare strategies after costs.

Scalping-এ Transaction Cost, Swing-এ Overnight Cost গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।

25 · Carry trade basics

A carry trade seeks to benefit from relative funding yields while taking exchange-rate risk. FX price losses can overwhelm financing income.

Carry Trade Interest Income-এর পাশাপাশি Currency Risk নেয়।

26 · Interest-rate announcements

Central-bank decisions can change expected rates and FX prices. A higher policy rate does not guarantee a positive swap or appreciation.

Rate Hike মানেই Swap Profit বা Price Rise নয়।

27 · Cost-aware backtesting

Model bid/ask spreads, commissions, financing, slippage, trading times and realistic position sizes. Stress-test costs under volatile conditions.

Backtest-এ Realistic Cost না দিলে Result Misleading হবে।

28 · Compare broker fee schedules

Check account type, average spread at your trading hours, commission unit, rollover schedule, long/short swap, currency conversion and withdrawal terms.

Broker Comparison-এ Total Cost Table বানাও।

29 · Trading journal fields

Record gross P&L, entry/exit bid/ask, spread, commission, swap, slippage, conversion charges and net P&L. Compare cost as a fraction of planned risk.

Journal-এ Net P&L আলাদা Column রাখো।

30 · Demo exercise

Record a hypothetical 10,000-unit EUR/USD trade's executable entry and exit prices, commissions and nightly swap. Calculate net result and compare with the broker's statement.

Demo Trade Statement দিয়ে Calculation Verify করো।

31 · Common misconceptions

Zero commission does not mean zero cost. Swap-free does not mean fee-free. Positive carry does not eliminate price risk. Advertised spreads are not always available during news.

Marketing Claim নয়, Written Contract আর Real Fill Check করো।

7. Do both long and short swaps have to have opposite signs?

No. Both may be negative depending on rates, broker charges and product.

8. Is swap-free necessarily cost-free?

No. Alternative fees may apply.

9. Should spread be deducted again after P&L uses actual entry ask and exit bid?

No, that would double-count the spread.

10. Does a higher policy rate guarantee positive carry?

No.

11. What costs matter especially to high-frequency scalping?

Repeated spread, commissions and execution slippage.

12. What should a realistic backtest include?

Spread, commissions, financing, slippage and applicable conversion costs.
