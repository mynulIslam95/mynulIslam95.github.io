Broker Models, Regulation and Counterparty Risk

01 · Broker versus trading platform

A broker is the legal firm that offers the trading account and executes or arranges transactions. MetaTrader and TradingView are software, not proof of broker authorization.

Platform ভালো দেখালেই Broker নিরাপদ হয় না।

02 · What counterparty risk means

Counterparty risk is the chance that the firm owing you money or performance fails to meet its obligations. In OTC FX and CFDs, this can include the broker or other contractual counterparty.

Broker বা Counterparty Obligation পূরণ না করলে Loss হতে পারে।

03 · OTC forex market structure

Much spot FX is over-the-counter. Transactions occur through banks, dealers, liquidity providers and intermediaries rather than one universal centralized exchange.

Forex-এর বড় অংশ OTC Market।

04 · Market maker and dealing desk

A broker may quote prices and act as principal to the client's trade. This can create conflicts of interest, but principal execution is not automatically fraudulent.

Market Maker হলেই Scam নয়, Conflict Disclosure দেখতে হবে।

05 · Agency or STP claims

Straight-through processing describes routing arrangements, but the label alone does not prove every client trade is passed to an external provider or that the broker has no conflicts.

STP লেখা থাকলেই সব Order External Market-এ যায় না।

06 · ECN terminology

Electronic communication networks connect participants electronically. Retail advertising that says ECN can be imprecise; verify the actual legal entity, execution model and costs.

ECN Marketing Claim-এর চেয়ে Contract Important।

07 · A-book and B-book

A-book often describes hedging or passing exposure to outside counterparties; B-book often describes internalizing client risk. Real firms can use hybrid risk management.

A-book/B-book Label দিয়ে Safety Guarantee হয় না।

08 · Execution-only and advisory services

Execution-only brokers process orders without personalized investment advice. Some firms offer advice or managed accounts, which may require different permissions.

Execution Service আর Investment Advice আলাদা।

09 · Principal versus agent

A principal transacts as the counterparty, while an agent arranges transactions on a client's behalf. Read the client agreement to identify the legal relationship.

Trade-এর Opposite Side-এ কে আছে বুঝতে Agreement পড়ো।

10 · Best execution and slippage

Applicable best-execution rules require firms to consider execution quality, but do not guarantee the best conceivable price or no slippage. Policies differ by jurisdiction and product.

Best Execution মানে Exact Price Guarantee নয়।

11 · Conflicts of interest

A broker that profits from spreads, dealing or client losses may have conflicts. Look for written conflict policies, transparent pricing and complaint channels.

Conflict of Interest Disclosure Check করো।

12 · Spread, commission and financing transparency

Compare actual bid-ask spreads, commissions, overnight rates, conversion charges and withdrawal terms. A zero-commission claim does not mean zero trading cost.

Total Cost Compare করো।

13 · Regulation: the legal entity matters

Large brands can operate multiple subsidiaries under different regulators. The protections applicable to your account depend on the specific contracting entity and product.

Brand Name নয়, Legal Entity আর License Verify করো।

14 · Germany: BaFin and European rules

For Germany, consult BaFin's company database and investor warnings. EU/EEA investment firms may provide services through passporting, subject to applicable rules and supervision.

Germany-তে BaFin Database দিয়ে Firm Verify করো।

15 · ESMA retail CFD measures

EU retail CFD rules include leverage limits and other protections for covered retail clients, with implementation and details depending on national law and the product. These rules do not eliminate risk.

EU Retail CFD Protection থাকলেও Trading Risk থাকে।

16 · UK: FCA register

For UK-facing accounts, use the FCA's official register and warning list. Confirm the exact firm's permissions, status and contact details.

FCA Logo Screenshot-এর বদলে Official Register Check করো।

17 · US: CFTC and NFA

In the United States, retail forex dealers and relevant intermediaries are subject to specific CFTC/NFA frameworks. Check NFA BASIC and official CFTC resources.

USA-তে NFA BASIC দিয়ে Firm Registration Check করা যায়।

18 · Regulatory status is not insurance

A regulated firm can still fail, and rules vary by entity and product. Licensing is a screening step, not a guarantee of capital safety.

Regulated মানেই Risk-Free নয়।

19 · Client money segregation

Some regimes require segregation of client money from a firm's own money. Segregation arrangements and recovery in insolvency depend on the applicable rules and actual handling.

Segregated Funds মানেই Instant Refund Guarantee নয়।

20 · Deposit protection versus investment compensation

Bank deposit insurance and investor compensation are different schemes with different eligibility and limits. Trading losses generally are not covered as insured deposits.

Bank Deposit Insurance আর Trading Account Protection এক নয়।

21 · Negative balance protection

Some retail CFD regimes require negative balance protection at account level. Scope, exclusions and eligibility depend on the legal entity and product.

Negative Balance Protection থাকলেও Deposit হারাতে পারো।

22 · Leverage limits and client classification

Retail leverage limits can differ from professional-client terms. Reclassification may reduce some protections and is not a shortcut to safer trading.

Professional Account-এ Protection কম হতে পারে।

23 · Margin close-out rules

Margin close-out can trigger forced liquidation. Actual execution prices may be worse than expected during gaps and illiquid conditions.

Stop-Out Rule বুঝে Leverage ব্যবহার করো।

24 · Offshore entities and cross-border risk

An offshore subsidiary may have different supervision, dispute procedures and protections from a local regulated entity, even under the same brand.

Offshore Entity-তে Legal Protection আলাদা হতে পারে।

25 · Clone firms and fake licenses

Fraudsters may copy genuine company names, logos, registration numbers and websites. Verify the domain and contact information against the regulator's official listing.

License Number Copy করেও Scam করা যায়।

26 · Withdrawal red flags

Unexplained advance tax payments, demands for extra deposits to release funds, pressure to use crypto transfers and blocked withdrawals are warning signs. Legitimate fees must be documented.

Profit Withdraw করতে আগে আরও Deposit চাইলে সতর্ক হও।

27 · Identity and account ownership

Open accounts using your own truthful identity and residence details. Using someone else's documents or false location can breach terms and create legal and withdrawal problems.

অন্যের NID বা VPN দিয়ে Residency Hide করা Avoid করো।

28 · Security and payment safety

Use official domains, strong unique passwords and MFA. Never share one-time codes, remote-access control or seed phrases with a broker representative.

OTP বা Remote Access কাউকে দিও না।

29 · Broker comparison checklist

Check legal entity, regulator, permitted services, product, execution policy, custody terms, spread, commission, swap, leverage, complaints and withdrawal policy.

Broker Select করার আগে Written Checklist Follow করো।

  1. Identify the exact legal company name.
  2. Find the regulator independently, not through a broker-supplied link.
  3. Match company registration, permissions and website.
  4. Read execution, client-money and withdrawal terms.
  5. Compare total trading costs and account protections.
  6. Save findings before considering any account opening.

30 · Practical verification exercise

Pick a hypothetical broker. Find its contracting entity in the client agreement, search the official regulator database, compare addresses and permissions, then review fees and withdrawal terms. Do not deposit money.

Demo Research দিয়ে Broker Verification Practice করো।

31 · Mastery and risk mindset

A regulated broker does not make an unprofitable strategy profitable. Keep broker risk separate from market risk, leverage risk and operational risk.

Broker Safe হলেও Strategy Loss করতে পারে।
