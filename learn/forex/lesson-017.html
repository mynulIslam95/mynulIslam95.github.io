01 · What OTC means

Over-the-counter (OTC) trading happens through networks of dealers, banks and other counterparties rather than a single central spot-forex exchange. A quoted price can differ by venue.

OTC মানে একটাই Central Exchange নয়। বিভিন্ন Dealer-এর Quote আলাদা হতে পারে।

02 · Spot forex versus exchange-traded futures

Spot FX is generally bilateral and decentralized. Currency futures are standardized contracts traded on exchanges with centralized matching and clearing arrangements.

Spot Forex আর Currency Futures-এর Market Structure আলাদা।

03 · Why the market is global

Currency conversion supports trade, investment, hedging, funding and speculation across time zones. Global banks and regional institutions connect through electronic and voice channels.

Forex শুধু Retail Trader-এর Market নয়।

04 · Core market participants

Commercial banks, non-bank liquidity providers, asset managers, corporations, central banks, hedge funds and retail intermediaries have different reasons for trading.

সব Participant Profit Speculation করার জন্য Trade করে না।

05 · Banks as dealers

A dealer can quote a bid at which it buys and an ask at which it sells. The dealer may manage inventory risk by offsetting exposures elsewhere.

Dealer Bid আর Ask Quote করে এবং নিজের Risk Manage করে।

06 · Bid and ask across dealers

Dealer A might show EUR/USD 1.1000/1.1002 while Dealer B shows 1.0999/1.1003. Different prices do not automatically imply manipulation.

Broker A আর Broker B-এর Spread আলাদা হতে পারে।

Dealer ABid 1.1000 / Ask 1.1002
Dealer BBid 1.0999 / Ask 1.1003

07 · Interdealer market

Banks and professional dealers trade with one another through interdealer brokers, electronic venues and direct relationships. This is not a single universally accessible price feed.

Interbank Price একটাই Universal Number নয়।

08 · Dealer-to-client market

Corporations, funds and other clients may request quotes from dealers or trade through electronic platforms. Client relationships and credit terms affect access.

Institutional Client-এর Execution Retail-এর মতো নাও হতে পারে।

09 · Prime brokerage

Prime brokers may provide credit intermediation and settlement services to eligible institutions, allowing access to multiple liquidity providers under agreed arrangements.

Prime Brokerage Institutional Access আর Credit Support দিতে পারে।

10 · Prime of prime

Prime-of-prime firms may intermediate access for smaller financial institutions or brokers. The marketing label alone does not establish execution quality or legal protection.

Prime of Prime লেখা থাকলেই Risk-Free নয়।

11 · Retail broker connection

Retail clients typically trade through a broker or dealer. The broker may internalize trades, hedge them or route orders according to its execution policy.

Retail Order সবসময় Direct Interbank Market-এ যায় না।

12 · Market maker versus agent

A market maker may be principal to a transaction; an agent arranges execution for the client. The contract determines the legal relationship, not a platform logo.

Principal আর Agent আলাদা Role।

13 · Internalization

A broker can match or warehouse client flow internally rather than immediately offset every trade. This creates potential conflicts that require scrutiny and disclosure.

Internalization মানেই Fraud নয়, কিন্তু Conflict Check করতে হবে।

14 · Liquidity providers

Liquidity providers quote executable prices under specific conditions. Their quotes may change, disappear or be rejected depending on the venue and relationship.

Liquidity Provider-এর Quote সবসময় Fill Guarantee করে না।

15 · Aggregators

An aggregator can combine quotes from multiple sources and select or display prices using configured rules. The best displayed quote may not be available for the desired size.

Aggregator একাধিক LP-এর Price Combine করতে পারে।

16 · Request for quote

In a request-for-quote workflow, a client asks for a price and the dealer responds. Quote validity and acceptance conditions matter.

RFQ-তে Price Request করে Dealer-এর Response পাওয়া যায়।

17 · Streaming prices

Streaming quotes update continuously while liquidity is available. They may be indicative or executable subject to size, timing and platform terms.

Streaming Quote আর Guaranteed Execution একই নয়।

18 · Last look

Some liquidity providers use a short acceptance window to assess a trade request under disclosed conditions. Last-look practices are debated and subject to venue policies and applicable standards.

Last Look থাকলে Requested Price-এ Trade Reject হতে পারে।

19 · Firm versus indicative liquidity

A firm quote is intended to be executable under its stated terms, while an indicative quote is informational. Both require attention to size, validity and market conditions.

Indicative Price দেখে Guaranteed Fill Assume করো না।

20 · Credit relationships

Professional FX access often depends on bilateral credit limits and counterparty agreements. Market access can tighten when credit conditions deteriorate.

Credit Limit কমলে Trading Access বদলাতে পারে।

21 · Settlement and value dates

Many conventional spot pairs settle on T+2 business days, while some have different conventions. Holidays and currency calendars alter settlement dates.

Settlement Calendar Pair অনুযায়ী আলাদা হতে পারে।

22 · CLS and settlement risk

Payment-versus-payment arrangements such as CLS can reduce certain FX settlement risks for eligible currencies and participants. They do not eliminate market or counterparty risk.

Settlement Risk আর Price Risk এক নয়।

23 · Price discovery

Prices emerge from participants updating bids and asks in response to news, order flow, funding conditions and inventory needs. No single spot quote is the universal official market price.

Price Discovery অনেক Participant-এর Quote আর Trade থেকে হয়।

24 · Order flow

Order flow refers to buying and selling activity and requests. Professional venues may observe subsets of flow; retail traders usually cannot see all global spot-FX activity.

Retail Trader পুরো Global Order Flow দেখতে পায় না।

25 · Fragmented liquidity

Liquidity is spread across multiple dealers, platforms and bilateral relationships. Fragmentation can produce different spreads and execution outcomes at the same moment.

Liquidity Fragmentation-এর জন্য Broker Price Different হতে পারে।

26 · Why spot FX has no universal volume

Unlike a centralized exchange, decentralized spot FX does not have one complete public trade tape. Broker tick volume is a proxy for quote changes, not total global traded volume.

MT4 Tick Volume Global Forex Volume নয়।

27 · Futures volume as a separate data source

Exchange-traded currency futures report venue-specific traded volume. This can inform research but is not identical to all spot FX flow.

CME Futures Volume Spot Forex-এর Complete Volume নয়।

28 · Spread and market impact

Trading costs include spread, commission, slippage and market impact. Larger orders can consume available liquidity and move the execution price.

Order Size বড় হলে Slippage বাড়তে পারে।

29 · News and liquidity shocks

Economic releases can prompt dealers to reprice or withdraw liquidity. Spreads and execution uncertainty can increase even if a forecasted direction proves correct.

News Time-এ Liquidity কমে Spread বাড়তে পারে।

30 · Broker counterparty exposure

For OTC derivatives, a client may have a contractual claim against the broker rather than ownership of an exchange-traded asset. Read product and insolvency disclosures.

OTC CFD-তে Counterparty Risk বুঝতে Agreement পড়ো।

31 · Worked example: quote comparison

Dealer A quotes EUR/USD 1.1000/1.1002, a 2-pip spread. Dealer B quotes 1.0999/1.1003, a 4-pip spread. For a 10,000-EUR immediate round trip, spread-only cost is approximately $2 versus $4.

একই Pair হলেও Spread Cost Broker অনুযায়ী বদলায়।

2 pipsDealer A spread ≈ $2 for 10,000 EUR
4 pipsDealer B spread ≈ $4 for 10,000 EUR

Illustrative immediate round trip with unchanged quotes, no other fees and sufficient liquidity.

32 · How to investigate market structure

Choose EUR/USD and compare demo quotes from two authorized providers at the same timestamp. Record bid, ask, spreads, feed latency and differences without placing real trades.

Demo Data দিয়ে Broker Quote Comparison Practice করো।

  1. Use demo quotes, not real funds.
  2. Record UTC timestamps and bid/ask for each provider.
  3. Compute spread in pips.
  4. Note whether quotes are indicative or executable.
  5. Compare results over multiple market conditions.

33 · Common misconceptions

OTC does not mean unregulated everywhere. ECN does not automatically mean no dealer conflict. A single broker's volume is not global FX volume. Deep liquidity does not guarantee zero slippage.

Market Structure বুঝলে Misleading Marketing চিনতে সহজ হয়।

Mastery check

প্রথমে নিজে উত্তর দাও, তারপর Solution খুলে দেখো।

1. Does spot FX trade on one universal central exchange?

No, it is largely OTC and decentralized.

2. Can two dealers quote different EUR/USD prices simultaneously?

Yes.

3. What is a dealer's bid?

The price at which the dealer is willing to buy, under stated conditions.

4. What is a dealer's ask?

The price at which the dealer is willing to sell.

5. Does every retail order go directly to an interbank venue?

No.

6. What does an aggregator do?

Combines or selects quotes from multiple sources.

7. What is an indicative quote?

An informational price, not necessarily executable.

8. Is MT4 tick volume total global spot-FX volume?

No.

9. Does currency futures volume cover all spot FX transactions?

No.

10. What is market impact?

The effect of executing an order on available prices.

11. Dealer A spread 1.1000/1.1002 in EUR/USD?

2 pips.

12. Dealer B spread 1.0999/1.1003 in EUR/USD?

4 pips.

13. Does CLS eliminate price risk?

No, it addresses certain settlement risks for eligible transactions.
