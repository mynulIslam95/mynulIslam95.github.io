01 · What is order flow?

Order flow describes submitted, executed, amended and cancelled buy and sell interest over time. It is more than a count of green or red candles.

Order Flow মানে Buy/Sell Order এবং তাদের Execution, Change ও Cancellation-এর চলমান তথ্য।

02 · What is price discovery?

Price discovery is the process by which participants' information, demand, supply and risk preferences become reflected in tradable quotes and transaction prices.

Price Discovery হলো Market Information Quote আর Trade Price-এ Reflect হওয়ার Process।

03 · Every trade has two sides

Every executed purchase has a seller. Prices do not rise because buyers outnumber sellers in completed trades. They may rise because aggressive buyers consume available offers and quotes reprice.

প্রতিটি Executed Buy-এর বিপরীতে Sell আছে। Aggressive Flow Price Move করতে পারে।

04 · Aggressive and passive orders

A marketable buy typically takes liquidity from resting asks. A resting sell limit may provide liquidity. Aggressiveness refers to execution behavior, not trader intelligence.

Aggressive Order Liquidity Consume করে, Passive Order Wait করে।

05 · Bid and ask determine execution

Buy market orders normally execute at available ask prices. Sell market orders normally execute at available bid prices. Quotes and sizes may change before fills.

Buy Ask Side, Sell Bid Side Consume করে।

06 · An illustrative order book

Suppose EUR/USD asks offer 20k EUR at 1.1002, 30k at 1.1003 and 50k at 1.1005. A 70k market buy consumes the first two levels and 20k of the third.

70k Buy করলে একাধিক Ask Level Consume হবে।

Ask priceAvailable EURConsumed by 70k buy
1.100220,00020,000
1.100330,00030,000
1.100550,00020,000

07 · Weighted fill from order flow

For the 70k buy, the weighted average price is (20k×1.1002 + 30k×1.1003 + 20k×1.1005)/70k = 1.100342857, about 1.43 pips above the best ask.

Average Fill = Total Quote Currency Paid ÷ Base Units।

(20,000 × 1.1002 + 30,000 × 1.1003 + 20,000 × 1.1005) ÷ 70,000 ≈ 1.100342857

08 · Immediate versus persistent impact

An order may move the best quote temporarily, but the price can recover as liquidity replenishes. Persistent moves depend on information, subsequent flow and dealer repricing.

একটি Big Order-এর Impact Temporary বা Persistent হতে পারে।

09 · Order flow imbalance

A common descriptive measure compares aggressive buy volume with aggressive sell volume over a defined interval. Its meaning depends on the data source and classification method.

Buy Volume − Sell Volume হলো একটি Possible Imbalance Measure।

10 · Worked imbalance example

If an observed venue records 8 million EUR of buyer-initiated trades and 5 million EUR of seller-initiated trades, signed imbalance is +3 million EUR. This alone does not predict the next price change.

+3m EUR Imbalance Future Price Guaranteed Up বোঝায় না।

Signed imbalance = 8,000,000 − 5,000,000 = +3,000,000 EUR

11 · Trade direction classification

Some data feeds flag aggressor side. Others infer it from trades and quotes, which can introduce errors, especially with delayed or fragmented data.

Aggressor Side সব Dataset-এ Directly Available নয়।

12 · Limit-order replenishment

When aggressive buyers lift offers, other participants may post new sell limits at the same or nearby prices. Strong replenishment can absorb flow without a large price move.

Sell Liquidity Replenish হলে Buy Pressure Absorb হতে পারে।

13 · Absorption versus exhaustion

Absorption describes substantial aggressive flow meeting sufficient opposite liquidity. Exhaustion describes declining willingness to continue at current prices. Neither is a reliable signal without context.

Absorption দেখলেই Reversal Confirm হয় না।

14 · Hidden and cancelled orders

Displayed depth is incomplete and can change rapidly. Some venues allow hidden interest while displayed orders can be cancelled before your order arrives.

Visible Order Wall Guaranteed Support/Resistance নয়।

15 · Why a price moves without a trade

Dealers may revise bids and asks in response to news, risk or other venues. Quotes can change even if no transaction occurs on the particular feed being observed.

Trade Print ছাড়াও Quote Move হতে পারে।

16 · Spot FX is fragmented

There is no single consolidated order book for global OTC spot forex. Different dealers and venues see different flows, credit conditions and quotes.

Retail Forex DOM পুরো Interbank Flow দেখায় না।

17 · Futures order flow is not spot flow

Exchange-listed currency futures can offer venue-specific traded volume and order-book data. These are useful for that futures contract but not a complete record of OTC spot trading.

Futures Volume আর Global Spot FX Volume Same নয়।

18 · Tick volume limitations

A retail FX tick-volume indicator often counts quote changes, not executed global currency volume. Treat it as a feed-specific proxy.

MT4 Tick Volume = Global Executed Volume নয়।

19 · Price discovery across venues

Related futures, spot and forward markets can influence each other through hedging, arbitrage and information transmission. Leadership can vary over time.

Spot আর Futures Price একে অপরকে Influence করতে পারে।

20 · Economic announcements

Surprises in inflation, employment or policy can change expected rates and FX valuations. Order flow and dealer quotes adjust quickly, sometimes with poor liquidity.

News Surprise Market Repricing Trigger করতে পারে।

21 · Informed versus uninformed flow

Dealers may infer information from trade patterns, but flow also reflects hedging, rebalancing and mechanical execution. You cannot infer every participant's intention.

প্রতিটি Large Buy মানে Insider Information নয়।

22 · Stop orders and cascades

Triggered stops may create marketable flow that consumes liquidity, but exact placement of other traders' stops is generally not observable from a retail chart.

Stop Cluster Chart থেকে নিশ্চিতভাবে দেখা যায় না।

23 · Liquidity sweep claims

A move beyond a recent high or low can reflect multiple mechanisms, including stop triggering and repricing. Calling every wick a deliberate liquidity hunt is not justified.

প্রতিটি Wick-কে Stop Hunt বলা Evidence নয়।

24 · Volume delta and CVD

Cumulative volume delta sums estimated or observed buyer-initiated minus seller-initiated traded volume. Its reliability depends on venue coverage and classification accuracy.

CVD শুধু যে Data Feed Use করছ তার Flow দেখায়।

25 · CVD divergence caution

Price rising while a venue's CVD falls can have multiple explanations, including passive liquidity changes and cross-venue activity. It is not a guaranteed reversal signal.

CVD Divergence Guaranteed Entry Signal নয়।

26 · Order flow and time horizon

Millisecond market making and daily macro trading operate on different horizons. A short-term imbalance need not matter to a long-term currency trend.

Timeframe অনুযায়ী Flow-এর Meaning বদলায়।

27 · Market impact versus transaction costs

The quoted spread, execution slippage, commissions and price impact are related but distinct. Avoid counting the same execution difference twice.

Spread, Slippage আর Impact হিসাব করতে Double Count এড়াও।

28 · Risk of interpreting screenshots

A single depth screenshot or footprint chart is incomplete without timestamps, venue, size and trade classification. Prefer repeatable datasets.

একটি Screenshot দিয়ে Market Manipulation Prove হয় না।

29 · Practical experiment design

Use historical or demo venue-specific data. Record quote, trade side if available, quantity, time and subsequent midpoint. Compare multiple windows rather than one anecdote.

Demo বা Historical Data দিয়ে Flow Study করো।

30 · Spreadsheet lab: signed flow

Create 12 hypothetical trades with side and EUR quantity. Encode buy-initiated volume as positive and sell-initiated as negative, then compute cumulative signed flow.

Spreadsheet-এ Signed Volume আর CVD Calculate করো।

31 · Spreadsheet lab: quote response

Alongside each hypothetical trade, record the midpoint before and after. Plot cumulative signed flow against midpoint, then describe cases where they disagree.

Flow আর Midprice একসাথে Chart করো।

32 · Common interpretation mistakes

Avoid saying buyers exceeded sellers in completed trades, treating volume delta as universal, equating price movement with manipulation or assuming every imbalance predicts continuation.

Order Flow Context ছাড়া Sure Prediction দেয় না।

33 · Trading application without prediction claims

Use order flow to describe execution conditions and test hypotheses with out-of-sample data, costs and risk controls. Do not assume an observed pattern creates an edge.

Backtest, Cost Model আর Risk Management ছাড়া Live Signal Trust করো না।

34 · Mastery project

Write a one-page report using a simulated 12-trade tape. Calculate signed volume, CVD and two average execution prices. Explain three limitations of the dataset.

12 Trade Dataset দিয়ে ছোট Report Complete করো।

  2. Calculate signed volume and cumulative volume delta.
  3. Add before/after midpoints and visualize both series.
  4. Calculate weighted fills for two hypothetical market orders using an ask or bid ladder.
  5. Discuss incomplete venue coverage, trade-side inference and execution costs.

No.

Signed buyer-initiated minus seller-initiated volume over time.

No.

No.
