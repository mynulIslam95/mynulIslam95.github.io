Module 02 · Market Structure and Participants

Liquidity, Depth and Order Books

Learn how bid-ask ladders, available size, order-book depth and market impact influence actual trade execution. বাংলা ও English explanations with worked calculations and a spreadsheet project.

  1. Explain liquidity, depth, spread and price impact.

Three concepts to separate

LiquidityAbility to trade
DepthAvailable size at prices
ImpactCost of consuming depth

01 · Liquidity: the practical meaning

Liquidity is the ability to transact a desired quantity promptly with limited price impact and reasonable cost. It is not merely how often prices change.

Liquidity মানে শুধু Fast Price Movement নয়, যথেষ্ট Quantity Fair Price-এ Trade করতে পারা।

02 · Depth: quantity behind the price

Market depth measures quantities available at different prices on a particular venue or feed. The best displayed price might support only a small order.

Best Quote-এর পেছনে Available Quantity Limited হতে পারে।

03 · Order book anatomy

A limit order book shows bids to buy and asks to sell, organized by price and quantity. Its scope is limited to that venue and its rules.

Order Book-এর Bid Side আর Ask Side আলাদা।

04 · Best bid and best ask

The highest currently available bid and lowest currently available ask form the top of book. A tradeable spread is ask minus bid.

Best Bid Highest Buy Quote, Best Ask Lowest Sell Quote।

05 · Illustrative EUR/USD book

Suppose asks are 1.1002 for 20,000 EUR, 1.1003 for 30,000 and 1.1005 for 50,000. Bids are 1.1000 for 25,000 and 1.0999 for 35,000. These are hypothetical venue-specific quantities.

এই Order Book Example পুরো Global Forex Market-এর নয়।

06 · Bid-ask spread calculation

If bid is 1.1000 and ask is 1.1002, the difference is 0.0002 USD/EUR, or 2 pips for EUR/USD.

EUR/USD Spread 2 Pip।

07 · Top-of-book versus effective execution

A 100,000-EUR buy cannot all fill at 1.1002 if only 20,000 EUR is offered there. It may sweep several ask levels.

Order Size Depth-এর বেশি হলে Multiple Price-এ Fill হয়।

08 · Weighted-average execution price

For 20,000 at 1.1002, 30,000 at 1.1003 and 50,000 at 1.1005, the average buy price is 1.10038. It is 1.8 pips above the best initial ask.

Weighted Average Price = Total Cost ÷ Total Units।

(20,000 × 1.1002 + 30,000 × 1.1003 + 50,000 × 1.1005) ÷ 100,000 = 1.10038

09 · Market impact cost

Relative to buying all 100,000 at the initial best ask 1.1002, the depth-based price difference is $18, excluding spread, fees and subsequent market changes.

Market Impact আলাদা Cost, শুধু Spread নয়।

(1.10038 − 1.1002) × 100,000 = $18

10 · Sell-side book walk

A 40,000-EUR sell fills 25,000 at 1.1000 and 15,000 at 1.0999 in this example. Average sell price is 1.0999625.

Sell Order Bid Levels Consume করে।

(25,000 × 1.1000 + 15,000 × 1.0999) ÷ 40,000 = 1.0999625

11 · Displayed versus hidden liquidity

Some venues support hidden, reserve or iceberg interest. Displayed depth is not necessarily all executable liquidity, and hidden orders may have different priority.

Displayed Order Book পুরো Liquidity দেখায় না।

12 · Order book is venue-specific

Exchange-traded futures have an exchange-specific central limit order book. OTC spot FX is fragmented across dealers, bilateral relationships and electronic venues.

Spot Forex-এর Single Global Order Book নেই।

13 · Retail DOM limitations

A retail platform's depth-of-market window may show broker or provider-specific quotes rather than the entire spot-FX market. Read the provider's documentation.

MT5 DOM দেখলেই Global Bank Orders দেখা যায় না।

14 · Liquidity providers and quote streams

Banks and non-bank firms can stream quotes with size limits, credit checks and acceptance rules. A quote may be withdrawn or repriced.

Liquidity Provider Quote Market Condition অনুযায়ী Change করে।

15 · Tight spread versus deep market

A 0.5-pip spread for a tiny available size may be less useful for a large trade than a 1-pip spread with substantial depth.

Low Spread মানেই Large Order-এর Best Execution নয়।

16 · Market depth and slippage

Thin depth makes larger market orders more likely to execute at worse prices. Slippage also depends on latency, volatility and execution rules.

Thin Liquidity-তে Slippage Risk বাড়ে।

17 · Resiliency

A resilient market can replenish liquidity after trades or shocks. A deep snapshot alone cannot show how quickly liquidity recovers.

Liquidity Recovery Speed-ও Important।

18 · Liquidity during news

Before major releases, quotes may become thinner and spreads wider. Rapid repricing can make snapshots stale.

High Impact News-এর সময় Depth কমে যেতে পারে।

19 · Liquidity during rollover

Daily FX rollover and holidays can bring thinner activity and wider spreads at some brokers. Broker-specific schedules matter.

Rollover Time-এ Spread Widen হতে পারে।

20 · Session overlaps

London-New York overlap can coincide with more participants, but conditions differ by currency pair and day. Session labels do not guarantee deep liquidity.

Session Overlap মানেই Guaranteed Low Spread নয়।

21 · Limit order queue

Resting orders at the same price may have time or other venue-defined priority. A touched price does not ensure a fill.

Price Touch করলেও Queue Priority-এর কারণে Fill Miss হতে পারে।

22 · Passive versus aggressive flow

A resting order may provide liquidity. A marketable order consumes available liquidity. Both have opportunity and execution costs.

Passive Order Wait করে, Aggressive Order Liquidity Consume করে।

23 · Adverse selection

Passive orders can be filled just before prices move against them. A maker rebate or narrow spread does not eliminate this risk.

Limit Fill হলেই Good Trade নয়।

24 · Cancellation and quote fading

Visible quotes can be cancelled or revised before your order reaches them. Screenshots are not promises of future fills.

Quote Cancel হলে Displayed Depth Disappear করতে পারে।

25 · Partial fills

Large orders may execute in several parts. Always reconcile filled size, remaining quantity and average price.

Partial Fill হলে Actual Exposure Check করো।

26 · Liquidity and volatility are different

Liquidity describes ability to trade; volatility describes price variation. A volatile market may have deep or thin liquidity.

Volatility আর Liquidity Same Concept নয়।

27 · Volume and liquidity are different

Reported volume measures trading on a defined venue or dataset. High volume does not always mean tight spreads for every order size.

High Volume মানেই Zero Slippage নয়।

28 · Tick volume caveat

Retail forex tick volume often counts quote changes. It is not a complete measure of global spot-FX trading volume or available depth.

Tick Volume Global Forex Volume নয়।

29 · Slippage journal

Track expected quote, actual fill, units, spread, order type, latency and market event. Compare executions across different conditions.

Journal-এ Expected আর Actual Fill লিখো।

30 · Demo order-book exercise

Using a demo or historical snapshot, calculate spread, total available size within 3 pips and average fill price for a 50,000-unit buy.

Demo Book দিয়ে Weighted Average Calculate করো।

31 · Sizing against depth

Avoid assuming a position can be entered or exited at one displayed quote. Include potential market impact when planning size.

Position Size Decide করার সময় Available Depth Consider করো।

32 · Liquidity risk in leveraged trades

Leverage magnifies the consequences of a poor exit. Stop orders may fill beyond the requested level when depth disappears.

Leverage আর Thin Liquidity একসাথে Dangerous।

33 · Common misconceptions

There is no universal spot-FX order book, a tight spread does not guarantee depth, a large wall can be cancelled and displayed quotes do not guarantee fills.

Order Book দেখে Sure Profit Prediction করা যায় না।

34 · Mastery project

Build a small spreadsheet with bid/ask ladders. Calculate spread, cumulative depth, weighted fill and impact for 10k, 50k and 100k units. Explain which assumptions fail in live trading.

Spreadsheet-এ Order Book Simulator বানাও।

  1. Make columns for price, size and cumulative size.
  2. Compute best bid and ask spread.
  3. For each buy quantity, consume asks from lowest to highest.
  4. Compute weighted average and cost versus the best ask.
  5. Write down why hidden liquidity, cancellations and latency limit the simulation.

Professional Forex case study · worked answer

Depth and execution-weighted pricing

Worked example: To buy EUR 1.5m, fill EUR 1m at ask 1.0850 and EUR 0.5m at ask 1.0853. VWAP = (1m×1.0850 + 0.5m×1.0853)/1.5m = 1.0851.

Professional limitation: Displayed size is conditional and may not be accessible. The midpoint does not measure executable depth.

বাংলা ব্যাখ্যা: Weighted Fill Price বের করতে Quantity দিয়ে Weight করবে।

Oral defense and verification task

Evidence standard and professional interpretation

What can be supported: Venue depth is conditional and cannot be generalized to the entire decentralized FX market. Distinguish quoted size from realized fills.

What cannot be inferred: A single quote, candle, order-flow indicator or illustrative calculation cannot prove hidden institutional intent, future price direction or persistent profitability.
