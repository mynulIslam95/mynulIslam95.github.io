Body, Wick and Range

Measure candle geometry correctly, interpret it cautiously and learn why long wicks are observations rather than proof of institutional intent.

Candle-এর Body, Upper Wick, Lower Wick ও Range-এর সঠিক Formula, Pip Calculation, Market Context এবং Research Method শিখবে।

Mastery objectives
  1. Derive all four OHLC components and verify their arithmetic identity.
  2. Compute body and wick ratios while handling zero-range bars correctly.
  3. Use pip conventions for EUR/USD and USD/JPY without confusing currency units.
  4. Explain intrabar ambiguity, feed differences and execution limitations.
  5. Design a falsifiable wick-pattern study without look-ahead bias.

1. The anatomy of a candle

For any valid candle, H is at least as high as both O and C, while L is no higher than either O or C. On a bullish candle C > O; on a bearish candle C < O. Wick formulas work for both without changing their definitions.

Bullish হলে Close ওপরে, Bearish হলে Open ওপরে থাকে। কিন্তু Upper Wick ও Lower Wick-এর Formula দুই ক্ষেত্রেই একই।

2. Mathematical identities

Body = |C − O|
Upper wick = H − max(O,C)
Lower wick = min(O,C) − L
Range = H − L

Because max(O,C) − min(O,C) = |C−O|, the components satisfy:

Upper wick + Body + Lower wick = Range

When Range > 0, body ratio = Body/Range, upper-wick ratio = Upper wick/Range and lower-wick ratio = Lower wick/Range. These nonnegative ratios sum to 1. When H=L, all four OHLC prices must be equal for a valid bar and the ratios are undefined, not zero.

Range শূন্য হলে Body/Range হিসাব করবে না, কারণ Zero দিয়ে ভাগ করা যায় না।

3. Fully solved examples

Example A: bullish EUR/USD

O=1.0840, H=1.0870, L=1.0825, C=1.0860. Body=20 pips, upper wick=10 pips, lower wick=15 pips and range=45 pips. Identity: 20+10+15=45. Ratios: body 44.44%, upper 22.22%, lower 33.33%.

Example B: bearish USD/JPY

O=150.20, H=150.50, L=149.80, C=150.00. Using 0.01 JPY per USD per pip: body=20 pips, upper wick=30 pips, lower wick=20 pips and range=70 pips. Identity: 20+30+20=70.

USD/JPY-তে সাধারণত 1 Pip = 0.01, EUR/USD-তে 1 Pip = 0.0001। একই Formula, কিন্তু Pip Unit আলাদা।

Example C: neutral and zero-range

O=C=1.1000, H=1.1010, L=1.0990: body=0, upper=10 pips, lower=10 pips, range=20 pips. This is neutral by body but not zero-range. If O=H=L=C=1.1000, range is zero and all body-to-range ratios are undefined.

5. What wick length can and cannot establish

Long upper wickHigh exceeded open and close substantiallyInstitutions definitely sold
Long lower wickLow fell below open and close substantiallyGuaranteed bullish reversal
Small body, large rangeOpen and close were close relative to extremaMarket must be indecisive next bar
No lower wickLow equals min(open,close)There were no sellers

OHLC does not show whether the high occurred before the low, how many trades occurred or which institution acted. Two very different intrabar paths can produce identical OHLC bars.

Long Wick দেখে Bank বা Smart Money-এর নিশ্চিত Intent জানা যায় না। একই OHLC-এর ভিতরে Price Movement-এর Sequence ভিন্ন হতে পারে।

6. Comparing candle shapes responsibly

Ratios help compare geometry across different ranges, but ratios alone ignore the absolute volatility scale. A 1-pip bar and a 100-pip bar can share identical proportions. Compare absolute range, typical recent volatility, session, spread and feed quality before interpreting any pattern.

Worked comparison: Candle A has body 4 pips and range 10 pips, so body ratio 40%. Candle B has body 40 pips and range 100 pips, also 40%. The geometry ratio matches, but the executable exposure and volatility conditions differ markedly.

Normalization by average true range (ATR) may add context, but ATR is based on historical bar data and can be unstable across regimes. For FX, distinguish quote-based bar range from actual tradable bid/ask movement.

7. Research: does a long lower wick predict anything?

Falsifiable hypothesis: For EUR/USD 15-minute midpoint candles, a lower-wick-to-range ratio greater than 0.6 predicts a higher next-bar net long return than a predeclared baseline.

  1. Record feed, instrument, pip convention, timezone and candle aggregation method.
  2. Exclude or separately classify zero-range and invalid bars before analysis.
  3. Use the completed signal candle only. Do not enter before its close.
  4. Enter the next bar at an executable ask and exit at a specified future bid. Model commissions, variable spreads, slippage and rejected fills.
  5. Predefine the 0.6 threshold, baseline, sample size and outcome metric before looking at holdout results.
  6. Split development and untouched holdout periods chronologically. Track all tested thresholds to reduce data-snooping bias.
  7. Report trade count, net expectancy, drawdown, uncertainty and sensitivity to costs. Compare multiple market regimes.
  8. Document null or negative results. Forward paper test any candidate before considering live execution.

Wick Ratio বড় হলেই Reversal হবে এমন নয়। Realistic Spread, Bid/Ask ও Out-of-Sample Test ছাড়া Trading Edge দাবি করবে না।

9. Mastery examination · 20 questions

Attempt each question before expanding its answer.

Q1. What is the real body?

Absolute difference |C−O|, measured in price units or pips.

Q2. What is the upper wick?

H−max(O,C).

Q3. What is the lower wick?

min(O,C)−L.

Q4. What is the total range?

H−L.

Q5. What must the candle components sum to?

Body + upper wick + lower wick = total range.

Q6. What is the body-to-range ratio?

|C−O|/(H−L) for nonzero range.

Q7. What happens if H=L?

Valid only if O=C=H=L; body/range is undefined.

Q8. Can a bullish candle have no upper wick?

Yes, when C=H.

Q9. Can a bearish candle have no lower wick?

Yes, when C=L.

Q10. Is a long lower wick always bullish?

No. It is a descriptive geometry and not a reliable standalone forecast.

Q11. Can upper wick exceed body?

Yes, without violating OHLC constraints.

Q12. What is a pip in EUR/USD?

Usually 0.0001 USD per EUR.

Q13. What is a pip in USD/JPY?

Usually 0.01 JPY per USD.

Q14. What is a pipette?

Typically one tenth of a pip in common retail quote formats.

Q15. Why are absolute pip ranges hard to compare across pairs?

Pip conventions, exchange rates, volatility and contract exposure differ.

Q16. Why normalize candle size?

To compare relative body/wick geometry and volatility across bars, with clear handling of zero range.

Q17. What does a large upper wick prove?

Only that the high exceeded the greater of open and close; not who traded or why.

Q18. Can OHLC tell whether high occurred before low?

No. Intrabar sequencing is not preserved.

Q19. Why might broker candles differ?

Different bid/ask/mid feeds, time boundaries, missing quotes and sampling rules.

Q20. How do you test a wick hypothesis?

Predefine thresholds, timestamps, baseline, executable costs, out-of-sample evaluation and uncertainty.

Worked independent verification

EUR/USD O=1.0900, H=1.0915, L=1.0870, C=1.0880: bearish body=20 pips, upper wick=15 pips, lower wick=10 pips, total range=45 pips. Check: 20+15+10=45.

Change the close to 1.0905. What happens?

It becomes bullish. Body=5 pips, upper wick=10 pips, lower wick=30 pips, total range=45 pips. Check: 5+10+30=45.

Evidence standards and institutional context

Price bars summarize observed quotes or trades. The FX market is decentralized and a chart cannot identify all institutional transactions or guarantee a future price path. Quote-side conventions, data coverage and venue access matter for interpreting candles.

  • Global Foreign Exchange Committee: FX Global Code and market conduct resources
  • BIS: FX market turnover and survey methodology
  • CME Group: exchange-traded FX contract and data context

These are institutional background resources, not proof of a wick-based trading strategy.

Risk reminder

Leveraged Forex and derivatives can cause rapid losses. A wick is a descriptive statistic, not a trading signal with guaranteed predictive power. Historical research and paper trading cannot guarantee future results.
