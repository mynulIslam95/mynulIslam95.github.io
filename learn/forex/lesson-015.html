01 · Platforms versus brokers

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and TradingView are software interfaces. A trading platform is not automatically the broker, exchange, regulator or custodian.

Platform আর Broker এক জিনিস নয়।

02 · MetaTrader 4 overview

MT4 is a long-established terminal popular for forex charting, order entry, indicators and Expert Advisors written in MQL4.

MT4-তে Forex Chart, Order আর EA ব্যবহার করা যায়।

03 · MetaTrader 5 overview

MT5 supports multiple asset classes depending on broker, more native order and testing features, an economic calendar and MQL5 automation.

MT5-এর Features MT4-এর থেকে আলাদা, কিন্তু Broker Support দেখতে হবে।

04 · TradingView overview

TradingView focuses on browser/mobile charting, watchlists, alerts, indicators and Pine Script. Live order execution requires a supported broker integration.

TradingView Charting Platform, সব Broker-এ Direct Trade করা যায় না।

05 · Comparison table

MT4 and MT5 are commonly installed terminals, while TradingView emphasizes web-based analysis. All three have mobile options. The best choice depends on instruments, broker and workflow.

06 · Demo versus live accounts

Demo trading uses simulated funds and may not reproduce live slippage, rejection, liquidity or emotional pressure. Confirm the account is demo before practice.

Demo Account-এ Real Money Risk নেই, কিন্তু Live Execution আলাদা।

07 · Create a watchlist

Add EUR/USD, GBP/USD and USD/JPY. Label base and quote currencies. Check whether displayed quotes are live, delayed or indicative.

Watchlist বানিয়ে Pair-গুলো আলাদা করে Observe করো।

08 · Bid, ask and spread display

The chart may show bid, ask, midpoint or last price. Enable an ask line where available and inspect symbol details before placing an order.

Chart Candle আর Executable Price সবসময় Same না।

09 · Timeframes

M1, M5, M15, H1, H4 and D1 refer to candle aggregation intervals. Timeframe choice changes chart noise, not underlying trading costs.

M1 থেকে D1 Timeframe Change করে Structure Compare করো।

10 · Candlestick basics on platform

Open OHLC candles, inspect the crosshair and measure price movement. Verify the broker's candle time zone and how daily candles are formed.

OHLC আর Candle Time Zone ঠিকমতো বুঝো।

11 · Market order ticket

Check symbol, side, volume, bid/ask, deviation/slippage rules and estimated margin before submitting a market order.

Order Click করার আগে Volume আর Direction Double Check করো।

12 · Pending order ticket

Practice buy limit, sell limit, buy stop and sell stop in demo. Stop-limit support and trigger rules differ between terminals and brokers.

Pending Order Type Broker অনুযায়ী Different হতে পারে।

13 · Stop-loss and take-profit fields

Set protective and target levels in the order ticket if available. Check minimum stop distance, freeze levels and whether the broker allows modification.

SL/TP Set করলেও Gap Risk থাকে।

14 · Position versus order

A pending order is an instruction awaiting execution. An open position is current exposure. Account mode may net opposite trades or hedge them separately.

Pending Order আর Open Position এক নয়।

15 · Lots and units

For many spot FX pairs, a standard lot commonly represents 100,000 base currency units, but contract specifications vary by broker and instrument.

Lot Size Trade করার আগে Contract Size Check করো।

16 · Margin and free margin

Review balance, equity, used margin and free margin in the terminal. Broker margin level and stop-out rules differ by account and jurisdiction.

Margin Level Low হলে Liquidation Risk বাড়ে।

17 · Order history and trade report

Inspect execution time, fill price, commission, swap and realized P&L. Export a statement to compare actual results with your trading journal.

Trade History Export করে Fees Verify করো।

18 · Indicators and templates

Moving averages, RSI and ATR can be added for analysis, but indicators are not predictive guarantees. Save a simple template rather than crowding the chart.

Indicator বেশি হলেই Signal Better হয় না।

19 · Drawing tools

Use horizontal levels, trendlines, rectangles and measurement tools to mark ranges, entry zones and invalidation. Drawings are hypotheses, not support guarantees.

Chart Drawing দিয়ে Plan Visualize করো।

20 · Alerts

Price alerts can notify you when a level is reached, but notifications may be delayed or missed. Alerts are not equivalent to server-side protective orders.

Alert Stop-Loss-এর Replacement নয়।

21 · Expert Advisors and MQL4/MQL5

MT4 automation uses MQL4 and MT5 uses MQL5. They are not drop-in compatible. Backtest and inspect source before enabling automated execution.

Unknown EA Live Account-এ Run করো না।

22 · Pine Script and TradingView strategies

Pine Script can create indicators, alerts and strategy simulations. A TradingView strategy backtest is not automatically a live broker execution system.

Pine Backtest আর Live Bot আলাদা।

23 · Backtesting assumptions

Include commission, spread, slippage, financing, position size and realistic fills. Beware look-ahead bias and overfitting.

Backtest-এ Realistic Cost Add করো।

24 · Strategy tester on MT5

MT5 provides a built-in strategy tester for compatible Expert Advisors. Data quality, model settings and broker symbols affect results.

Strategy Tester Result Live Profit Guarantee করে না।

25 · Paper trading on TradingView

TradingView paper trading is useful for order workflow practice. Broker integration and paper account features may differ.

Paper Trading দিয়ে UI আর Order Practice করো।

26 · Mobile versus desktop

Mobile apps are convenient for monitoring, while desktop/browser interfaces may provide richer charting or automation. Check where alerts and trailing stops are hosted.

App Close করলে Client-side Feature বন্ধ হতে পারে।

27 · Security and broker verification

Download from official sources, use strong unique passwords and MFA where available. Verify broker licensing with the relevant regulator, not just a logo on a website.

Official Source ছাড়া Trading App Install করো না।

28 · Platform connection problems

If prices freeze or orders fail, inspect connectivity, market hours, symbol availability and broker notices. Never assume a failed click means no order exists; verify the order list.

Connection Problem হলে Duplicate Order Place করো না।

29 · Journal integration

Capture screenshot, timestamp in UTC, pair, direction, units, setup, stop, target, spread, commission and actual fill in a journal.

Journal-এ Entry Reason আর Actual Fill Save করো।

30 · Hands-on demo lab

Open a demo account, chart EUR/USD on H1, enable bid/ask view, place a small hypothetical limit order with SL/TP, cancel it and export history. Do not use real funds for the exercise.

Demo-তে Order Place, Modify, Cancel আর History Export Practice করো।

  1. Use demo only.
  2. Record bid, ask and spread.
  3. Measure 20 pips on EUR/USD.
  4. Place a pending order with a hypothetical stop and target.
  5. Cancel the pending order and verify status.
  6. Export or record the result.

31 · Common mistakes

Confusing a platform with a regulated broker, assuming a chart price is executable, trusting unverified robots, using live money to learn buttons and forgetting account mode or contract size.

Platform শিখতে Live Money দরকার নেই।
