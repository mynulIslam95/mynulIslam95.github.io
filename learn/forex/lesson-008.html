Equity, Balance, Free Margin and Margin Level

Simplified common retail trading-platform definitions. Broker adjustments and product rules may differ.

01 / Five numbers on your trading terminal

Trading Terminal-এ Balance, Equity, Used Margin, Free Margin আর Margin Level দেখাবে। এগুলো পাঁচটা আলাদা Concept। Balance হলো Closed Transactions-এর Result, Equity হলো Open Position-এর Current Floating P&L-সহ Account Value।

$1,000Balance
$920Equity
$300Used margin

Hypothetical USD account, with −$80 floating P&L and no other adjustments.

02 / Balance: what has already been booked

Balance সাধারণত Deposit, Withdrawal আর Realized Profit/Loss-সহ Booked Amount। Open Trade-এর Floating Profit বা Loss সাধারণত Balance Change করে না, যতক্ষণ না Position Close হয়।

New balance = prior balance + realized P&L − booked fees ± deposits/withdrawals

Some providers post financing or other adjustments while a position is open. Always check the broker's account statement.

03 / Equity: the live account value

Equity = Balance + Open Trades-এর Floating P&L, Broker-specific Adjustments সহ। Balance $1,000, Floating Loss −$80 হলে Equity $920।

Equity = $1,000 − $80 = $920

Unrealized gains can raise equity without changing balance. Unrealized losses can reduce equity while balance stays the same.

04 / Why balance can look healthy while equity falls

Balance $2,000 দেখে ভাবতে পারো Account Safe। কিন্তু Open Position-এ −$900 Floating Loss থাকলে Equity মাত্র $1,100। তাই Risk Monitor করতে Balance একা যথেষ্ট নয়।

$2,000Balance
$1,100Equity

Key idea: the balance can stay unchanged even while losses erode the amount supporting your open trades.

05 / Used margin: collateral assigned to positions

Used Margin হলো Open Positions Support করতে Broker-এর Calculation অনুযায়ী Required Collateral। এটা Profit/Loss নয়। Used Margin Position Open, Close, Resize বা Margin Requirement Change হলে বদলাতে পারে।

Illustrative used margin = $300

Some brokers use hedged-position discounts, tiered margin, currency conversion or dynamic requirements. The platform figure is authoritative for that account.

06 / Free margin: what's left after used margin

Free Margin সাধারণ Platform Convention-এ Equity থেকে Used Margin Subtract করলে পাওয়া যায়।

Free margin = equity − used margin
$920 − $300 = $620

Free margin is not necessarily withdrawable cash. Broker withdrawal restrictions, open P&L, unsettled charges and margin rules can limit withdrawals.

07 / Margin level: a ratio, not money

Margin Level Percentage দিয়ে Equity আর Used Margin-এর Ratio Measure করা হয়। এটা Dollar Balance না।

Margin level (%) = equity ÷ used margin × 100
$920 ÷ $300 × 100 ≈ 306.67%

If used margin is zero, this ratio is mathematically undefined. Platforms may display a dash, zero or another placeholder.

09 / Winning trade: which values change?

Balance $1,000, Used Margin $300। Floating P&L +$150 হলে Equity $1,150, Free Margin $850 আর Margin Level প্রায় 383.33%। Balance এখনো $1,000।

Equity = 1,000 + 150 = $1,150
Margin level = 1,150 ÷ 300 × 100 ≈ 383.33%

10 / Losing trade: which values change?

Same Balance $1,000 আর Used Margin $300। Floating Loss −$400 হলে Equity $600, Free Margin $300 আর Margin Level 200%।

Equity = 1,000 − 400 = $600
Margin level = 600 ÷ 300 × 100 = 200%

11 / Closing a winning position

ধরো Balance $1,000 আর Open Trade Floating Profit +$150। Trade Close করলে, Fees বাদে Balance $1,150 হবে এবং Floating P&L Zero হবে। Equity Closure Moment-এ প্রায় Same থাকতে পারে।

Before closeBalance $1,000 · Floating +$150
After closeBalance $1,150 · Floating $0

Assumes no additional fees, slippage or price movement at closing. Closing may release used margin.

12 / Closing a losing position

Balance $1,000 আর Floating Loss −$200 হলে Position Close করলে Balance প্রায় $800 হয়ে যায়। Floating Loss Realized হয়, কিন্তু সেটা Closure Moment-এ New Loss নয়।

New balance ≈ $1,000 − $200 = $800

Closing may also free the margin associated with that position. The exact posted result can differ because of final execution and charges.

13 / Deposits and withdrawals

Deposit করলে Balance আর Equity সাধারণত দুটোই Increase করে। Withdrawal করলে দুটোই Decrease করে। Used Margin Same থাকলে Margin Level-ও Change হয়।

Example: balance $1,000, equity $900, used margin $300. Deposit $200:

New balance = $1,200; equity = $1,100
New margin level ≈ 366.67%

Assumes instant posting, no fee and unchanged floating P&L.

14 / Multiple open positions

EUR/USD Long +$100 আর GBP/USD Short −$180 হলে Combined Floating P&L −$80। Equity Calculation-এ সব Open Positions Count হবে।

Combined floating P&L = +$100 − $180 = −$80

Used margin may be summed, offset or otherwise adjusted depending on account netting and hedging rules.

15 / Free margin is not your risk budget

Free Margin $700 আছে মানেই $700 Risk নেওয়া উচিত না। এটা Account's Collateral Capacity Estimate, Planned Maximum Loss নয়। Risk Budget আলাদাভাবে Stop Distance, Position Size, Fees আর Worst-Case Execution দিয়ে Consider করতে হয়।

Margin capacity ≠ affordable loss. A broker may permit an exposure that would be financially inappropriate for the trader.

16 / Margin level can change for several reasons

Margin Level শুধু Price Loss-এর কারণে Change হয় না। Floating Gain, Deposit, Withdrawal, Added Positions, Closed Positions, Margin Requirement Changes এবং Currency Conversion সব Affect করতে পারে।

  1. Equity rises → margin level tends to rise if used margin stays fixed.
  2. Equity falls → margin level tends to fall if used margin stays fixed.
  3. Used margin rises → margin level tends to fall if equity stays fixed.
  4. Used margin falls → margin level tends to rise if equity stays fixed.

17 / What happens as equity approaches used margin?

Equity = Used Margin হলে Margin Level 100%। কিন্তু 100% মানেই Every Broker Margin Call করবে, এমন না। Warning, Liquidation এবং Close-Out Threshold Broker/Product/Regulation অনুযায়ী আলাদা।

Equity $300 ÷ used margin $300 × 100 = 100%

Do not assume that 100% is a universal liquidation threshold.

18 / Margin close-out is not a stop-loss

Broker Margin Close-Out Rules Account Protection-এর জন্য Position Close করতে পারে। কিন্তু Price Gap, Spread Widening আর Slippage-এর কারণে Exact Threshold-এ Execution Guaranteed নয়।

For EU retail CFDs, applicable product-intervention measures generally include an account-level margin close-out rule at 50% of the minimum required margin. This is not the same as a personal stop-loss or a guarantee of the execution price.

19 / Why negative free margin is a warning

Free Margin Negative হলে Equity Used Margin-এর নিচে নেমেছে। কিন্তু Broker কী Action নেবে সেটা Contract Terms আর Close-Out Policy Determine করে।

Equity $250 − used margin $300 = −$50 free margin

A negative free-margin reading is not necessarily a negative account balance.

20 / Margin usage percentage

কিছু Dashboard Margin Utilization বা Margin Usage দেখায়। অনেক ক্ষেত্রে এটা Used Margin ÷ Equity × 100। এটা Margin Level-এর Reciprocal Relation, তবে Provider Definition Check করতে হবে।

Margin usage = $300 ÷ $920 × 100 ≈ 32.61%

Margin level ≈ 306.67%; 100 ÷ 3.0667 ≈ 32.61% usage. Percentages use different denominators.

21 / Currency conversion complicates equity

Account EUR-তে আর Open Position-এর P&L USD-তে হলে Broker USD Profit/Loss-কে EUR-তে Convert করে Equity Show করতে পারে। Conversion Rate Change হলে Account-Currency Equity Change হতে পারে।

Example: +$110 floating P&L at EUR/USD 1.1000 is approximately +€100 before conversion fees.

$110 ÷ 1.1000 USD/EUR = €100

22 / Swap, commissions and financing

Spread ছাড়াও Commission, Swap, Financing বা Other Fees Equity/Balance Affect করতে পারে। Provider Charge কখন Book করে সেটা Platform-ভেদে আলাদা।

Review account statements and the broker's P&L methodology rather than assuming every charge stays unrealized until close.

23 / Stress test: a falling account

Balance $1,000 আর Used Margin $400। Floating P&L 0, −$100, −$300, −$600 হলে Equity, Free Margin আর Margin Level কীভাবে Change হয় দেখো।

Floating P&LEquityFree marginMargin level
$0$1,000$600250%
−$100$900$500225%
−$300$700$300175%
−$600$400$0100%

Pure arithmetic, not a prediction of when a provider would liquidate positions.

24 / A real-world monitoring checklist

Position Open থাকলে শুধু Balance না দেখে Equity, Floating P&L, Used Margin, Free Margin, Margin Level, Upcoming News, Spread আর Overnight Charges Check করো।

  1. Confirm account currency and instrument profit currency.
  2. Review floating P&L and equity together.
  3. Check used margin and margin level.
  4. Identify broker-specific warning and liquidation thresholds.
  5. Estimate adverse-move losses, not just available margin.
  6. Check fees, conversion and market-gap risks.
  7. Do not treat deposit or averaging down as a guaranteed solution.

1. Balance $1,200, floating P&L −$250, used margin $400. Equity?

$950.

2. Same account: free margin?

$950 − $400 = $550.

3. Same account: margin level?

$950 ÷ $400 × 100 = 237.5%.

4. Balance $800, floating +$120, used margin $230. Equity and free margin?

Equity $920; free margin $690.

5. Balance $2,000, floating −$1,000, used margin $500. Margin level?

Equity $1,000; margin level 200%.

6. Balance $1,000, floating −$100. Close position with no additional charges. New balance?

$900; floating P&L becomes zero.

7. Equity $500, used margin $600. Is the account balance necessarily negative?

No. Free margin is −$100, but balance could still be positive.

8. Used margin is zero. What is the mathematical margin-level ratio?

Undefined because the denominator is zero; broker display conventions vary.
