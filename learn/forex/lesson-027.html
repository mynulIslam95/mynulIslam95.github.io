Module 02 · Market Structure and Participants

Market Impact and Transaction Costs

Learn how spread, liquidity, market impact, slippage and fees affect real execution. বাংলা ও English explanations with weighted-average fill calculations and a spreadsheet project.

Study objectives and readiness

What you should be able to do

  1. Separate explicit costs from implicit execution costs.
  2. Calculate weighted fill prices and benchmark shortfall.
  3. Avoid double-counting spreads and market impact.
  4. Build a realistic hypothetical transaction-cost journal.

This is educational, not investment advice. Use hypothetical or demo data. Completion is not proof of live trading proficiency.

Three cost lenses

SpreadBid versus ask
ImpactSize and available depth
FeesCommissions and financing

01 · What market impact means

Market impact is the change in executable prices associated with an order or trading program. It can arise from consuming available liquidity and from other participants repricing.

বড় Order Available Liquidity Consume করলে Execution Price খারাপ হতে পারে।

02 · Why transaction costs matter

A strategy's theoretical profit is not the same as its realized profit. Spread, commissions, market impact, slippage and financing can erase a small expected edge.

Gross Profit আর Net Profit আলাদা।

03 · Explicit versus implicit costs

Explicit costs include stated commissions and exchange or broker fees. Implicit costs include crossing the spread and execution shortfall relative to a benchmark.

Commission Visible Cost, Spread আর Impact Often Implicit Cost।

04 · Quoted spread

For EUR/USD bid 1.1000 and ask 1.1002, the quoted spread is 0.0002 or 2 pips. Buying then immediately selling 10,000 EUR at unchanged executable quotes costs $2 before fees.

Spread = Ask − Bid।

05 · Half-spread versus full spread

Relative to midpoint 1.1001, buying at 1.1002 costs 1 pip and selling at 1.1000 costs 1 pip. The immediate round trip costs 2 pips, not 4.

One-way Midpoint Cost Half Spread, Round Trip Full Spread।

06 · Order book depth

The best price is available only up to its displayed or executable quantity. Larger orders may sweep several price levels.

Top Quote-এ Unlimited Volume পাওয়া যায় না।

07 · Illustrative buy-side book walk

Assume 20k EUR offered at 1.1002, 30k at 1.1003 and 50k at 1.1005. A 100k market buy consumes all three levels.

100k Buy তিনটি Ask Level Consume করে।

08 · Weighted average fill

The 100k buy averages 1.10038. Against the initial best ask 1.1002, that is 1.8 pips or $18 additional cost. Relative to midpoint 1.1001, total execution shortfall is $28.

Benchmark অনুযায়ী Cost Different হয়।

(20,000 × 1.1002 + 30,000 × 1.1003 + 50,000 × 1.1005) ÷ 100,000 = 1.10038

09 · Benchmark discipline

Always name the benchmark: arrival midpoint, arrival best ask, decision price or volume-weighted reference. The same fill can have different shortfall numbers.

Reference Price না লিখলে Slippage/Impact Compare Misleading।

10 · Temporary versus persistent impact

A quote can recover as liquidity replenishes or remain changed when new information or continued demand matters. A single fill cannot isolate permanent impact.

Price Move সবসময় Permanent Impact নয়।

11 · Market order trade-off

Market orders favor execution promptly but accept uncertainty about the final price, especially for larger sizes or thin markets.

Market Order Fill Priority দেয়, Price Guarantee নয়।

12 · Limit order trade-off

A limit order restricts acceptable execution price but may fill partially or not at all. Missing a trade can carry opportunity cost.

Limit Order Better Price Control দেয়, Fill Guarantee নয়।

13 · Opportunity cost

If a buy limit remains unfilled and the market rises, the trader misses a possible opportunity. That hypothetical foregone gain is not a realized trading loss.

Unfilled Trade-এর Opportunity Cost Realized Loss নয়।

14 · Implementation shortfall

For a buy, one common signed implementation shortfall is actual acquisition cost minus benchmark cost, plus explicit fees. A sell reverses the price difference. Define treatment of unfilled shares or units separately.

Implementation Shortfall-এর Formula Benchmark আর Fill Quantity-এর ওপর Depend করে।

15 · Worked implementation shortfall

A 100k EUR buy at average 1.10038 versus arrival midpoint 1.1001 costs $28 more in USD. With a $7 commission, total measured shortfall is $35, excluding financing and other charges.

$28 Price Shortfall + $7 Commission = $35।

(1.10038 − 1.1001) × 100,000 + $7 = $35

16 · Avoid double counting

The $28 shortfall versus midpoint already includes the initial half-spread and the order-book walk. Do not add the same spread and impact a second time.

Midpoint Shortfall-এর সাথে Same Spread আবার Add করো না।

17 · Worked sell-side example

Sell 40k EUR: 25k at 1.1000 and 15k at 1.0999. Average sale price is 1.0999625. Versus arrival bid 1.1000, shortfall is $1.50; versus midpoint 1.1001 it is $5.50.

Sell-এর Cost Reference Bid না Midpoint তা Specify করো।

(25,000 × 1.1000 + 15,000 × 1.0999) ÷ 40,000 = 1.0999625

18 · Slippage versus impact

Slippage measures fill minus a specified reference. Impact refers to price effects associated with trading. Observed slippage may include impact, quote changes and latency.

Slippage আর Impact Overlap করতে পারে।

19 · Commission structure

A commission may be charged per side, per lot or as a percentage. Verify whether quoted amounts apply to entry, exit or both.

Per Side Commission আর Round Trip Commission আলাদা।

20 · Financing and rollover

Overnight positions can incur or receive financing depending on instrument and broker terms. Funding can dominate costs for longer holds.

Overnight Swap Cost Position Duration অনুযায়ী Important।

21 · Currency conversion

When your account currency differs from quote currency, realized P&L and fees may require conversion at applicable rates, sometimes with a markup.

Account Currency Different হলে Conversion Cost Include করো।

22 · Position size scaling

For EUR/USD, 1 pip equals about $1 per 10k EUR units. Five pips cost about $5 at 10k and $50 at 100k, before fees.

Same Pip Cost Position Size-এর সাথে Scale করে।

23 · Why impact can be nonlinear

Doubling order size may sweep much more expensive depth. Impact is not guaranteed to rise proportionally with size.

Double Size মানে Always Double Impact নয়।

24 · Liquidity and session effects

Market depth and spreads vary by instrument, venue, news, session and holiday. A calm-session cost estimate may fail during stress.

Session আর News-এর সময় Execution Cost Change হয়।

25 · Execution latency

Time between observation, submission and fill creates exposure to quote changes. A faster connection cannot guarantee zero slippage.

Low Latency Zero Cost Guarantee নয়।

26 · News and gaps

During fast repricing, spreads may widen and stops may fill beyond triggers. A stop-limit may remain unfilled.

Stop Loss Maximum Loss Guarantee করে না।

27 · Dealer and venue differences

OTC spot FX is fragmented. Broker quote streams, dealing models and available depth differ from a centralized futures order book.

Retail Spot FX-এ Single Global Order Book নেই।

28 · TWAP and VWAP concepts

Time-weighted schedules divide an order across intervals. Volume-weighted schedules allocate according to observed or expected volume. Neither guarantees a better price.

TWAP/VWAP Execution Method, Profit Signal নয়।

29 · Splitting orders

Smaller child orders can reduce immediate depth consumption but increase exposure to later price moves, repeated spreads and information leakage.

Order Split করলেও Cost Automatically কমে না।

30 · Measuring all-in cost

Record timestamped arrival bid/ask, midpoint, side, size, each fill, commission, financing and conversion. Compute costs consistently.

Execution Journal-এ সব Fee আর Fill লিখো।

31 · Trading journal template

Use columns UTC time, pair, direction, units, benchmark, fill, quoted spread, commission, financing, conversion and realized P&L.

Data ছাড়া Strategy Edge Verify করা যায় না।

32 · Backtest realism

Use bid/ask data when available, plausible spreads, commissions, slippage scenarios and position-size constraints. Midpoint-only fills are often unrealistic.

Backtest-এ Free Midpoint Fill Assume করো না।

33 · Break-even hurdle

If expected gross gain is 4 pips but total round-trip cost is 5 pips, the modeled net result is −1 pip. Higher win rate alone cannot remove costs.

Gross 4 Pip − Cost 5 Pip = Net −1 Pip।

34 · Risk stress testing

Estimate losses under wider spreads, partial fills, gaps and poor liquidity. A 20-pip planned stop plus 6 pips of adverse execution can lose 26 pips before fees.

Planned Risk আর Realized Risk Different হতে পারে।

35 · Demo exercise: order book

Build an ask ladder and calculate average prices for 20k, 50k and 100k EUR buys. Compare each with best ask and midpoint.

Spreadsheet-এ Market Impact Simulator বানাও।

  1. Make a table of ask prices and available units.
  2. Consume levels from cheapest to most expensive for each buy size.
  3. Calculate weighted fill and shortfall versus both best ask and midpoint.
  4. Record commissions separately without double counting spread.
  5. Explain why the simulation cannot predict real fills.

36 · Demo exercise: compare strategies

Simulate a market buy versus a resting buy limit. Compare filled price, non-fill possibility and opportunity cost without assuming either always wins.

Market বনাম Limit Order-এর Trade-off Compare করো।

37 · Common mistakes

Do not double-count spread, confuse midpoint with executable price, assume fixed slippage, ignore unfilled orders or use futures depth as global spot-FX depth.

Cost Model-এ Same Component Twice Add করো না।

38 · Mastery project

Create a 30-row simulated execution journal with calm and stressed quotes. Calculate benchmark shortfall, fees, median cost and worst-case cost, then document limitations.

30-row Execution Cost Report তৈরি করো।

  1. Create 30 hypothetical fills with timestamps and reference quotes.
  2. Compute signed price shortfall in pips and dollars.
  3. Add commissions and any financing only when applicable.
  4. Compare mean, median and worst-case total costs.
  5. Explain assumptions, missing depth and live-versus-demo differences.

No.

Yes, but it is not a realized trading loss.

No.

No.

−1 pip.

Yes.

Execution cost depends on the chosen reference.
