01 · Stop-loss: what it actually does

A stop-loss is an exit instruction intended to reduce further exposure after an adverse move. A typical stop-market becomes a market order after its trigger and may fill worse than the stop price.

Stop-loss Loss কমাতে সাহায্য করে, কিন্তু Exact Price Guarantee করে না।

02 · Take-profit: planned exit

A take-profit is generally a limit-style exit targeting a favorable price. A target can remain unfilled when executable prices or available liquidity do not satisfy it.

Take-profit Target Price-এ Exit করার চেষ্টা করে।

03 · Trailing stop: dynamic protection

A trailing stop follows favorable price movement by a chosen distance or percentage, then remains at its best achieved level when the market reverses. Exact behavior depends on platform rules.

Trailing Stop Profit-এর দিকে Price গেলে Follow করে, উল্টো গেলে সাধারণত পিছিয়ে যায় না।

04 · Long trade stop placement

A hypothetical EUR/USD long enters at 1.1000, with a stop at 1.0950 and target at 1.1100. Planned distance is 50 pips risk and 100 pips reward, before spread and slippage.

Long Trade-এ Stop সাধারণত Entry-এর নিচে, Target ওপরে।

1.0950Stop: −50 pips
1.1000Long entry
1.1100Target: +100 pips

05 · Short trade stop placement

A hypothetical EUR/USD short enters at 1.1000, with stop at 1.1050 and target at 1.0900. Planned risk distance is 50 pips and reward distance 100 pips.

Short Trade-এ Stop সাধারণত Entry-এর ওপরে, Target নিচে।

06 · Risk-to-reward ratio

Planned reward-to-risk = target distance divided by stop distance. For a 100-pip target and 50-pip stop, it is 2:1. This says nothing by itself about win probability or profitability.

Risk-Reward 1:2 হলেই Strategy Profitable হবে না।

07 · Position sizing from stop distance

Suppose you risk $20 and your stop is 40 pips away on EUR/USD. Desired pip value = $20 ÷ 40 = $0.50 per pip, corresponding to about 5,000 EUR units before costs.

Stop Distance জানার পরে Risk Budget দিয়ে Lot Size ঠিক করো।

$20 ÷ 40 pips = $0.50/pip ≈ 5,000 EUR/USD units

08 · Stop-loss does not guarantee a $20 maximum loss

A 40-pip stop and $0.50 per pip imply a planned $20 loss. A weekend gap, spread widening or slippage can make the realized loss larger.

Planned Risk আর Realized Loss আলাদা হতে পারে।

09 · Spread and trigger side

For typical retail FX, a long closes by selling at bid and a short closes by buying at ask. Some charts display only bid or midpoint. Verify which quote triggers your stop.

Bid-Ask Trigger না বুঝলে Stop অপ্রত্যাশিতভাবে Hit হতে পারে।

10 · Stop-market versus stop-limit

Stop-market prioritizes exiting after trigger but accepts available price. Stop-limit controls minimum/maximum price after trigger, so it can fail to exit during a fast market.

Stop-Limit দিয়ে Price Limit করা যায়, কিন্তু Fill না হওয়ার Risk বাড়ে।

11 · Trailing stop for a long

Example: EUR/USD long at 1.1000 with a hypothetical 40-pip trailing distance. If the reference rises to 1.1080, a platform tracking that reference might move the stop to 1.1040. Actual trigger conventions differ.

Long Trailing Stop নতুন High-এর সঙ্গে Upward Move করতে পারে।

12 · Trailing stop for a short

A short at 1.1000 with a 40-pip trailing distance may initially have stop near 1.1040. If the reference falls to 1.0920, the trailing stop may descend to 1.0960.

Short Trailing Stop Price কমলে নিচে নামতে পারে।

13 · Trailing stop is not a guaranteed profit lock

Even when the trailing level is above entry on a long, a price gap or execution delay can cause a worse fill. The stop level is not an insured payout.

Trailing Stop Profit Lock করলেও Fill Price Guaranteed নয়।

14 · Fixed-pip versus percentage trailing

A fixed 30-pip trailing distance stays constant in pips. A 0.5% trailing distance changes with price. Platform support and calculation conventions vary.

Fixed Pip আর Percentage Trailing-এর Behavior আলাদা।

15 · ATR-based stop distance

Average True Range measures recent price range and can help describe volatility. A stop at 1.5 × ATR is a design choice, not an empirically guaranteed safe distance.

ATR Volatility বোঝায়, Safe Stop Guarantee করে না।

16 · Structural stop placement

A long trader may place invalidation below a well-defined swing low, while a short trader may use a swing high. Structural levels can fail and clustered stops may be vulnerable to gaps.

Stop এমন জায়গায় রাখো যেখানে Trade Thesis Invalid হয়।

17 · Too-tight stops

A stop very close to entry can be triggered by ordinary spread changes or noise. A wider stop may reduce routine triggers but increases loss per unit unless position size decreases.

Stop Wide করলে Lot Size Adjust করতে হবে।

18 · Too-wide stops

A very distant stop can create large losses or poor reward-to-risk. Never move a stop farther away merely to avoid admitting that a thesis failed.

Losing Trade বাঁচাতে Stop আরও দূরে সরানো Risk বাড়ায়।

19 · Break-even stop

Moving a stop to the original entry price does not guarantee zero realized P&L. Spread, commission, financing and slippage can still produce a loss.

Break-even Stop মানে Guaranteed Zero Loss না।

20 · Partial take-profit

Closing part of a position at a first target reduces remaining exposure. The rest can continue toward a later target, but extra costs and different exit outcomes matter.

Partial Exit করলে Remaining Risk আর Reward নতুনভাবে Calculate করো।

21 · OCO and bracket orders

One-cancels-the-other or bracket instructions may cancel the remaining protective or target order after an exit. Verify that the broker supports linked orders and how partial fills are handled.

OCO না থাকলে Position Close হওয়ার পরও Pending Order Active থাকতে পারে।

22 · Server-side versus client-side trailing

Some platforms maintain trailing stops on the client terminal and require it to stay connected. Others host them server-side. Never assume a mobile app or desktop shutdown leaves trailing behavior active.

Trailing Stop Server-এ নাকি Device-এ চলে তা Check করো।

23 · News, gaps and volatility

High-impact news can widen spreads and reduce available liquidity. Stop-market fills can slip and stop-limit exits may not execute at all.

News Time-এ Stop Loss Trigger আর Actual Fill আলাদা হতে পারে।

24 · Trailing stops can underperform in ranges

A narrow trailing distance may repeatedly exit on normal retracements. A broad distance may surrender much of an unrealized gain. Backtest different regimes and account for costs.

Range Market-এ Trailing Stop বারবার Hit হতে পারে।

25 · Expected value matters more than reward-to-risk alone

With average win 2R and average loss 1R, a 40% win rate yields 0.4 × 2 − 0.6 × 1 = +0.2R before costs. Real results depend on execution and sample quality.

Win Rate, Average Win, Average Loss এবং Cost একসঙ্গে Evaluate করো।

0.40 × 2R − 0.60 × 1R = +0.20R before costs

26 · Trade journal fields

Record pair, direction, entry bid/ask, planned stop, target, trailing rule, risk amount, fill prices, slippage, commissions and post-trade notes.

Journal ছাড়া Strategy Improvement Measure করা কঠিন।

27 · Demo exercise

Simulate a long with 50-pip stop and 100-pip target. Then use a 40-pip trailing stop in a demo environment. Record when the stop changes, how it triggers and the actual fill.

Demo-তে Stop Movement আর Fill Price Compare করো।

28 · Common mistakes

Assuming stop-losses guarantee loss caps, setting position size before stop distance, ignoring spread, moving stops emotionally and forgetting linked pending orders.

Risk Management-এর নিয়ম Trade-এর আগে লিখে রাখো।

Professional Forex case study · worked answer

Stop-loss is a contingent instruction

Worked example: Long EUR/USD 10,000 at ask 1.1000, intended stop exit bid 1.0975 gives a planned 25-pip loss = USD 25 before fees. If a gap produces a 1.0965 fill, loss is 35 pips = USD 35.

Professional limitation: Guaranteed stops, if offered, have specific terms and costs. A regular stop does not guarantee maximum loss.

বাংলা ব্যাখ্যা: Stop-Loss দিলেও Gap ও Slippage-এর কারণে বেশি Loss হতে পারে।
