These session labels describe typical regional business hours, not a centralized exchange schedule.

01 · Forex trades across global business days

The decentralized FX market generally runs from late Sunday New York time to late Friday New York time, excluding interruptions and holidays. Trading access depends on broker and product.

Forex সপ্তাহের পাঁচ Business Day প্রায় 24 ঘণ্টা চলে, কিন্তু সব সময় একই Liquidity থাকে না।

02 · Sessions are conventions, not exchanges

Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York sessions describe major regional trading hours. There is no single centralized spot-FX exchange opening bell.

Session মানে একটি Regional Activity Window, Central Exchange নয়।

03 · UTC is your reliable reference

UTC does not shift for daylight saving. Convert to local time using the actual date and time-zone rules. Fixed GMT offsets can mislead.

UTC ব্যবহার করলে DST Confusion কম হয়।

04 · Sydney session

Sydney's commonly quoted session is around 08:00–17:00 local Sydney time. UTC conversion changes with Australian daylight saving and holidays.

Sydney Session Asia-Pacific Trading Day-এর শুরুতে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।

05 · Tokyo session

Tokyo is often approximated as 09:00–18:00 Japan Standard Time (JST, UTC+9). Japan does not observe daylight saving time.

Tokyo Time Year-Round UTC+9 থাকে।

06 · London session

London is commonly approximated as 08:00–17:00 local time. London uses GMT in winter and BST (UTC+1) in summer.

London Session UTC অনুযায়ী Summer/Winter-এ Shift করে।

07 · New York session

New York is commonly approximated as 08:00–17:00 local time. It uses EST (UTC−5) in winter and EDT (UTC−4) in summer.

New York Session-ও DST অনুযায়ী UTC-তে Shift করে।

08 · Indicative UTC timetable

These are illustrative session windows based on common local-hour conventions, not guaranteed broker hours. Sydney can be 21:00–06:00 UTC during AEDT or 22:00–07:00 UTC during AEST; Tokyo 00:00–09:00 UTC; London 08:00–17:00 UTC in winter or 07:00–16:00 in summer; New York 13:00–22:00 UTC in winter or 12:00–21:00 in summer.

09 · Sydney–Tokyo overlap

Sydney and Tokyo trading hours overlap for part of the Asian day. AUD, NZD and JPY-related activity can be relevant, but liquidity depends on instrument and date.

Asia-Pacific Overlap সব Pair-এ Same Volatility দেয় না।

10 · London–Tokyo overlap

The end of Tokyo trading and beginning of London can overlap, especially depending on seasonal clock conventions. This can coincide with a transition in liquidity.

Asian Session-এর শেষ আর London Open-এর Transition Observe করো।

11 · London–New York overlap

A commonly active window occurs when London and New York business hours overlap. During much of the year it is around 12:00–16:00 UTC in summer and 13:00–17:00 UTC in winter, with exceptions during differing DST switch weeks.

London–NY Overlap-এ Activity বেশি হতে পারে, কিন্তু Profit Guaranteed নয়।

12 · Daylight saving mismatch weeks

The US and Europe change clocks on different dates. During the intervening weeks, overlap timing relative to UTC differs from the usual winter/summer pattern.

March আর October/November-এর DST Transition-এ Session Time Double Check করো।

13 · Hamburg and German local time

Germany uses CET (UTC+1) in winter and CEST (UTC+2) in summer. Do not assume a fixed UTC+1 offset throughout the year.

Germany-তে Winter CET আর Summer CEST।

14 · Bangladesh local time

Bangladesh Standard Time is UTC+6 year-round. London 08:00 local becomes 14:00 in Bangladesh during GMT winter, and 13:00 during BST summer.

Bangladesh UTC+6, DST নেই।

15 · Session open versus broker server time

A trading platform may show UTC+2 or UTC+3 server time and switch according to its own policy. Broker server candles need not match local market hours.

MT4/MT5 Chart Time Local Time-এর সমান নাও হতে পারে।

16 · Liquidity changes during the day

Market depth and executable spreads vary with active banks, counterparties, news and venue. The busiest time is not automatically the safest.

High Liquidity সাধারণত Spread কমাতে পারে, কিন্তু সব সময় নয়।

17 · Volatility changes by currency pair

JPY crosses can react strongly during Asian events. EUR and GBP pairs can react around European releases. USD pairs often respond to US news. These are tendencies, not rules.

Pair-এর News Calendar অনুযায়ী Activity বুঝো।

18 · The London open

The opening European hours can bring repricing and increased volume. A breakout at the open can fail or reverse, so do not trade solely because the clock reaches London open.

London Open মানেই Guaranteed Breakout নয়।

19 · The New York open

US data releases, equity-market opening and London–NY overlap can affect FX prices. The US stock-market open is not the same as the FX New York session convention.

New York FX Session আর NYSE Opening Bell আলাদা।

20 · Session highs and lows

Traders mark the high and low of a chosen session to study range, volatility and subsequent price action. Define the session timezone consistently before backtesting.

Session High/Low Mark করার আগে Exact Time Window Fix করো।

21 · Asian range example

Suppose EUR/USD trades between 1.0800 and 1.0830 during a defined Asian window. The range is 30 pips. A later break beyond 1.0830 does not by itself prove continuation.

Asian Range Breakout-এর False Signal হতে পারে।

(1.0830 − 1.0800) ÷ 0.0001 = 30 pips

22 · London breakout versus false breakout

Price may trade above an earlier range then return inside it. Evaluate spread, volume proxies, event timing and stop placement rather than treating every range break as a trend.

Liquidity Sweep আর Genuine Breakout আলাদা করে Test করতে হবে।

23 · News calendar matters more than labels

Major central-bank decisions, inflation, employment and GDP releases can cause sharp moves regardless of the nominal session. Use the event's exact timezone.

High-Impact News-এর সময় Session Name-এর চেয়ে Event Timing গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।

24 · Holiday liquidity

Christmas, New Year and national bank holidays can reduce participation, widen spreads or change trading schedules. Japan, UK and US holidays affect different currency pairs.

Holiday-তে Low Liquidity আর Unusual Spread হতে পারে।

25 · Weekend reopening and gaps

Prices can gap between Friday's last executable quotes and Sunday's or Monday's reopening. Stops may execute at worse prices and broker opening times vary.

Weekend Gap-এর জন্য Risk Budget Conservative রাখো।

26 · Rollover and spread widening

Around the broker's daily rollover cutoff, often near 5 p.m. New York for retail FX, liquidity may thin and spreads may widen. Exact timing varies.

Daily Rollover Time-এ Spread Check করো।

27 · Avoid rigid session stereotypes

No session guarantees a trend, reversal or narrow spread. Measure your chosen pair's realized volatility, spread and execution across dates.

Sydney Slow, London Fast এই Rule সবসময় True নয়।

28 · Session-based trading plan

Choose a pair, date, timezone, session window, scheduled events, entry conditions, risk limit and maximum number of trades. Record actual spreads and fills.

Session Plan লিখে Trade করলে Backtest Compare করা সহজ।

29 · Backtest without look-ahead bias

When studying session highs/lows, use only information available at the decision time. Include DST changes, holidays, spreads and slippage.

Future Candle দেখে Past Trade Decision বানিও না।

30 · Practical chart exercise

Select two historical dates, one winter and one summer. Mark Tokyo, London and New York windows in UTC and your local timezone. Compare spreads, range and news.

Winter আর Summer-এর Chart-এ Session Mark Practice করো।

31 · Common beginner mistakes

Mixing broker and local time, ignoring daylight saving, assuming London and New York overlap is fixed all year, trading low-liquidity holidays and treating volatility as guaranteed profit.

Time Conversion ভুল হলে Strategy Result-ও ভুল হবে।

Mastery check

1. Does spot FX have one centralized exchange opening bell?

No.

2. Which session city uses JST UTC+9 all year?

Tokyo.

3. Does London observe daylight saving?

Yes, GMT in winter and BST in summer.

4. Does New York observe daylight saving?

Yes, EST and EDT.

5. Is Bangladesh UTC+6 year-round?

Yes.

6. Is Germany UTC+1 year-round?

No, CET UTC+1 in winter and CEST UTC+2 in summer.

7. What is the London winter open in UTC using the 08:00 local convention?

08:00 UTC.

8. What is the London summer open in UTC using the 08:00 local convention?

07:00 UTC.

9. Can the US–Europe DST mismatch alter overlap times?

Yes.

10. Does London–New York overlap guarantee a profitable trade?

No.

11. Asian range 1.0800 to 1.0830: how many pips?

30 pips for EUR/USD.

12. What should a session backtest include?

Correct historical time zones, DST, events, spreads, slippage and no look-ahead bias.

Professional Forex case study · worked answer

Session conversion across daylight-saving changes

Worked example: At 12:00 UTC, London is 12:00 in winter (GMT) and 13:00 in summer (BST). New York is 07:00 in winter (EST) and 08:00 in summer (EDT), outside transitional mismatch weeks.

Professional limitation: The US and UK change daylight-saving time on different dates. Exchange holidays, venue schedules and broker server time affect actual market access.

বাংলা ব্যাখ্যা: UTC এবং Local Time এক নয়; DST Date যাচাই করতে হবে।
