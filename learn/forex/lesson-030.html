01 · What OHLC stands for

Open is the first observed price of an interval, high the maximum, low the minimum and close the last. Their meaning depends on the data feed and bar construction.

OHLC মানে Open, High, Low, Close। একটি Candle নির্দিষ্ট সময়ের চারটি Price Summary।

02 · Candle anatomy

A candlestick draws the body between open and close, plus upper and lower wicks reaching high and low. It compresses many price observations into one shape.

Body হলো Open-Close Gap, Wick হলো High-Low পর্যন্ত Extension।

03 · Bullish and bearish definitions

If close exceeds open, the candle is commonly called bullish; if close is below open, bearish. Equal open and close creates a zero or tiny body.

Close > Open হলে Bullish, Close < Open হলে Bearish।

04 · A numerical bullish candle

EUR/USD opens 1.1000, reaches 1.1050, falls to 1.0980 and closes 1.1030. The body is 30 pips, full range 70 pips, upper wick 20 pips and lower wick 20 pips.

Open 1.1000 আর Close 1.1030 হলে Body 30 Pip।

05 · A numerical bearish candle

Open 1.1030, high 1.1040, low 1.0970 and close 1.0990. Body is 40 pips, upper wick 10 pips and lower wick 20 pips.

Bearish Candle-এ Open Close-এর উপরে থাকে।

06 · OHLC consistency checks

A valid bar satisfies high ≥ max(open, close) and low ≤ min(open, close). If not, inspect your feed, aggregation or data transformation.

High সবসময় Open/Close-এর সমান বা বেশি, Low সমান বা কম।

07 · Range calculation

Total range equals high minus low. On EUR/USD, 1.1050−1.0980 = 0.0070 or 70 pips. A large range alone does not prove directional strength.

Range = High − Low।

08 · Real body calculation

Absolute body equals |close−open|. Divide by pip size for pips, or by total range for body-to-range ratio.

Body = |Close − Open|।

09 · Upper wick calculation

Upper wick equals high minus max(open, close). A long upper wick records prices above the body but not necessarily why the market reversed.

Upper Wick = High − max(Open,Close)।

10 · Lower wick calculation

Lower wick equals min(open, close) minus low. It shows an excursion below the body, not guaranteed institutional buying.

Lower Wick = min(Open,Close) − Low।

11 · Worked ratio

For bullish OHLC 1.1000, 1.1050, 1.0980, 1.1030, body-to-range is 30/70 ≈ 42.86%. Neither this ratio nor wick length alone predicts the next candle.

Body/Range = 30/70 = 42.86%।

Body ratio = |1.1030 − 1.1000| ÷ (1.1050 − 1.0980) × 100 ≈ 42.86%

12 · Quote-side caveat

Retail FX platforms may construct candles from bid, ask or midpoint quotes. A bid-only candle may not show the ask price that triggers a short stop.

Bid Chart-এ Ask Stop Trigger দেখা নাও যেতে পারে।

13 · Timeframe matters

A five-minute candle and daily candle summarize different periods. A bullish daily candle can contain many bearish five-minute candles.

Daily Bullish হলেও ভিতরে অনেক Bearish 5m Candle থাকতে পারে।

14 · Candles do not show path

The same OHLC can arise from different sequences of price movement. You cannot know whether high or low occurred first from OHLC alone.

OHLC দেখে High আগে না Low আগে হয়েছে বোঝা যায় না।

15 · The missing volume problem

A candle contains prices, not a complete record of traded global OTC spot FX volume. Broker tick volume is not a universal executed-volume measure.

Candle Shape থেকে Global Volume জানা যায় না।

16 · Gaps between bars

A new bar can open away from the previous close due to missing trading, session changes or feed conventions. Do not assume every interval trades continuously.

Previous Close আর Next Open Different হতে পারে।

17 · Candle color settings

Chart platforms can use different colors for rising and falling bars. Read open and close instead of assuming green or red always means the same thing.

Color Setting Change হতে পারে, OHLC Check করো।

18 · Price precision and pips

EUR/USD commonly uses 0.0001 per pip while many JPY pairs use 0.01. A fifth or third decimal digit is often a fractional pip.

Pip Size Pair অনুযায়ী Different।

19 · Candlestick versus trade signal

A candle describes what happened, not what must happen next. Pattern-based claims require out-of-sample testing, realistic spreads and risk controls.

এক Candle Guaranteed Buy/Sell Signal নয়।

20 · A simple OHLC worksheet

Create columns timestamp, open, high, low, close, body, upper wick, lower wick, range and body ratio. Use valid simulated quotes.

Spreadsheet-এ OHLC Calculator বানাও।

21 · Excel formulas

For row 2: body = ABS(E2-B2), upper wick = C2-MAX(B2,E2), lower wick = MIN(B2,E2)-D2, range = C2-D2. Body ratio = IF(range=0,0,body/range).

Excel Formula দিয়ে প্রতিটি Candle Analyze করো।

22 · Zero-range candles

If open, high, low and close are identical, range is zero. A body-to-range calculation must handle division by zero.

Range Zero হলে Divide by Zero Avoid করো।

23 · Backtesting candle rules

Specify timeframe, quote side, timezone and exact entry/exit assumptions. A backtest that enters at the candle close may not be executable at that historical midpoint.

Candle Close-এ Free Fill Assume করো না।

24 · Look-ahead bias

A candle's final high, low and close are only known after its interval finishes. Do not use them to simulate decisions made earlier inside that candle.

Candle Finish হওয়ার আগে Final Close জানা যায় না।

25 · Ambiguous stop and target

If a historical candle touches both stop and target, OHLC alone cannot establish which executed first. Use finer data or conservative rules.

এক Candle-এ Stop আর Target Hit হলে Sequence Unknown।

26 · Multi-timeframe reading

Use higher timeframe for context and lower timeframe for detail, but avoid assuming a higher-timeframe candle predicts the next lower-timeframe move.

Multi-Timeframe Context দেয়, Certainty নয়।

27 · Common mistakes

Confusing candle color with guaranteed direction, mixing pip sizes, using incomplete candles, ignoring bid/ask and assuming wick patterns prove hidden orders are frequent errors.

Wick দেখে Guaranteed Stop Hunt Declare করো না।

28 · Practical exercise

Analyze five simulated candles, compute each body and wick, then identify which has the greatest range and whether its close exceeds open.

৫টি Candle-এর Body, Wick আর Range নিজে বের করো।

  1. Use five valid hypothetical OHLC rows.
  2. Compute each body, range and two wicks.
  3. Check high ≥ max(open, close) and low ≤ min(open, close).
  4. Compare body-to-range ratios.
  5. Explain why the values cannot reveal intrabar event order.
