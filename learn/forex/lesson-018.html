01 · Five ways to obtain FX exposure

Spot, forwards, futures, options and CFDs all reference exchange rates but have different legal rights, settlement methods, leverage and risk. The product name alone cannot tell you the exact terms.

সব Forex Product একইভাবে কাজ করে না। Contract আগে বুঝতে হবে।

02 · Spot forex explained

A conventional spot FX trade exchanges one currency for another at an agreed rate for near-term settlement. It is usually OTC and commonly settles T+2 business days for many pairs, with exceptions.

Spot Forex-এ Currency Exchange-এর Settlement সাধারণত কয়েক Business Day-এর মধ্যে হয়।

03 · Spot transaction example

A company buys EUR 10,000 against USD at 1.1000, agreeing to deliver USD 11,000 and receive EUR 10,000 on the value date. Fees and settlement details are separate.

10,000 EUR × 1.1000 = 11,000 USD।

04 · Deliverable spot versus retail rolling spot

A deliverable spot transaction exchanges currencies at settlement. Some retail products marketed as spot FX are rolling or margined contracts without routine physical delivery. Check your agreement.

Retail Spot Account-এ সবসময় Physical Currency Delivery হয় না।

05 · What is a forward?

An FX forward fixes an exchange rate today for exchanging currencies on a specified future date. It is generally OTC and can be tailored to amount and date.

Forward-এ Future Date-এর Exchange Rate আজ Fix করা হয়।

06 · Forward hedge example

An importer owes EUR 100,000 in three months and agrees to buy euros forward at 1.1100 USD/EUR. Contracted dollar payment is USD 111,000, excluding fees. The forward reduces exchange-rate uncertainty but may forgo favorable moves.

Forward Hedge Currency Risk কমায়, কিন্তু Better Future Rate Miss হতে পারে।

EUR 100,000 × 1.1100 USD/EUR = USD 111,000

07 · Why forward prices differ from spot

Forward points primarily reflect relative funding rates and the contract's maturity under covered-interest-parity relationships, with market and credit adjustments. A higher forward rate is not a forecast guarantee.

Forward Rate Future Spot Price-এর Guaranteed Prediction নয়।

08 · Forward settlement and credit risk

A bilateral forward can create counterparty exposure before settlement. Collateral, netting and documentation can mitigate but not eliminate this risk.

Forward Contract-এ Counterparty Risk থাকে।

09 · Non-deliverable forwards

An NDF generally settles a cash difference in an agreed currency rather than delivering the restricted underlying currency. Fixing sources and settlement terms are contractual.

NDF-এ Underlying Currency Delivery না হয়ে Cash Difference Settle হতে পারে।

10 · What is a currency future?

Currency futures are standardized contracts traded on exchanges, with defined contract specifications, expiry and clearing rules. Many positions are closed before expiry.

Futures Exchange-Traded Standardized Contract।

11 · Futures margin is performance collateral

Initial margin is a performance bond, not the purchase price of the full notional exposure. Maintenance margin and variation settlement can create additional funding needs.

Futures Margin Deposit মানেই Maximum Loss নয়।

12 · Daily mark-to-market

Exchange-traded futures typically credit or debit gains and losses through daily settlement. An adverse move can trigger margin calls or forced liquidation.

Daily Settlement-এ Loss Account Balance-এ Impact করে।

13 · Futures example

A hypothetical futures position representing EUR 125,000 gains 0.0020 USD per EUR. Ignoring fees, its USD P&L is 125,000 × 0.0020 = $250. Contract size and tick values must be verified for the exact contract.

Notional × Price Change দিয়ে Basic P&L Calculate করা যায়।

EUR 125,000 × 0.0020 USD/EUR = $250

14 · Futures expiry and rolling

A futures contract has an expiry. To maintain exposure, traders may close the near contract and open a later contract, incurring execution costs and a possible calendar spread.

Futures Position Keep করতে Roll করতে হতে পারে।

15 · What is an FX option?

An option gives its buyer a right, but not an obligation, to transact under specified terms. A call gives a right to buy the relevant underlying; a put gives a right to sell. FX pair conventions matter.

Option Buyer-এর Right থাকে, Obligation নয়।

16 · Option premium

An option buyer pays a premium. For a plain purchased option, the premium plus costs is generally the maximum contractual loss if fully paid, but exercise, settlement and product details matter.

Purchased Option-এর Premium আগে Pay করতে হয়।

17 · Call option payoff example

A simplified EUR call/USD put has strike 1.1000, EUR 10,000 notional and premium $150. At expiry spot 1.1300, intrinsic value is $300, giving $150 net before other fees under cash-settlement assumptions.

Option Payoff আর Net Profit আলাদা, Premium Minus করতে হবে।

(1.1300 − 1.1000) × EUR 10,000 − $150 = $150

18 · Put option payoff example

A simplified EUR put/USD call with strike 1.1000 and EUR 10,000 notional has intrinsic value $200 if spot at expiry is 1.0800. With $150 premium, net is $50 before other costs under the simplified cash-settlement assumption.

Put Option Underlying Price কমলে Value পেতে পারে।

19 · Option writer risk

Selling options can create substantial obligations. Uncovered short calls may have theoretically unbounded loss in simplified models, and other short options can also incur large losses.

Option Sell করা Option Buy-এর চেয়ে অনেক বেশি Risky হতে পারে।

20 · Time decay and implied volatility

Option values depend on spot, strike, time to expiry, interest rates and expected volatility. Time decay often hurts long options, while changes in implied volatility affect premiums.

Direction Right হলেও Option Trade Loss হতে পারে।

21 · European versus American exercise

European-style options generally exercise only at expiry; American-style options may be exercisable earlier. FX option conventions and settlement terms differ by venue.

European আর American Style Option-এর Exercise Rule আলাদা।

22 · What is a CFD?

A contract for difference is a derivative settling the difference in an underlying reference price between opening and closing. A forex CFD typically does not convey ownership of the currencies.

CFD-তে Currency Own না করেও Price Difference-এর Exposure নেওয়া হয়।

23 · CFD P&L example

Buy a hypothetical EUR/USD CFD for 10,000 EUR-equivalent units at 1.1002 and close at 1.1050: gross USD price P&L is $48 before commissions, financing and conversion.

CFD Profit/Loss Underlying Price Change-এর ওপর নির্ভর করে।

(1.1050 − 1.1002) × 10,000 = $48 gross

24 · CFD leverage and margin

CFDs often allow leveraged exposure. A small margin deposit controls larger notional risk, so losses can be large relative to deposited funds. Retail protections depend on jurisdiction and entity.

Leverage Profit যেমন বাড়ায়, Loss-ও বাড়ায়।

25 · CFD financing and rollover

Rolling CFD positions may incur overnight financing or swap adjustments. Read the broker's actual instrument schedule, including triple-charge days and holidays.

Overnight CFD Hold করলে Financing Cost হতে পারে।

26 · Spot versus CFD counterparty relationship

Deliverable spot, margined rolling FX and CFDs have different ownership, settlement and contractual counterparty arrangements. The exact product disclosure is decisive.

Product Name দেখে Legal Ownership Assume করো না।

27 · Central clearing versus bilateral exposure

Exchange-traded futures typically use central clearing, while many spot and forward transactions are bilateral. Central clearing changes the risk structure but does not make a contract risk-free.

Clearing House থাকলেও Market Loss হতে পারে।

28 · Price transparency and market data

Exchange futures have venue-specific order books and reported volume. OTC spot and CFDs may rely on dealer quotes and lack a complete global volume tape.

Futures Volume আর Spot Tick Volume এক নয়।

29 · Spread and transaction cost comparison

Spot may involve spread and settlement charges; forwards embed forward points and dealer pricing; futures have spread and fees; options have premium and transaction costs; CFDs have spread, commissions and financing.

Cost Compare করার সময় শুধু Spread দেখো না।

30 · Tax and regulation differ

Tax treatment, trading permissions, leverage restrictions and investor protections vary by residence, legal entity and instrument. Seek jurisdiction-specific guidance rather than assuming all FX products are taxed alike.

Germany-তে Product অনুযায়ী Tax/Regulation Check করতে হবে।

31 · Hedging versus speculation

A company may use forwards to lock a known future currency payment, while a trader may speculate using futures or options. The same instrument can serve different objectives.

Hedging-এর Goal Risk Reduce, Speculation-এর Goal Price Move থেকে Profit।

32 · Choosing the right instrument

Before choosing, ask whether you need physical currency, a future locked rate, standardized exchange exposure, asymmetric option rights or cash-settled price exposure. Then compare maximum possible loss and funding needs.

Instrument Select করার আগে Objective, Settlement আর Risk বুঝো।

33 · Practice comparison exercise

Compare a EUR 10,000 spot purchase at 1.1000, a forward at 1.1100, a hypothetical EUR 10,000 futures-equivalent position and a EUR 10,000 CFD. Write down legal obligation, settlement, costs and loss scenario.

Demo Worksheet-এ চার Product-এর Difference লিখো।

34 · Common mistakes

Do not treat a CFD as owning euros, assume forward points predict future prices, confuse futures margin with maximum loss, ignore option premiums or assume every broker offers identical contracts.

Product Terms না বুঝে Trade শুরু করো না।
