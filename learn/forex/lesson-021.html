Module 02 · Market Structure and Participants

Market Orders versus Resting Orders

Learn when orders execute immediately, when they wait, why fills differ from quotes and how to choose order instructions in a demo environment. বাংলা ও English with practical exercises.

35 teaching sections 15 mastery questions 3 demo labs

Study objectives and readiness

What you should be able to do

  1. Distinguish market, limit, stop and stop-limit orders.
  2. Calculate spread and weighted-average fill prices.
  3. Explain slippage, partial fills and queue priority.
  4. Practice orders in a demo account without risking funds.

Lesson completion means you studied the material, not that you are proficient at live trading.

The key trade-off

MarketPrioritize immediate execution, not exact price
Resting limitSpecify price, accept possible non-fill

01 · Market orders: what they request

A market order requests execution promptly against available liquidity. It prioritizes execution over a particular price, so the final fill can differ from the screen quote.

Market Order দ্রুত Fill চায়, কিন্তু Exact Price Guarantee করে না।

02 · Resting orders: what they request

A resting order waits in a venue or broker system until its conditions are met. A resting limit order specifies an acceptable price boundary but is not guaranteed to execute.

Resting Order Wait করে। Price Condition Meet হলেও Fill নাও হতে পারে।

03 · Marketable versus non-marketable

A buy limit priced at or above the available ask may execute immediately and act as a marketable limit. A buy limit below the ask usually rests until sellers are available.

Limit Order দিলেই সেটা সবসময় Resting থাকে না।

04 · Buy market order

A buy market order normally consumes available sell-side liquidity, starting at the best available ask. Its average execution price depends on quantity and depth.

Buy Market Order সাধারণত Ask-side Liquidity Consume করে।

05 · Sell market order

A sell market order normally consumes available buy-side liquidity, starting at the best available bid. Fast-moving markets can produce slippage.

Sell Market Order সাধারণত Bid-side Liquidity Consume করে।

06 · Buy limit order

A buy limit specifies the maximum acceptable price. It can fill at that price or better, subject to execution rules, but may remain unfilled.

Buy Limit-এর Maximum Price Fix থাকে।

07 · Sell limit order

A sell limit specifies the minimum acceptable price. It can fill at that price or better, but it may never execute.

Sell Limit-এর Minimum Price Fix থাকে।

08 · Stop order is different

A stop order waits for a trigger, then generally becomes a market order under the broker's rules. The trigger price is not a guaranteed fill price.

Stop Trigger আর Actual Fill Price Same নাও হতে পারে।

09 · Stop-limit order

A stop-limit order triggers a limit order. It can protect against fills beyond its limit but may fail to execute entirely during a gap.

Stop-limit Slippage Bound করতে পারে, কিন্তু No Fill Risk থাকে।

10 · Order book and depth

An order book shows available interest at different prices on a particular venue. OTC spot forex does not have one complete global order book.

Retail Forex-এ Universal Order Book নেই।

11 · Top of book

The best bid and best ask are the most competitive displayed prices for a specified venue or feed. They may be available only for limited size.

Best Bid/Ask-এর Quantity Limited হতে পারে।

12 · Liquidity taking versus providing

An aggressive order consumes available liquidity. A passive resting order may provide liquidity when it is displayed and accessible to other participants.

Aggressive Order Liquidity Take করে, Passive Order Provide করতে পারে।

13 · Spread and immediate round trip

At EUR/USD 1.1000 bid / 1.1002 ask, buying at ask and immediately selling at bid loses 2 pips before other fees if quotes remain unchanged.

Instant Buy-Sell করলে Spread Cost দিতে হয়।

14 · Worked market-order depth example

Suppose a 100,000-EUR buy market order fills 40,000 at 1.1002 and 60,000 at 1.1004. Weighted average entry is 1.10032, which is 1.2 pips above the initial ask.

Large Market Order Multiple Level-এ Fill হলে Average Price Worse হতে পারে।

(40,000 × 1.1002 + 60,000 × 1.1004) ÷ 100,000 = 1.10032

15 · Partial fills

An order may fill in several pieces or only partly, depending on venue and order instructions. Always check actual filled quantity and remaining order status.

Partial Fill হলে Position Size Expected-এর থেকে Different হতে পারে।

16 · Time-in-force instructions

Good-till-cancelled, immediate-or-cancel, fill-or-kill and day orders describe how long or under what conditions an order remains active. Availability varies by broker.

GTC, IOC, FOK আর Day Order-এর Rule Broker অনুযায়ী Check করো।

17 · Queue priority

Resting limit orders at the same price may be filled according to venue-specific priority, often involving time. Touching your limit price does not guarantee your order was reached.

Price Touch করলেও Queue-তে তোমার Order Fill নাও হতে পারে।

18 · Price improvement

Some orders execute at a better price than requested. Compare price improvement with fees, slippage and fill probability rather than considering only one statistic.

Better Fill Possible, কিন্তু Guaranteed নয়।

19 · Positive and negative slippage

Slippage is the difference between expected and executed prices. It can be favorable or unfavorable, depending on order type and execution rules.

Slippage সবসময় Negative নয়।

20 · Volatility and news

During economic announcements, prices may gap and displayed liquidity may shrink. Market orders and triggered stops can execute far from the last observed quote.

News-এর সময় Market Order High Risk হতে পারে।

21 · Market order versus limit order trade-off

A market order usually favors execution certainty but sacrifices price certainty. A limit order controls the worst permitted price but sacrifices fill certainty.

Market Order-এ Fill Priority, Limit Order-এ Price Boundary Priority।

OrderPrimary priorityMain trade-off
MarketImmediate executionSlippage and price uncertainty
LimitAcceptable priceMay not fill
StopTrigger at thresholdExecution can gap
Stop-limitTrigger then limitMay remain unfilled

22 · When a limit order misses

EUR/USD may approach but never reach your buy limit. Missing a trade is not itself a loss, and chasing a missed entry can change the original risk plan.

Limit Fill না হলে Impulsive Chase করো না।

23 · When a market order hurts

A sudden spread expansion can make the market fill substantially worse than a pre-trade estimate. Always inspect spread and size before submitting.

Spread Wide হলে Market Entry Cost বাড়ে।

24 · Entry and exit are both executions

An excellent entry price does not guarantee a profitable exit. Stop-loss and take-profit behavior depends on order type, market depth and broker rules.

Entry ভালো হলেও Exit Slippage Profit/Loss Change করতে পারে।

25 · Retail OTC broker differences

Some forex brokers execute as principal and maintain their own quote streams; others route or hedge exposure. Platform labels do not prove a centralized exchange order book.

Retail Broker-এর Execution Model Read করো।

26 · Order rejection and requotes

A broker may reject or requote under its stated rules. If a submission times out, check positions and pending orders before clicking again.

Timeout হলে Duplicate Order Place করো না।

27 · Execution cost formula

For a long EUR/USD position, gross USD P&L equals (exit bid − entry ask) × EUR units. Subtract commissions and financing to calculate net P&L.

Net Profit বের করতে Spread, Commission আর Swap Include করো।

Gross long P&L = (exit bid − entry ask) × base units

28 · Risk and sizing before orders

Define invalidation, acceptable loss, instrument units and available margin before submitting an order. A stop-loss is not an absolute loss guarantee.

Order Submit-এর আগে Risk Budget আর Size Calculate করো।

29 · Worked limit order example

Suppose the ask is 1.1002 and you place a buy limit at 1.0995. If the market never trades at an executable price for your order, you remain flat. If filled, subsequent losses are still possible.

Buy Limit Price Better হলেও Trade Fill Guarantee নেই।

30 · Worked stop versus stop-limit gap

A sell stop at 1.0950 may trigger and fill near 1.0920 after a gap. A sell stop-limit with a 1.0940 limit may instead remain unfilled. Neither design eliminates risk.

Gap-এ Stop Fill Worse হতে পারে, Stop-limit Fill Miss করতে পারে।

31 · Order type comparison

Market: prioritizes immediate execution. Limit: enforces price bound. Stop: triggers an order after a threshold. Stop-limit: triggers a limit with non-fill risk.

চার Order Type-এর Priority আলাদা।

32 · Demo lab: order ticket

On a demo platform, record bid and ask, place a tiny hypothetical market order, inspect the actual fill, then close and calculate spread and fees.

Demo-তে Actual Fill আর Expected Price Compare করো।

  1. Open demo only and record bid/ask.
  2. Submit a small market order and record fill.
  3. Check the position and commission.
  4. Close and calculate realized P&L.
  5. Compare with your pre-trade estimate.

33 · Demo lab: pending orders

Place an unfilled buy limit, change its price and cancel it. Test an eligible stop or stop-limit order on demo if supported, recording trigger and fill behavior.

Pending Order Modify আর Cancel Practice করো।

34 · Demo lab: slippage journal

Log UTC timestamp, order type, direction, expected price, executed price, quantity, spread, fees and market conditions for ten simulated trades.

১০টি Demo Trade-এর Execution Journal বানাও।

35 · Common mistakes

Confusing a stop with a limit, assuming market orders guarantee price, treating limit touches as guaranteed fills, ignoring partial fills and repeating orders after timeouts.

Order Type না বুঝে Live Trade করো না।

Yes, depending on broker and venue.
