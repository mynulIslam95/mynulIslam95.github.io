Turn an ISO request into a discovery conversation

A customer says, “Our suppliers must meet our ISO/IEC 27001 requirements.” Ask for the exact requirement and decide with the customer what evidence would satisfy it. ISO publishes standards. Independent certification bodies issue certificates.

ClarifyAsk the customer
RequirementDo you need a certificate, evidence of specific controls or both?
ScopeWhich service, environments and data flows are in scope?
EvidenceWhat will you accept, who reviews it and when is it due?

Practice with an audit log. Ask which events the customer needs to see. Agree on one acceptance check with the security or compliance owner. For example, make a test change to a user role, then confirm that the evidence records the actor, action and timestamp. Give the reviewer the evidence and let them confirm whether it meets the agreed requirement.
