ops-duty-scripts

View repository

Problem

A support shift needs the same checks every time: is the service up, did the log grow ERROR/WARN lines, and is the handover written before the next person sits down.

Implementation

Python CLIs for HTTP health poll with jsonl history, regex scan of logs, and a duty checklist. Dry-run is the default. An incident runbook covers /health down.

Verification

pytest covers the scripts. Sample log and health history are in the repository.
